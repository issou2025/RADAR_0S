## Research sequence

1. **50-candidate desk pass:** for each role, locate current provider documentation and provider-authored code; record exact source URL, observed date and evidence class. Separate credential existence and API usage from issuer-documented lexical grammar. Record sibling public IDs/keys, role and scope, format drift, issuance/rotation, and overlap with existing provider, generic assignment, Bearer, JWT and structural detection. A link to a scanner rule or an illustrative placeholder does not establish T1.
2. **Select for deeper research:** rank by risk at LLM/agent runtime boundaries, independent lexical or constrained-context signal, benign collision risk, observed generic coverage, and Rust/WASM/streaming cost. No quota forces weak candidates into implementation. Retain negative findings.
3. **Deep handoff per selected family:** supported/excluded shapes with provenance for prefix, length, alphabet and separators; T1/T2/T3 rationale; independent synthetic positive, near-miss twin and benign axes; overlap and output-policy behavior; explicit false-positive/false-negative boundary; issuance checklist describing structural observations only. Distinguish an existing detector extension from a new registry detector/finding type.
4. **Disposition for all 50:** distinct family; extend existing family; generic coverage sufficient; or pending/unsupported. If official grammar is missing, mark it unknown, not inferred. A credential's importance alone cannot justify a distinct finding type.
5. **Implementation handoff only after review:** open separately scoped product and benchmarks issues for approved families. Core conformance and benchmark arrival/profile evidence both gate promotion. Do not mark a family stable from inventory presence or fixture count alone.

## Recording and security

- Post exploratory research and source links in this issue (and follow-on research issues where a candidate needs its own thread). Freeze final product judgement in the product evidence location; measured benchmark results stay in the benchmarks repository. Link exact revisions for any cross-repo measurement.
- Never request, paste, hash, retain, log or derive a fixture from a real issued credential. Use independently authored synthetic examples. Public comments may cite documented nonsecret prefixes, never a complete live key.
- Do not publish shadow-score features, thresholds or attacker-useful tuning details.
