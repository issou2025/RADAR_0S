# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Build an admin web panel with manual video face verification**

*Project description:*
## Why

Paying for an automated face-check API makes no sense at our size. Until volume is very high, we will verify every profile by hand. Members record a short liveness video in the app, we store it privately, and an admin reviews it in a web panel next to the member's photos. Approving it turns on the verified badge everywhere.

To do that we need an **admin web panel**, built inside `clubmatesWeb`. Face verification is its first job. User review, reports and photo moderation come next.

This replaces the "call an API" part of #3 for now. #3 stays open for automating checks later.

## How it works

1. During onboarding the member sees **Get verified**, with a **Skip for now** option. Verification is never required to enter the app. They can also start it later from Profile → Verification or from a reminder.
2. The app records a **10-second video** while guiding them to blink, turn their head right to left, then left to right, in clear light.
3. The video uploads to a **private storage bucket**, and a verification request is created with status `PENDING`.
4. The app shows **Under review: usually approved within 2 hours**. The member can keep using the app, and the badge stays off.
5. An admin opens the request in the panel, watches the video next to the profile photos, and chooses **Approve**, **Reject** or **Ask for a retake**.
6. On approval, `profiles.is_verified` becomes `true`. The app shows **You're verified**, and the verified tag appears on their profile, their Club card and in Mates.
7. The member gets a push notification with the result.

### Skipping, visibility and the 48-hour hold
- Members who skip can use the app straight away.
- **Verified** profiles are promoted in Club, shown first in the deck.
- Profiles with a pending request are shown normally.
- Profiles without a submitted video are **not promoted**: they are ranked after verified and pending profiles.
- **48 hours after the account is created**, a profile with no pending or approved request goes **on hold**:
  - It is hidden from Club for everyone else.
  - Existing mates and chats keep working.
  - The member sees a banner: "Your profile is on hold. Record a 10-second video to show up in Club again."
- The hold lifts **as soon as a video is submitted**; they don't have to wait for approval. If the request is later rejected or a retake is asked for, the hold comes back until they submit again.
- Reminder notifications for members who haven't submitted:
  - at **24 hours**: "Get verified to stand out in Club"
  - at **44 hours**: "Your profile goes on hold in 4 hours"
  - at **48 hours**, when the hold starts: "Your profile is on hold"
  - one more after 3 days, then stop

  Each reminder is sent once and respects the member's notification settings.

## Part 2: Supabase (storage, data, rules)

### Storage
- [ ] New **private** bucket `verification-videos`, limited to 15 MB and the types `video/mp4` and `video/quicktime`.
- [ ] Path: `<user_id>/<request_id>.mp4`.
- [ ] Policies: a member can **upload only into their own folder** and can **never read, list or delete** videos, including their own. Only the admin backend reads them, through short-lived signed URLs.

### Tables
- [ ] `face_verifications`
  - `id`, `user_id`, `storage_path`, `status` (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `RETAKE`)
  - `duration_ms`, `device` (platform and app version), `submitted_at`
  - `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`, `decision_reason`, `video_deleted_at`
  - Only one `PENDING` request per member at a time.
  - Row-level security: a member can read **only the status fields** of their own rows, never `storage_path` or who reviewed it. No direct insert, update or delete from the app.
- [ ] `admin_users`: `user_id`, `role` (`ADMIN` or `REVIEWER`), `created_at`. Not readable or writable from the app.
- [ ] `admin_audit_log`: `admin_id`, `action`, `target_user_id`, `target_id`, `details` (JSON), `created_at`. Rows can only be added, never edited or deleted.

### Functions (RPCs)
- [ ] `submit_face_verification(p_request_id, p_duration_ms, p_device)`, for the member:
  - Checks the uploaded object exists in their folder.
  - Creates the `PENDING` row.
  - Rate-limited to 3 submissions a day and 10 a month.
  - Rejected for blocked or inactive accounts.
- [ ] `my_verification_status()` returns the status and reason only.
- [ ] `review_face_verification(p_id, p_decision, p_reason)`, for admins only:
  - Checks `admin_users`.
  - Sets the status and, on approval, `profiles.is_verified = true`. A later rejection of an already-verified member is an explicit admin action that sets it back to `false`.
  - Writes an audit row and queues a push notification.
- [ ] The existing `protect_profile_fields` trigger stays. Members still can never change `is_verified` themselves.

### Retention and deletion
- [ ] Delete the video **30 days after a decision** (set `video_deleted_at`), using a nightly cron like the chat cleanup.
- [ ] Deleting an account removes all verification videos and rows.
- [ ] Never log video URLs, faces or phone numbers.

### Visibility and hold
- [ ] `get_discovery_candidates` hides members whose account is **older than 48 hours** and who have **no `PENDING` or `APPROVED`** request.
- [ ] It orders results verified first, then pending, then unsubmitted members within their first 48 hours.
- [ ] `my_verification_status()` also returns `on_hold` and `hold_starts_at`, so the app can show the banner and countdown.

### Push
- [ ] Extend the `notify` Edge Function with a `VERIFICATION_RESULT` type: "You're verified" or "Please record your verification again".
- [ ] Add a `VERIFICATION_REMINDER` type, sent by an hourly cron at 24 hours, 44 hours, 48 hours and 3 days after sign-up. Track what was sent in a `verification_reminders` table so nobody gets the same reminder twice. Skip members who have submitted, and respect their notification settings.

## Part 3: Admin web panel (`clubmatesWeb`, under `/admin`)

### Access and security
- [ ] Only users listed in `admin_users` can open `/admin`. The check happens **on the server** for every page and action, not just by hiding links.
- [ ] Admins must use **two-factor login** (Supabase TOTP multi-factor, assurance level 2) before they reach any admin page.
- [ ] All admin data loads on the server. The service role key and database URL never reach the browser, and nothing goes through a public API route.
- [ ] Videos and photos are shown through **signed URLs valid for about 5 minutes**, created per view.
- [ ] Every admin action is written to `admin_audit_log`.
- [ ] Session timeout of about 30 minutes idle.
- [ ] Rate-limit admin actions and protect forms against cross-site request forgery (we already have `lib/security/rateLimit.ts`).
- [ ] `noindex` on all admin pages, excluded from the sitemap and `robots`.

### Pages
1. **Dashboard**
   - [ ] Counts: pending verifications, open reports, new sign-ups today and this week, verified members.
2. **Verification queue** (main screen)
   - [ ] List of `PENDING` requests, oldest first, with name, age, city, time waiting and attempt number.
- [ ] Highlight requests waiting **over 2 hours** (our promised review time), and show the count on the dashboard.
   - [ ] Review screen:
     - The video player, with replay and 0.5× speed.
     - The member's approved profile photos next to it.
     - Name, age, city and account age.
     - Earlier attempts and their reasons.
   - [ ] Reviewer checklist: real person and not a screen or photo, blinks visible, head turns both ways, face matches the profile photos, clear light, only one person.
   - [ ] Buttons: **Approve**, **Ask for retake** (with a reason picker plus a note) and **Reject** (with a reason, and an option to also suspend the account).
   - [ ] Keyboard shortcuts and "next in queue" after each decision.
3. **Users**
   - [ ] Search by name or user ID. Show phone numbers masked (`+91 ••••• ••123`), with a full reveal only for `ADMIN`, and log every reveal.
   - [ ] Filters: verified, pending, reported, suspended.
4. **User profile**
   - [ ] Profile: photos, prompts, plan tonight, interests, bio, city, height, sex, created date.
   - [ ] Verification history, including the video if it hasn't been deleted yet.
   - [ ] Reports made against them and by them, block counts, mates count.
   - [ ] Actions: verify or unverify, suspend or reactivate (`account_status`), remove a photo. Each action asks for confirmation and a reason.
5. **Reports**
   - [ ] Queue from the existing `user_reports` table, including the reported message where there is one.
   - [ ] Mark as resolved or dismissed, or open the user.
6. **Photo moderation**
   - [ ] Queue for `profile_photos.moderation_status`: approve or reject.
7. **Audit log**
   - [ ] Who did what and when, filterable by admin and user.

## Out of scope for this issue
- Automated face matching or liveness checks (stays in #3).
- Aadhaar or PAN document upload.
- Showing the video, match result or reasons to other members. They only see the badge.

## Decisions
- **Verification is optional to enter the app.** Members can skip, and verified profiles are promoted. Members who haven't submitted a video are not promoted, and they go **on hold** (hidden from Club) 48 hours after sign-up until they submit one. They get reminder notifications before and when the hold starts.
- **Admins are the developers.** They are added to `admin_users` by hand and verify each profile from the admin panel.
- **Review time is 2 hours.** The app says "usually approved within 2 hours when recorded as asked", and the queue highlights anything waiting longer.
- Videos of rejected members are kept for the same 30 days. We can revisit this if abuse shows up.

## Done when
- [ ] A member can skip verification in onboarding and still use the app.
- [ ] A member records a guided 10-second video on iOS and Android, submits it and sees **Under review: usually within 2 hours**.
- [ ] Verified profiles appear first in Club. A profile 48 hours old with no submitted video is hidden from Club, and it comes back as soon as a video is submitted.
- [ ] Reminder notifications go out at 24 hours, 44 hours, 48 hours and 3 days, each only once, and none after a video is submitted.
- [ ] The video is in the private bucket. Members, including the owner, cannot read or list it, and anonymous users cannot reach it.
- [ ] An admin with two-factor login sees the request in `/admin`, watches the video next to the photos, and approves it.
- [ ] The member's app shows **You're verified**, the tag appears on their profile, Club card and in Mates, and they get a push notification.
- [ ] Retake and reject both show the reason in the app and allow a new attempt within the rate limits.
- [ ] A non-admin who signs in cannot open any `/admin` page, server action or signed URL.
- [ ] Every admin decision appears in the audit log.
- [ ] Videos are deleted 30 days after the decision and immediately when the account is deleted.
- [ ] Row-level security and storage policies are tested: members can't approve themselves, can't read others' requests and can't read any video.
