## Zero-paid external dependency policy
- Prefer version-pinned self-hosted open source and standard cryptography with verified license. Reject paid SaaS, trial credits, time-limited free tiers, metered/usage-based plans and silently paid fallbacks. Do not subscribe, buy, or activate paid APIs.
- Never promise an external commercial service will be 'free forever' without a supportable permanent grant. Distinguish free software licenses from hosting/CPU, disks, bandwidth, domains, Apple developer account, SMS, transaction fees and hardware, which may incur actual cost. If a demanded capability has unavoidable costs or approvals, say so candidly and mark BLOCKED_BY_COST or BLOCKED_BY_APPROVAL.
- For DRM: self-hosted Shaka Packager + keys + CBCS/CENC may be implemented without a DRM SaaS, but does NOT provide equivalent hardware CDM protection. Widevine access requires Google's license approval (Google currently says no usage fee), FairPlay production credentials require Apple's approval and Apple Developer Program membership. Under zero-paid policy mark production Widevine/FairPlay conditional/blocked; do not claim DRM complete or 'free forever' absent evidence. License/credentials never hardcoded in generated projects.
- AI live business facts: allowlisted non-sensitive Backend APIs only; never arbitrary AI-generated SQL or direct business Prisma access.
