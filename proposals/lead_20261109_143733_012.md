# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**refactor(i18n): finish the #711 seam — presentation formats through AppFormat only; Intl.defaultLocale, WorkspaceTime.displayMode and WorkspaceCurrency.installDefault are no longer written (G2 of #3482)**

*Project description:*
Part of the globalization epic **#3482** (G2). Successor of #711 (the seam), #701 (dates in `en_US`) and #1150 (the 25 `DateFormat.Hm()` sites). Touches `lib/core/i18n/`, `lib/core/time/workspace_time.dart`, `lib/features/**/presentation` and `test/lint/`.

## Outcome

Every number, amount, date and time a member reads in a screen goes through `AppFormat`, in the member's format locale, clock and zone. The `appFormat` provider stops writing three globals (`Intl.defaultLocale`, `WorkspaceTime.displayMode`, `WorkspaceCurrency.installDefault`), because nothing in presentation depends on them any more. One money path, one percent path, one time path.

## Measured (master ad22e6f68)

- `app_format.dart` describes itself as the seam for "the two dozen `DateFormat.MMMd()` calls the app grew before this seam existed". Today: **189** direct `DateFormat`/`NumberFormat` calls outside `lib/core/i18n`, **175** of them under `lib/features/**/presentation` (money/presentation/widgets 33, workspace/presentation/widgets 26, reservations/presentation/widgets 13, …).
- **41** no-argument calls (`DateFormat.yMMMd()` ×11, `MMMd()` ×11, `MMMEd()` ×6, `E()` ×5, `d()` ×3, …) that only read correctly because `format_controller.dart:64` writes `Intl.defaultLocale`.
- Two money paths side by side: **100** `moneyFormat(` sites in `lib/features` (locale from the global) against **87** `AppFormat` method calls. `MoneyFormat` has a documented legitimate use, the document locale passed explicitly (`bill_pdf.dart:167`, `report_data.dart:296`); the other ~95 are screens.
- **43** `.toLocal()` in `lib/features/**/presentation` (event lines, note rows, the validation trail, MCP usage cards, directory chips, the personal overview…): the device clock, whatever `TimeZoneMode` the member chose. **9** `WorkspaceTime.display` calls honour it only through the second global.
- `ClockPref.h12 => DateFormat.jm('en_US')` twice (`app_format.dart:110,118`): a French or Italian member who chooses the 12-hour clock gets English `AM/PM` markers and spacing.
- The VAT-rate render `'${p.toStringAsFixed(0)} %'` (whole rate without decimals, else as typed) is written in `vat_price_label.dart`, `vat_report_actions.dart`, `invoice_detail_sections.dart` and `invoice_preview_body.dart`; `AppFormat.percent` takes a fraction and `percentText` (`lib/core/text/number_text.dart`) is the file renderer, so none of the four could use either.
- `format_controller.dart:64–66`: the three writes, documented in its own words as "a thing to be suspicious of". `appFormatOf(context)` is the no-`ref` reader; `no_raw_dateformat_test.dart` tells the developer to use `AppFormat.read(context)`, which does not exist.
- `no_raw_dateformat_test.dart` pins only `DateFormat.Hm(` and `DateFormat.jm(` (#1150's sweep). Everything above is unpinned.

## Shape (each checkbox one PR, in order)

- [ ] **Ratchet first.** `test/lint/no_raw_dateformat_test.dart` becomes `format_seam_test.dart` on the `literal_ratchet` kit: counts of `DateFormat.`, `NumberFormat.`, `moneyFormat(`, `.toLocal()` and `WorkspaceTime.display` under `lib/features/**/presentation`, recorded ceilings (189/41/100/43/9 as measured), down only. Fix the reason text to `appFormatOf(context)`.
- [ ] **`AppFormat.rate(double percent)`** (`20 %`, `5,5 %`, `7.7 %` per locale, whole rates without decimals); the four copies go. `percentText` stays for files.
- [ ] **Dates.** The 41 no-argument and the explicit `DateFormat.X(locale)` calls in presentation → `fmt.date / shortDate / monthDay / …`. Where a shape is missing (`weekday`, `monthYear`, `dayNumber`), add it to `AppFormat` once rather than keep a raw call.
- [ ] **Times and zones.** The 43 `.toLocal()` and the 9 `WorkspaceTime.display` → `fmt.time / dateTime / wallTime`; `zoneSuffix` beside any time a device-clock reader sees.
- [ ] **Money.** `moneyFormat(` in presentation → `fmt.money / moneyCompact`. `MoneyFormat(code, locale:)` keeps the document path only and its `locale` becomes required.
- [ ] **12-hour clock.** `h12 => DateFormat('h:mm a', locale)`; `'en_US'` leaves `app_format.dart`.
- [ ] **Delete the globals.** `Intl.defaultLocale =`, `WorkspaceTime.displayMode =` and the `displayMode` field, `WorkspaceCurrency.installDefault` (keep `install` for the ambient booking grain, which is #711's documented design) leave `appFormat`. The provider becomes pure; its doc comment loses the "three side effects" section.
