## A source's schema-invalid shapes still pass through self-compare, standalone accept/reject and the glossary

A follow-up to #1712, found by MiniMax and crush in their reviews of that change.

#1712 normalizes three schema-invalid shapes that a source carries, in every part the comparison writes:

- an empty `w:shd/@w:fill`;
- `w:gridCol/@w:type`;
- `w:tblCellMar`/`w:tcMar` children out of order.

Three paths still pass them through unchanged:

1. **Self-compare.** Comparing a document with itself returns the source package as it is (IDENTICAL-INPUT-01, `src/document_comparer.rs:6559`).
2. **Standalone accept and reject.** `jubarte accept` and `jubarte reject` on a source never run the sweep (`src/revision_processor.rs`). Rejecting a redline an older build made also restores its dirty `tcPrChange`, `tblGridChange` and `tblPrChange` snapshots verbatim.
3. **The glossary.** `word/glossary/document.xml` is copied byte for byte. A part that is no story the comparison wrote comes out as it went in (`m_cell_final_marks::a_part_that_is_no_story_is_copied_as_it_was`, #1680 round 11).

`jubarte validate` reports none of the three shapes either.

Word opens all three cleanly: no alert and no repair prompt (Word 16 A/B, 2026-10-24, probes `s1`–`s4` in `~/temp/T/fix013w/ab1712/`). So none of them is Word-fatal, but the Office 2019 validator refuses all three.
