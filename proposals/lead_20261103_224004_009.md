*Project description:*
## What was asked

The app must speak Portuguese, English, Spanish and French (João, 2026-11-03):

- by default, the device's language; when the app does not have it, **English** (today the fallback is Portuguese, `lib/ui/core/app_locales.dart:12`);
- the language can be changed in an intuitive place;
- the picker is intuitive, good-looking and consistent with the current style;
- the plan names the AI skills and tools in this project's scope.

The localization base is on `main` since #225 / `v1.3.0`: texts in `lib/l10n/app_pt.arb` (template) and `app_en.arb`, typed `Failure`/`UserNotice`, and the `ui_hard_coded_text` rule of `tool/check_layers.dart`. Adding a language is mostly adding an ARB file.

## Decisions

Agreed with João:
- **D1** The picker is reached from the **side menu** (an *Idioma* item below *Trocar de organização*, the current language on its right) and from a **globe button** on MonAmie's entry screens (Login, Sem organização, Escolher organização), so a person who does not read the device's language can change it before signing in.
- **D2** The choice is a **bottom sheet** in the style of `adminActionSheet` (`lib/ui/core/ui/admin_ui.dart`) with the four languages, each written in itself, and the one in use marked: the device's language until the person chooses, English when the device's language is not offered. There is **no "device language" row**: the sheet always marks a concrete language. The change applies at once, no Save button. The sheet wears the colors of the screen that opens it (approved boards in the design canvas): white from the menu, navy ground and cream text from the Login, cream ground and white cards from *Sem organização* and *Escolher organização*.
- **D3** Spanish and French are translated by AI (a translator agent and a separate reviewer agent, with a fixed glossary) and **reviewed by a native speaker** before the version is promoted to closed testing.
- **D4** Play release notes also in Spanish and French.

Taken by the agent (decide-and-note, open to challenge):
- **D5** The choice is stored **on the device** (`SharedPreferences`), not in the account: entry screens need it before sign-in, and no Firestore schema changes.
- **D6** `app_pt.arb` stays the template; only the fallback becomes English.
- **D7** One neutral Spanish file `app_es.arb` (*tú*) and one French file `app_fr.arb` (*vous*).
- **D8** No Android 13+ per-app language (`localeConfig`) for now: it would be a second source of truth (follow-up issue below).
- **D9** One branch, one commit per sub-issue, one PR at the end, as in #203.

## Design

- `AppLanguage` (`lib/domain/models/`): `pt, en, es, fr` and their `Locale`, plus a pure rule `resolve(deviceLocales)`: the first device locale whose language the app has, else English. `MaterialApp` and the sheet both use it, so the language marked in the sheet is the one the app shows.
- `LanguageRepository` (`lib/data/repositories/language/`, contract + `SharedPreferences` implementation) in the **app scope** (the Login needs it too), exposing one `ValueListenable<AppLanguage?>`: `null` until the person chooses (the device's language is in use), then their choice. `MonAmieApp` passes the resulting locale to `MaterialApp.router(locale:)`.
- `LanguageViewModel` (freezed state, `Command1 choose`); the sheet opens on the root navigator and receives it as a parameter.
- Each ARB names its own language (`languageName`); the sheet reads `lookupAppLocalizations(locale).languageName`, so no literal and no `check_layers` exception.
