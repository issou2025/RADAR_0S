# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Wall niches and recesses (parametric, hosted)**

*Project description:*
### Problem

In renovation and interior design it is very common to need recesses or niches in walls (radiator niches, ornamental niches, built-in shelves, etc.).

Currently the only way to create a void in a wall is with a door or window opening (which cuts through the entire wall thickness).


### Proposed solution

### Requested feature
(request wrote with the help of Grok)

Native **Wall Niche / Recess** tool:
- Hosted element on a wall (like doors/windows).
- Parameters: width, height, depth (or “to opposite face”), sill height, and optional arched/rounded top.
- Does **not** cut completely through the wall (unless depth = full thickness).
- Appears correctly in plans, elevations, sections and 3D.
- Can host a radiator component or other objects later.
- Schedules should report volume of material removed.

### Reference
ArchLine.XP has a dedicated “Wall recess / niche” command that works exactly this way.

### Why it matters
Essential for realistic interiors and for many renovation projects (especially Italian existing buildings).

### IFC export considerations

Niches should be exported as proper voids of the wall, not as separate elements that just “look like” a recess.

Recommended approach (in order of preference):

1. **IfcOpeningElement** with limited depth  
   - Create an `IfcOpeningElement` whose extrusion depth equals the niche depth (not the full wall thickness).  
   - Relate it to the wall with `IfcRelVoidsElement`.  
   - This is the cleanest and most interoperable solution (Revit, Archicad, etc. understand it).  
   - The opening’s profile can be rectangular or arched.

2. **Boolean subtraction on the wall solid** (fallback)  
   - Export the wall body as `IfcBooleanClippingResult` or `IfcBooleanResult` (Difference) where the subtracted solid is the niche volume.  
   - Useful if you want the niche already “baked” into the geometry for viewers that ignore openings.

Avoid exporting the niche as a separate `IfcBuildingElementProxy` or as a negative component — it breaks quantity take-offs and wall schedules in receiving software.

Schedules in ConstructCraft should still report the volume of material removed.
