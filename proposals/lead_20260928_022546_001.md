# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Element records beyond 2024/2025: derive the marker from the schema and measure the older envelope (RE-80 follow-up)**

*Project description:*
RE-80 (reports/element-framing/RE-80-schema-tag-constants.md) showed the element-record marker is `[tag of Outline][0xFF x 4][tag of ElementParents]` in the file's own schema, on every release from 2016 to 2026: every such prefix in the 11-release family corpus and three projects is followed by a well-formed bounding box. The record-chain header (RE-35) carries the ElementHeader tag, but only from 2024.

So the marker a reader needs is no longer the blocker for other releases. What is:

- **2026**: the chain and the markers are present in the 2026 family file. A 2026 project with Revit's own export is needed to confirm the 88-byte prologue and the RE-21 instance rule before `supports_revit_version` admits it.
- **2019 to 2023**: markers are present but no RE-35 chain; the prologue before the marker must be measured. Steffen's pre-2024 envelope (Discussion #112, measured on 2015 and 2017) is the lead.
- **2016 to 2018**: same, and the second prologue (RE-30) is unmeasured.

Acceptance:
- `bbox_marker` derives the marker from `RevitFile::schema_classes`, and is cross-checked against the 2024 and 2025 constants.
- For each release admitted, a project and Revit's own export of it (licensed or local-only, per docs/reference-models.md) reproduce the exported ElementId set, as RE-32 did for 2025.
- Releases without such a pair stay fail-closed.
