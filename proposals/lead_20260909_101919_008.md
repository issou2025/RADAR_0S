## Owner decision — canonical, 09/09/2026

The product must make location availability explicit.

If the driver phone/app stops producing location while the vehicle is expected to be tracked, Operations must see a clear state equivalent to:

**“MẤT GPS / KHÔNG NHẬN ĐƯỢC VỊ TRÍ”**

with:

- last received time;
- age of the last observation;
- source (phone vs hardware/telematics when known);
- no stale coordinate presented as “current”.

Future vehicle-mounted GPS/GSHT/telematics must be pluggable as an independent source. A phone going silent must not prevent the architecture from later using hardware telemetry.

Important semantic distinction:

> The server can observe **loss of location updates**. From silence alone it cannot truthfully conclude whether the driver closed the app, disabled GPS, lost network, the OS suspended the app, the battery died, or the sensor failed.

Therefore internal state/reason codes must describe **signal/update availability**, while Vietnamese UI may say “Mất GPS / Không nhận vị trí” and show last-seen evidence. Do not accuse the driver or invent the cause.

# T1 — Define location-health semantics at vehicle/source level

Build a deterministic read model that distinguishes at least these **business meanings** (exact enum names may follow repo conventions):

1. **NOT_EXPECTED / NOT_TRACKED** — no active tracking expectation or capability disabled. This is NOT “GPS lost”.
2. **LIVE / CURRENT** — recent accepted observation from a source.
3. **DEGRADED / STALE** — observations are older than the healthy window but not yet considered lost.
4. **LOST / UNAVAILABLE** — vehicle is expected to be tracked but no acceptable observation has arrived beyond the configured/derived loss window.
5. **SOURCE_FALLBACK** — phone source is lost/stale but a recent hardware/telematics source is available.
6. **ALL_SOURCES_LOST** — tracking is expected and neither phone nor hardware source is recent enough.

Do not force these exact enum names if current vocabulary has a better typed shape, but the six meanings must remain distinguishable.

### No fake cause

Absence of updates may produce a reason like `NO_RECENT_OBSERVATION`; it must not produce `DRIVER_CLOSED_APP` unless the client explicitly and reliably reported that fact before disconnection.

### Last-seen evidence

Every non-NOT_TRACKED health result should expose safe metadata sufficient for authorized Operations UI:

- `lastReceivedAt`;
- age;
- normalized source family;
- source-specific status;
- whether returned coordinate is current enough to use as current location.

Do not expose raw exact history to roles lacking existing need-to-know permissions.

`NOT TRACKED`
