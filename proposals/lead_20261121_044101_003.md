## Summary

Auditing `MaterialManagement` (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/MaterialManagement/`) and `ElementUtil` (`src/SyntheticShared/Shared/RevitAPI/ElementUtil.cs`) during [Platform Kernel, Feature Slice Seams & Legacy Allowlist Policy](https://github.com/amcgoey/Synthetic-Revit/issues/158) surfaced four latent functional defects:

1. **`MaterialUtil.ReplaceBitmapPaths` overwrites accumulated results on every iteration** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/MaterialManagement/Utilities/MaterialUtil.cs` line 154):
   - Inside the per-material loop, `results` is reassigned rather than appended, discarding repath results for every material except the last one processed.
2. **`MaterialUtil._ReplaceBitmapPaths` fails to reset `newFilePath` per asset property** (`MaterialUtil.cs` line 189):
   - `string newFilePath` is declared outside the asset-property `for` loop and never reset between iterations, allowing a resolved path from an earlier property (`UnifiedBitmap` / `BumpMap`) to bleed into subsequent properties when a match is not found, alongside an unguarded `AppearanceAssetEditScope` lacking `Cancel()` on exception.
3. **`ElementUtil.PaintElement` re-paints previously accumulated faces inside the geometry loop** (`src/SyntheticShared/Shared/RevitAPI/ElementUtil.cs` lines 535–559):
   - `paintFaces` is invoked inside `foreach (GeometryObject geo in geoElem)` over the growing face collection rather than once after collecting faces (or per `Solid` face set).
4. **`PaintElements.Execute` silently swallows all exceptions and returns `Result.Succeeded`** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/MaterialManagement/Commands/PaintElements.cs` lines 70–86):
   - Any exception (e.g., `ArgumentException` on non-paintable geometry) rolls back the entire single `Transaction` silently inside `catch { trans.RollBack(); }` and still returns `Result.Succeeded` with no partial-commit isolation or failure reporting.
