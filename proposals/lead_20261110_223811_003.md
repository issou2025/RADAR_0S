# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Send takes a Lightning request tapped over NFC from a point of sale, starting with Square Register (2nd generation)**

*Project description:*
Chicory should read a payment request over NFC and carry it into the normal review and hold, so a payer can tap a point of sale instead of finding its QR. The headline target is Square Register (2nd generation), the one Square device that takes bitcoin by tap. A tap only ever fills in Send. It never pays by itself.

## Is it possible

Very likely yes, with one unknown that a short hardware check has to settle before most of the work starts.

- Reading a request over NFC is buildable today on both platforms with public APIs. No Apple or Google program, fee or partner agreement is involved.
- Square has not published what its Register presents over NFC. The inference below fits everything public, but it is an inference. Phase 0 settles it.
- Chicory already pays a Square bitcoin QR by scanning, so the invoice side is proven. What is new is only how the request reaches the phone.
- Whatever Square does, the same reader pays tills that follow the open convention (Numo, Minibits, LaWallet POS, and static `lightning:` or `bitcoin:` tags).

## What Square supports

- Bitcoin over Lightning at about a million US sellers. On every Square device the buyer can scan a Lightning QR.
- Tap is narrower. Release notes Vol 5 (June 4, 2026, v7.10) add it to Square Register (2nd generation): buyers "pay with Bitcoin by simply tapping their phone using an NFC-enabled Lightning wallet". The help article says: "NFC bitcoin payments are not available on Square Terminal, Square Stand, or mobile devices."
- The buyer or the seller picks Bitcoin, then the buyer holds the phone near the NFC reader on the customer display. Square names Cash App, Coinbase and Wallet of Satoshi as examples of wallets that work.
- A payment that takes more than five minutes is declined. The cap is the bitcoin equivalent of $600.
- The NFC exchange is undocumented. The community list awesome-bitcoin-nfc (reviewed September 2026) marks it "protocol unpublished" and notes that which side emulates is not documented.

**The inference.** All three named wallets ship on iOS, where an ordinary app can read a tag but cannot act as one (card emulation needs Apple's HCE entitlement, which is EEA only, or the paid NFC and SE Platform agreement). So the Register most likely emulates an NFC Forum Type 4 tag (NDEF application `D2760000850101`) holding one URI record, `lightning:<bolt11>`. That is the convention other tills already use, and wallets read it with the plain NDEF APIs.

## Problem

At a Register showing a tap prompt, a Chicory user has to look for the QR instead. Chicory has no NFC code, so a tap does nothing, or opens another wallet.

## Current behavior

Verified on `main` at `3bb7c0a` with wallet-core pinned at `9027384`.

- No NFC anywhere. The manifest asks for `INTERNET`, `CAMERA` and `USE_BIOMETRIC` only (`android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:3-8`). `ios/chicory/Info.plist` has no `NFCReaderUsageDescription`, and the app target has no entitlements file and no `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS`.
- `bitcoin:` and `lightning:` links are taken on both platforms (`AndroidManifest.xml:35-46`, `Info.plist:32-35`). `usePaymentLinks` (`src/services/links.ts:16-51`, wired at `App.tsx:61-66`) dispatches `link`, which opens a fresh Send with the request filled in and without `scanned` (`src/stage/scene.ts:186-200`, `:283-286`). A link is dropped while a payment is in flight.
- React Native 0.87.1 already forwards an `ACTION_NDEF_DISCOVERED` intent that carries a URI to `Linking`, both at launch and warm (`node_modules/react-native/ReactAndroid/src/main/java/com/facebook/react/modules/intent/IntentModule.kt:56-63`, `runtime/ReactHostImpl.kt:687-699`). Android lower-cases the scheme of a URI record before matching (`NdefRecord.toUri`). So on Android a tapped `lightning:` tag can ride the link lane with a manifest filter alone.
- A camera read goes through `take` (`src/components/Scanner.tsx:1001-1013`) to the `scanned` action, which sets `scanned: true` (`scene.ts:293-300`). That flag is the only way into Auto-pay (`src/scenes/send/autoPay.ts:34`, REDESIGN.md rule 5). `take` marks everything it takes as camera-read.
- `parsePayment` in wallet-core already reads what a till is expected to present: BOLT11 with or without an amount, a `lightning:` prefix (`payment-uri.js:820`), capitals (`:534`, with the `CASE_FOLDED_FROM_QR` warning and so the 1000ms hold) and `bitcoin:?lightning=`. It refuses LNURL (`:511`) and Lightning addresses, and the engine refuses BOLT12 offers at quote.
- It also refuses an invoice whose amount is not a whole number of sats (`FRACTIONAL_SAT_INVOICE`, `payment-uri.js:543`). A Square bitcoin QR has been scanned and paid from Chicory, so Square's invoices pass this check and the rest of the parser in practice. The tap is expected to carry the same invoice as the QR, which Phase 0 confirms.
- The app lock starts its 30 second grace on `inactive` as well as `background` (`src/services/useAppLock.ts:22`, `:83-99`). The Core NFC sheet can stay up for about a minute.

## Proposed behavior

### Android

- **From anywhere, phone unlocked.** A tag carrying a `lightning:` or `bitcoin:` URI opens Chicory on Send with the request filled in, exactly as a link does. If several wallets claim the scheme, Android shows its chooser.
- **Inside the scan overlay.** While the payment scanner is open it also listens for a tag, so one surface takes a code or a tap, with no chooser. A mark in the controls row shows that it is listening. With NFC switched off, the mark is a button to the system NFC settings. On a phone with no NFC it is absent.

### iOS

- A glyph button in the scanner's controls row, beside Paste (`Scanner.tsx:1100-1128`), starts a read. iOS shows its own sheet until a tag is read, the payer cancels, or 20 seconds pass.
- iOS cannot hand a `lightning:` tag to an app in the background (background tag reading supports universal links and a few system schemes only), so the read always starts in the app.
- The button is absent where NFC reading is unavailable (iPad).

### A tap fills Send, it never pays

- A tapped request lands in the same Send as a link: review, then the hold. Auto-pay never applies to a tap. REDESIGN.md rule 5 gains "a tap" in its never list.
- Rule 6 stands: a held or already paid request lands on its ring or mark, however it arrives.
- A tap while locked, busy or before a wallet is ready behaves as a link does today.

### States

Each in the existing visual language, felt, said to a screen reader and logged by code only:

| Event | Felt | Seen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read, payable | `thud`, as a scan | Corners snap sage, the overlay collapses into Send |
| Read, not payable (LNURL, a web link, two different requests) | `error` | Radish ring and shake on the tap control; held tint under Reduce Motion |
| Tag lost or unreadable | `error` | The same refusal; Android keeps listening |
| iOS session timed out | none | Control back at rest |
| Cancelled | none | none |

## Suggested implementation

### Phase 0: learn what the Register presents (no app code)

1. Already known: a Square bitcoin QR scans and pays from today's build, so the QR's invoice is one Chicory takes. At the next sale, note the invoice's length, its expiry and whether it arrives in capitals, to compare with what the tap carries.
2. At a Register (2nd generation) in bitcoin tender, read it with NXP TagInfo on Android. Record: whether a tag appears at all, the tech list (NfcA or NfcB, IsoDep), the capability container, every NDEF record (TNF, type, payload), any Android Application Record, whether the payload changes per sale, and whether it matches the QR's invoice.
3. Try an NDEF read from an iPhone with NFC Tools.
4. Note whether a contactless card or a phone wallet starts a card payment while the Register is in bitcoin tender.
5. If no tag appears, the Register is the reader. Capture what it selects with an Android HCE probe and the NFC snoop log.

Record the findings in `docs/TAP-TO-PAY.md`, in the manner of `docs/IROH.md`.

### Phase 1a: Android, tags dispatched by the system

Add two filters to `.MainActivity` after the existing ones, for `lightning` and `bitcoin`:

```xml
<intent-filter>
    <action android:name="android.nfc.action.NDEF_DISCOVERED" />
    <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
    <data android:scheme="lightning" />
</intent-filter>
```

No permission, no native code and no JS change: the intent reaches `usePaymentLinks` through React Native's own forwarding. Add `__tests__/NativeTap.test.ts` (see Test plan). This serves the open convention whatever Phase 0 finds, so it can ship first.

### Phase 1b: Android, reading inside the scan overlay

- **A small seam**, `src/services/tapReader.ts`, over `NativeModules.ChicoryNfc`, in the manner of `src/services/processInfo.ts`: `status()` answers `'missing' | 'off' | 'ready'`, `read(prompt)` resolves the records of one tag or rejects with a code (`TAP_CANCELLED`, `TAP_TIMEOUT`, `TAP_NO_NDEF`, `TAP_TOO_LARGE`, `TAP_FAILED`), plus `cancel()` and `openSettings()`. A `setTapReader(fake)` setter serves Jest and the gallery, as `setPaymentListLookup` does (`src/services/paykit.ts:102`). With no native module the stub answers `'missing'` and the control is absent.
- **Native code returns raw records and parses nothing.** Decoding lives in a pure `src/services/ndef.ts` (URI prefix codes, exactly one payable `bitcoin:` or `lightning:` record or a refusal), so it is the same on both platforms, runs under Jest, and can change after Phase 0 without a native rebuild.
- **`android/app/src/main/java/com/chicory/NfcReaderModule.kt`**, a plain bridge module like `ProcessModule.kt`, registered at `MainApplication.kt:18-20`. `enableReaderMode` with `FLAG_READER_NFC_A | FLAG_READER_NFC_B | FLAG_READER_NO_PLATFORM_SOUNDS`, the tag's cached NDEF message, an 8 KiB cap, and `disableReaderMode` on cancel, host pause and `invalidate`.
- **Manifest:** `android.permission.NFC`, and `android.hardware.nfc` with `required="false"` so Play does not filter out phones without it.
- **Reader mode only while the payment scanner is open.** It switches off the phone's own card emulation, so holding it app-wide would break the payer's card wallet while Chicory is in front.
- **The lane.** No new action. `scanned` gains an optional `by?: 'camera' | 'tap'` (`scene.ts:100`), and only a camera read sets `scanned: true` (`:300`). Thread `by` from `Scanner.take` through `ScanReveal`, `Canvas.onScanned`, `ScanReceiver.receive` (`src/stage/StageContext.tsx`) and `SendScene.tsx:88-91`. It stays optional so existing callers and suites are untouched.
- **Design:** a `tap` glyph in `src/design/glyphs.tsx` (regenerate `glyphLengths.ts` with `scripts/glyph-lengths.mjs`), words in `src/design/copy/scan.ts`, the scanner states in REDESIGN.md and the gallery.
- **Small related fix:** add `WRONG_NETWORK` to `REFUSED` (`src/scenes/send/model.ts:356-371`), so a mainnet till tapped by a test wallet draws as a refused request and not as a generic failure.

### Phase 2: iOS

- `ios/chicory/NfcReaderModule.swift` and its `.m` extern, like `ProcessModule`: `NFCNDEFReaderSession` with `invalidateAfterFirstRead`, the sheet's message passed in from JS (`copy.scan`), so the Swift file holds no prose. `status()` reads `NFCNDEFReaderSession.readingAvailable`.
- `ios/chicory/chicory.entitlements` with `com.apple.developer.nfc.readersession.formats` set to `TAG` only, and `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS` in both target configurations of `project.pbxproj`.
- `NFCReaderUsageDescription` beside the camera string (`Info.plist:50-53`): "Chicory uses NFC only to read a payment request from a payment terminal. Nothing is stored or sent."
- Wrap the read in `duringSystemPrompt` (`src/stage/systemPrompt.ts:18-29`) so the privacy cover leaves the scanner visible behind the sheet.
- Cancel the session after 20 seconds, under the lock's 30 second grace, so a slow tap cannot come back to a lock screen. `useAppLock` is not changed (rule 8).
- Provisioning: turn on NFC Tag Reading for the `com.chicory` App ID and regenerate profiles. A free personal team cannot provision it, so document a `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS=` override for contributors.

### Why in-repo modules and not `react-native-nfc-manager`

The need is one NDEF read per platform, roughly a hundred lines each, and the repo already carries three bridge modules in this pattern. The library (4.2.0, New Architecture ready) brings every tag technology and writing into a wallet's process, and it does not do card emulation, which is the one thing a bad Phase 0 result would need. Because the app sees only the seam, swapping it in later is a one file change. Using it on a throwaway branch for the Phase 0 spike is fine.

### If Phase 0 disproves the inference

- **The Register is the reader and the phone must emulate.** Android can, with a `HostApduService` behind the same `TapReader.read()`, enabled only while the scanner is open. iOS cannot without Apple's programs, and stays on QR.
- **The payload is LNURL or a BOLT12 offer.** No NFC change. Paying those is separate wallet-core and engine work.
- **The exchange is proprietary or tied to named partners.** Not buildable. Keep both lanes for the open convention, drop Square from the title, and document QR as the way to pay at a Register.

## Trust and privacy

- A tag is unauthenticated, like a QR sticker, and unlike one it is invisible. A hidden emulator near a till could present its own request. The defence is the one a link has: the review shows the amount and the hold commits it. That is why a tap never Auto-pays.
- More than one payable record on a tag is refused as ambiguous. An oversize message is refused natively, then by the parser's own cap (`payment-uri.js:800`).
- A read sends nothing from the wallet. No payload is logged, only codes.
- Android may redeliver a launch intent from recents, and with it an old tap. Rule 6 and `INVOICE_EXPIRED` contain it.

## Out of scope

- Card emulation on the payer's phone, on either platform, unless Phase 0 requires it.
- Square Terminal, Stand and phone sellers, which are QR only on Square's side.
- LNURL, Lightning addresses, BOLT12 offers and Bolt Cards.
- Receiving by tap, and Chicory acting as a till.
- A Settings switch. A tap has the trust of a link, which has none.
- Auto-pay for taps, a Home Screen quick action, and Text records on a tag.

- Jest, `__tests__/Ndef.test.ts`: the URI prefix table, capitals and mixed case schemes, `bitcoin:?lightning=`, several records (one payable among others, two different payables, duplicates), an absolute URI record, an empty message, bad UTF-8 and oversize.
- Jest, `__tests__/TapReader.test.ts`: the missing module stub, the error codes, `cancel`, and the 20 second deadline with fake timers.
- Jest, existing suites: `SceneReducer.test.ts` (`by: 'tap'` opens Send without `scanned`, the default is unchanged), `ScanOverlay.test.tsx`, `ScanPrompts.test.tsx` (the iOS read runs inside a system prompt span), `guards/scan.test.tsx`, and `auto-pay.test.tsx` ("an invoice tapped" beside "an invoice pasted").
- Source contract, `__tests__/NativeTap.test.ts`, in the manner of `NativeLaunch.test.ts` and `CameraUsage.test.ts`: both NDEF filters with lowercase schemes and no `TECH_DISCOVERED` or `TAG_DISCOVERED`; the NFC permission and the feature with `required="false"`; React Native still naming `ACTION_NDEF_DISCOVERED` in `IntentModule.kt` and `ReactHostImpl.kt`; the package registered and `disableReaderMode` called; the exact plist string; `UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities` (`Info.plist:77`) not gaining `nfc`; the entitlement holding `TAG` only; the session deadline shorter than `BACKGROUND_GRACE_MS`.
- Device, without a Register:
  - An NTAG216 sticker (888 bytes, so an invoice with route hints fits) written with NFC Tools: lowercase and capitals, a BIP21 link, LNURL, a web link, two records, no amount, expired, another network.
  - A second Android phone as the till. Numo is an open source point of sale that emulates a tag with a `lightning:` invoice. It issues mainnet invoices, so use small amounts. For regtest and testnet, a minimal `HostApduService` fixture under `native-tests/tap-emulator/` (a Type 4 tag answering SELECT and READ BINARY, the payload set by an intent extra), built apart from the app like `native-tests/android-qualification.gradle`.
  - Android: cold start, warm from Home, from the scanner, with a second wallet installed (the chooser), locked, mid payment, NFC off.
  - iOS: the button, cancel, timeout, with the lock on, with the camera denied, and a QR read while the sheet is up.
- Device, at a Register: the happy path on both platforms, checking that the invoice paid is the one the Register settles.

## Assumptions

- The Register emulates a tag that carries a BOLT11 invoice in a `lightning:` URI record. Unverified until Phase 0.
- The tap carries the same kind of invoice as Square's QR, which Chicory already pays by scanning. One paid QR supports whole-sat amounts but does not prove every sale rounds that way.
- The Core NFC sheet makes the app `inactive`, as the Face ID and paste prompts do. The 20 second cap holds either way.
- No wallet-core or engine change is needed for a whole-sat BOLT11 or BIP21 payload.

## Open questions

- Should Phase 1a ship before Phase 0 returns? It is small and serves open tills either way, but it also makes Chicory a candidate in Android's chooser for every `lightning:` tag.
- Should an explicit, button-started tap ever qualify for Auto-pay? It is as deliberate as a camera scan. A system-dispatched Android tap and the silently listening scanner should never qualify.
- With no prose outside Settings, is a glyph and its Whisper enough for people to find this, or does it earn a line in Settings?
- Should Android get an opt-out for the system lane? It would cost a Keychain entry and an `activity-alias` to gate the filter.
- Should an amountless `bitcoin:` address on a tag be refused, since the payer cannot see where it came from?
