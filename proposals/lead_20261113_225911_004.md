# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Beams run to the ends of their location line, where Revit's export cuts them back at the columns they frame into (#1829)**

*Project description:*
Found by HWT-25's report (#1829) on a confidential Revit 2027 structural model (CHL): a concrete frame, read with 0.18.0 and compared with Revit's IFC4 export.

**What happens.**
- Only 275 of 694 beams match Revit's box and volume.
- The rest come out longer, by a median of about 475 mm. They run to the ends of their location line, where Revit's export cuts them back at the columns they frame into.
- Shear walls modelled as structural columns do not cut the beams either.
- In the reporter's clash run, this adds about 160 clashes that Revit's IFC does not give.

**What the reader does.** A beam's body runs along the line its record holds: `partition_beam_axis` (604 of CHL's beams) and `partition_beam_box_along_axis` (310). No join between a beam and a column is read.

**To find out first:**
- Where Revit keeps the cutback. Either the beam's own record (its join cutbacks, or the line's ends) holds it, or only the joined geometry does.
- Whether the saved mesh of a cut beam is the cut body. On CHL, the saved-mesh export matched 72.6% of elements, against 18.7% without saved meshes.
- Which measured models have beams framing into columns in Revit's export, so that a rule can be scored. The BIM4LCA offices' 291 beams (RE-428) are one candidate.

**Done when** the beams Revit cuts at a column are cut by the same amount on the measured models. If no record holds the cut, the negative is reported.
