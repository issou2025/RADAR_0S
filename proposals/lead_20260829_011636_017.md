## The decision

Home Assistant is demoted to a device driver. All grow state and logic move into a tested Python service (the Brain) with recipes as data. The phone UI is a **Grow tab in the Nat app**, not a fourth Lovelace dashboard. Jeff is hand-managing one sunflower tray during the rebuild, so there is **no migration constraint** — the old state is deleted, not ported.

## Why now (diagnostic, 2026-08-28)

The tent ran for ~6 months looking healthy while doing nothing:

- `production_mode` was on and the **light schedule still worked**
- **The Kasa integration was dead** — all 6 power sensors reading `N/A`, entity IDs drifted, manual dashboard buttons inert. Automations were firing into a void.
- Slot helpers held **180–195 day ghost grows** from January/February
- Bottom camera snapshot **frozen since Feb 18**
- 47 pending HA updates spanning breaking syntax changes

**Every one of those failures was invisible from the dashboard.** That is the thesis of this rebuild.

## Two rules that came out of it

1. **The 64-reference problem.** The six switch entity IDs appear 64 times across the YAML (`exhaust_fan` alone: 19). One device re-pair silently broke the system. Entity IDs live in **exactly one config file** from now on (`06-brain-spec.md` §0.4).
2. **Silent failure is the enemy.** Verification and watchdogs are not polish, they are the product. Every actuation is confirmed by power draw; every sensor has a staleness watchdog; the Brain has a deadman switch.
