Description

This project aims to develop an AI-powered multi-agent automation system specifically designed for Shopify dropshipping businesses.

The system will consist of specialized AI agents working together to automate and optimize the entire dropshipping marketing workflow.

Core Agents

1. Product Research Agent
   
   - Finds potentially profitable products.
   - Analyzes product demand and competition.
   - Evaluates supplier prices and shipping costs.
   - Estimates profit margins.

2. Market Research Agent
   
   - Analyzes competitors.
   - Identifies market trends.
   - Researches customer demand.
   - Finds potential market opportunities.

3. TikTok Trend Research Agent
   
   - Analyzes TikTok trends.
   - Identifies winning product content.
   - Researches competitor advertising strategies.
   - Finds creative opportunities.

4. AI Creative Agent
   
   - Generates TikTok video concepts.
   - Creates advertising scripts.
   - Writes hooks, captions and CTAs.
   - Generates multiple creative variations.

5. TikTok Ads Management Agent
   
   - Creates campaign structures.
   - Recommends audience targeting.
   - Monitors campaign performance.
   - Suggests budget allocation.
   - Requires human approval before publishing campaigns or increasing advertising spend.

6. Performance Optimization Agent
   
   - Analyzes CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA and ROAS.
   - Identifies underperforming campaigns.
   - Recommends optimization strategies.
   - Calculates actual net profit.

7. Shopify Integration Agent
   
   - Connects with Shopify Admin API.
   - Monitors products, orders and inventory.
   - Tracks sales performance.
   - Analyzes store conversion rates.

8. Profit Intelligence Agent
   
   - Calculates product-level profitability.
   - Includes product cost, shipping, advertising costs, payment fees, refunds and returns.
   - Tracks COD cancellations and RTO.
   - Recommends profitable scaling opportunities.

Why Is It Useful?

Most dropshipping businesses manage product research, advertising, store operations and profitability separately.

This multi-agent system connects these activities into one intelligent workflow.

Its primary objective is to help business owners make data-driven decisions, reduce unnecessary advertising expenses, identify profitable products and improve net profit.
