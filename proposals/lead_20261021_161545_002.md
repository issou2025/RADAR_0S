# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Hip roofs over T and U footprints stay level blocks: straight-skeleton split events (RE-199 follow-up)**

*Project description:*
RE-199 (#870) draws a footprint roof with several defining edges as its edges' straight-skeleton planes. Each plane is held to the wedge between the lines where it meets its two neighbouring edges' planes. That is right for convex footprints and for an L, but not where a non-neighbouring edge's plane cuts an edge's wedge (a split event in the straight skeleton), as on a T or a U footprint with long arms. There the body comes out wrong, the record-box check rejects it, and the roof stays a level block.

**Oracle needed:** a Revit 2024 or 2025 project with a hip roof over a T or a U footprint, with Revit's IFC export of it, licensed for redistribution (as `R2025_Models.zip` is, #356).

**Done when:** the planes' domains come from the full straight skeleton (split events included), and such a roof's body matches Revit's in `tools/re/roof_bodies_vs_ifc.py`.
