*Project description:*
RE-199 (#870) reads these roof cases, or draws them by rule, but no measured model has them:

- **Revit 2024 roofs with several defining edges** (gables, hips): they take the same planes path as 2025's, gated by the record box. The only 2024 sloped roof measured is RE-56's 1:12 shed on the Autodesk tutorial house, which is local only and was not re-run.
- **A square two-cut rafter with a non-zero fascia depth:** not drawn (the roof stays a level block), since none has been measured.
- **A slope arrow whose head stops short of the far edge:** drawn with the plane running on past the head, as the PR describes Revit doing; not measured.

**Oracle needed:** small Revit 2024 and 2025 projects with these roofs and Revit's IFC export of each, licensed for redistribution. Measure would score them as it scores `R2025_Models.zip` (`-f r2025=true`, `tools/re/roof_bodies_vs_ifc.py`).
