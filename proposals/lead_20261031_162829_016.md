## NON-NEGOTIABLE: MEASURABLE EXPERT-LEVEL AUTONOMOUS QUANTITATIVE INTELLIGENCE

**End state:** RivaL is a continuously operating, evidence-grounded **quantitative market research and model-governance system** for exchanges, Polymarket **and** Kalshi. It uses the existing RivaL brain and infrastructure, never requires Discord usage to learn, and keeps models/controls appropriate to the exact selected market and outcome. “Complete expert” is a **measurable capability goal, not a claim of infallibility, guaranteed profit, unrestricted autonomous code modification or universal prediction skill**.

### Research competencies to implement and keep current
- **Probabilistic forecasting, not binary guesswork:** learn an empirically tested conditional *distribution* for underlying settlement variables and tradable contract/futures prices over each horizon. Explicitly represent tails, jumps, regime changes, forecast uncertainty and correlated paths; compare parametric and nonparametric distributional approaches against strong simple baselines. Score probability calibration and full distributions with proper scores (e.g., log loss/Brier for events, CRPS or appropriate distribution scores for prices), interval coverage and tail-risk checks.
- **First-passage and competing-risk targets:** estimate `P(TP1 before stop by horizon)`, `P(TP2 before stop by horizon)`, `P(stop before TP)`, `P(neither)`, and the separate **exact contract settlement** probability. Validate these from causally ordered paths with time/expiry/cutoffs, venue tick and suspension conditions, and ambiguity-aware fills. No invented percentages; do not use pure direction probability as substitute.
- **Market microstructure and economics:** learn venue-specific order-book spread/depth, market impact, quote staleness, realized/ex ante volatility, slippage/partial-fill likelihood, fees, liquidity withdrawal and time-to-expiry dynamics. Price all setup legs at plausible executable bid/ask, include uncertainty and abstain on missing costs or unreliable quotes.
- **Continuous scientific learning:** issue immutable prospective shadow forecasts unattended, verify their exact later outcomes, learn only after authoritative labels mature, maintain offline historical/point-in-time experiments and online challenger models, and use controlled chronological/rolling holdouts with purging/embargo when horizons overlap. Preserve model artifacts, feature definitions, training fingerprints, lineage, corrections, and exact original forecast probabilities.
- **Independent forward testing and promotion:** continuously compare incumbent/challengers and appropriate market-implied or simple distribution baselines using *matched* prediction events and grouped independence units (contract/event/date). Measure calibration by probability bin, Brier/log loss, path/first-hit coverage, distribution fit, missed-event rate, net after-cost paper PnL, drawdown and economic sensitivity to fees/slippage, separately per venue/series/horizon and market regime. Repeated tests and model selection require guardrails against overfitting. A favorable backtest or one great week never promotes an unqualified model or proves actual fills.
- **Automatic diagnostic loop:** investigate wrong/off-target outcomes and novel data/worker errors from timestamped evidence; distinguish aleatory losses from real model mistakes, data/source corruption, misidentified contracts, liquidity shocks and execution assumptions. Form causal hypotheses, run reproducible offline checks and prospective challenger evaluations, quarantine known-bad inputs or degraded candidates, and retain incumbent models unless independently validated improvements justify a controlled change.
- **Safe self-governance:** defined, auditable policy may select and roll back already-reviewed model configurations **only after** strict forward gates; no self-modifying/deploying Python, changing security boundaries or inventing labels. Missing or deteriorating evidence yields honest **NO CALL** plus a diagnostic reason.
- **Actual automatic operation:** integrate with current PerpetualMarketCollector, AutonomousResearch, Polymarket book/outcome learning, Kalshi forward research, model checkpoints, health and archive/settlement workers. Bounded schedules and persistent cursors must work without user interaction or extra DMs, across retries/restarts, respecting rate limits and latency. Market-specific backfill can support research, but must never be scored as issuance-time forward evidence.

- **LONG / SHORT / NO QUALIFIED SETUP** decision; market symbol and exact quote currency.
- Verifiably fresh **entry price/zone**, optional invalidation/expiry, **stop-loss**, **TP1 and TP2** levels, estimated position-agnostic reward/risk and time horizon.
- **Probability that each target is hit before the stop within the horizon**, estimated from an honest price-path distribution (not directional confidence, candle direction, arbitrary percentages or an LLM's adjectives).
- Clear explanation of mathematical signals, source freshness, volatility, liquidity, spreads, order-book depth, fees, expected slippage, assumptions and no-call reasons.
- Evidence panel with forward sample size, calibration, Brier/log loss, coverage, expected after-cost result and uncertainty; never claim guaranteed profit.

## Polymarket and Kalshi full target calls (user selects venue; mandatory feature parity)

The complete target-call experience **must be built for both Polymarket and Kalshi** when a user chooses either market, but **never recycle perpetual-market entry, TP, SL, or path-probability math as if it were contract-native**. Use separate adapters, market-specific settlement policies and independent proof cohorts. This mode is **optional**: Exchange Perpetuals remains the default.

- **Simple market selector:** `Market → Exchange Perpetuals | Polymarket | Kalshi → exact event/contract + YES/NO or UP/DOWN outcome → expiry/horizon → paper setup`. Hide unavailable venues and never silently switch a user's selected market.
- **Exact immutable contract identity:** provider market ID, condition/event ID, outcome token/ticker, official market question, resolution source, payout amount/currency, start/end/cutoff, tie/void/cancel provisions, and proof of the current rule version. Validate freshness, liquidity, settlement eligibility, restrictions and provider health. Incompatible/unconfirmed rules must return **NO CALL**.
- **Contract-native paper setup:** enter **YES/NO or UP/DOWN** at a quoted *share/contract price*, with model-generated market-price **TP1/TP2 exit bids** and a modeled conditional stop/invalidation level, plus time-bounded **target-before-stop probabilities**. For Kalshi cents and Polymarket dollars, normalize units precisely; calculate maximum theoretical loss if held to expiry (possibly full paid premium), bid/ask spread, fees and slippage. A stop or TP is **not guaranteed to execute**, especially across market suspension, wide spreads or resolution.
- **Separate probabilities:** `P(contract resolves YES/UP)` must correspond to the exact market's official outcome and price-to-beat/tie/void rules; `P(exit price hits TP before stop and expiry)` is a different first-passage question. Do not substitute a BTC spot threshold, exchange direction score, CLOB implied odds or LLM-generated confidence for verified settlement probability.
- **Expected value and execution:** use validated outcome probabilities, contract payout, fees, estimated fill prices and execution uncertainty. Distinguish a profitable settlement thesis from a feasible early exit. For absent or stale order books, impossible size/tick, unavailable oracle or unproved model calibration, abstain rather than invent levels.
- **Paper audit:** save exact bid/ask snapshot, rule/source version, timestamp, expiry, venue, fees, user-selected preferences and immutable hypothetical position. Track exit filled vs unfilled, stop/TP race, expiry settlement, split/void/cancelled outcomes, and any unavailable or ambiguous result separately. Never turn unfilled targets into wins.
- **Alerts:** explicit per-market opt-in; only new qualified paper setups or meaningful invalidations, bounded caps/cooldowns and no DM spam. No auto-execution or credential/trading requirement.
- **Validation:** independent Polymarket and Kalshi fixtures, negative cases for market mismatch, inverted outcome, stale quote, late strike/oracle, ties, void, fractional payouts, one-sided books, false TP/stop fills, provider outage, settlement identity and paper ledger idempotence. Forward validate against **actual same-contract settlement receipts and executable quotes**, not shared exchange-candle labels.
- **Release gate:** no qualified live signals until separate, contract-specific prospective evidence passes out-of-sample calibration, after-cost performance and source-integrity checks; defer to issue #138 for exact venue/oracle verification. Initially research/paper only.
