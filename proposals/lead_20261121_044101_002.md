## Summary

Auditing `FamilyManagement` (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/FamilyManagement/`) and `FamilyUtil` (`src/SyntheticShared/Shared/RevitAPI/FamilyUtil.cs`) during [Platform Kernel, Feature Slice Seams & Legacy Allowlist Policy](https://github.com/amcgoey/Synthetic-Revit/issues/158) surfaced three latent runtime defects:

1. **`FamiliesForceReinsert` calls `familyDoc.LoadFamily(doc, opt)` without an open `Transaction` on the host document** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/FamilyManagement/Commands/FamiliesForceReinsert.cs` lines 47–60 & `src/SyntheticShared/Shared/RevitAPI/FamilyUtil.cs` lines 270–348):
   - `FamiliesForceReinsert` is marked `[Transaction(TransactionMode.Manual)]` and delegates directly to `FamilyUtil.ForceReinsertAnnotation(doc)`, which calls `familyDoc.LoadFamily(doc, opt)` (line 333) without an outer `TransactionGroup` or per-family `Transaction` on `doc`, and without a `try/finally` block closing `familyDoc`.
2. **Null-reference hazard on `family.FamilyCategory` in `ForceReinsertAnnotation`** (`FamilyUtil.cs` line 302):
   - Dereferences `family.FamilyCategory.CategoryType` without checking `family.FamilyCategory != null`.
3. **Wrong-document `PurgeSchema(..., doc)` inside the per-family loop in `AuditPurgeEventHandler`** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/FamilyManagement/Handlers/AuditPurgeEventHandler.cs` lines 125–147):
   - Checks `StorageUtil.DoesAnyStorageExist(familyDoc)` on the opened family document, but then calls `StorageUtil.GetDocumentSchemas(doc)` and `StorageUtil.PurgeSchema(..., doc)` against the *host project document* (`doc`) inside the per-family loop—and `PurgeSchema` invokes `Schema.EraseSchemaAndAllEntities`, erasing matching extensible storage entities across all open documents in the Revit session.
