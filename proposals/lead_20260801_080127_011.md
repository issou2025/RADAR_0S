## Problem Statement

Tracefold's current News Story implementation no longer behaves like the WorldMonitor design it claims to adapt. The lexical clustering kernel is still WorldMonitor-compatible, but the persisted feature graph, similarity edges, prior membership inheritance, seven-day aliases, per-item frontiers, score-bucket frontiers, and archived Story continuity turn current 96-hour clusters into historical unions. On the live database at `2026-08-01 04:30:43 UTC`, 2,958 active NewsItems produced 1,720 active Stories and the largest current Story contained 938 items. Running the exact WorldMonitor clustering function directly over approximately the same corpus produced 2,569 clusters with a largest cluster of 50 in 9.8 seconds. The additional persistence logic, not the WorldMonitor kernel, is causing the extreme merge.

The current acquisition schema is also RSS-shaped. Every observation is tied to a fetch receipt, every NewsItem requires an HTTP URL, and observation identity includes the fetch attempt. That model cannot accept a long-lived NewsLiquid/OpenNews WebSocket cleanly. The live database contains 449,666 observation rows but only 17,683 distinct `(source, source item key, payload)` combinations, so approximately 96% of stored rows are repeated sightings rather than new evidence.

NewsLiquid/OpenNews adds further realities: its WebSocket has no resumable sequence or cursor; the same report can arrive through WebSocket, REST recovery, wrappers, and translations; many legitimate wire reports have no article URL or only a publisher homepage; and provider AI ratings and update events are not Story identity evidence. Treating every frame, connection, recovery page, translation, or provider annotation as a new business entity would repeat the current mistake.

The user needs NewsLiquid to be additive to the existing RSS pipeline, with realtime intake and bounded recovery, while keeping one deterministic NewsItem-to-Story product. The result must follow the proven WorldMonitor clustering and canonical-title behavior, accept its known precision limits, and remove Story history machinery rather than adding a second ontology, scheduler, broker, compatibility layer, or audit model.

## Solution

Keep the existing News fact and public product vocabulary:

```text
RSS/RSSHub plus NewsLiquid WebSocket/REST
  -> news_feed_observations
  -> news_items
  -> exact WorldMonitor 96-hour clustering every 60 seconds
  -> news_stories plus news_story_members
  -> existing bounded Feed, Story Detail, Status, Sources, and Brief read models
```

NewsLiquid/OpenNews is one additional acquisition provider, not a replacement for RSS and not a separate Story system. One News acquisition Module owns RSS polling, the bounded OpenNews WebSocket receiver, REST overlap recovery, normalization, and the sole NewsItem publication path. It reuses the existing News tables and adds no new domain table, provider-run entity, connection-session entity, event ontology, generic scheduler, or durable message broker.

Only `news.update` reports with `engineType=news` may materialize a NewsItem. Translation and `news.ai_update` payloads may remain immutable observations but cannot create or change a NewsItem or Story. `strategy.triggered` is ignored. `aiRating`, provider scores, summaries, and annotations never affect identity, classification, corroboration, importance, or Brief selection.

Every 60 seconds, the sole Story writer reads the complete enabled 96-hour NewsItem set, runs the exact pinned WorldMonitor `clusterTexts` algorithm once, derives current Stories and memberships, and transactionally publishes only changed rows. Story ID is the SHA-256 hash of the normalized title of the earliest-published member, with deterministic ties. When that member leaves the 96-hour window, the Story ID is allowed to change exactly as it does in a new WorldMonitor batch. Story is a current read model, not a historical identity or audit ledger.

Delete the persisted identity features, similarity edges, Story aliases, Story input state, and per-item/hourly News projection frontiers. Retain existing bounded public caches only where they are already required by the read contract, and refresh them in the same complete Story publication. Immutable observations, idempotent NewsItems, fetch receipts, and immutable Brief publications remain the audit boundary.

## Implementation Decisions

### Authority and hard-cut boundary

- WorldMonitor commit `f73de5b7dde76ff292f800d7d06f3529d2178d43` remains the only algorithm baseline for title normalization, vectorization, candidate generation, containment rescue, threshold `0.615`, exact-title union, hot-bucket limit, deterministic union-find, deterministic keyword classification, and importance factors.
- This Issue replaces #19's seven-day Story alias continuity, prior-membership identity inheritance, archived Story guarantee, and physical-acquisition-source corroboration.
- This Issue replaces the News-specific persisted feature/edge/frontier projection described by #33. It does not alter #33's one Workers process, one PostgreSQL pool, resource capabilities, structured concurrency, fatal handling, graceful shutdown, or other domains.
- Deliver one atomic hard cut. No feature flag, dual read, dual write, alias route, compatibility Story ID, shadow projection, or rollback schema remains runnable after migration.
- Any implementation need for a new News table, second Story writer, second projection path, broker, generic scheduler, new ontology, or compatibility layer must stop and amend this Issue first.

### Minimal acquisition Module

- Extend the existing News acquisition Module; do not introduce a second public News capability or a generic acquisition framework.
- Keep RSS/RSSHub scheduled fetches and their existing bounded receipts.
- Add one code-owned OpenNews acquisition source. Acquisition identity is OpenNews; reporting origin is carried separately on each NewsItem.
- The Module owns a persistent WebSocket receiver, a bounded in-memory handoff, bounded REST recovery, RSS turns, normalization, and the sole NewsItem publication path.
- The WebSocket stream owns its connect, subscribe, receive, idle, reconnect, buffer, and close bounds. It consumes no finite-operation permit for its connection lifetime.
- REST recovery uses the existing finite external-operation capability. Run it immediately on startup, reconnect, and buffer overflow, and normally every five minutes with a 30-minute overlap, `limit=100`, and one page per turn. Stop at the 96-hour/10,000-row boundary.
- OpenNews provides no resume cursor or sequence. Delivery semantics are explicitly at-least-once with REST overlap recovery; never claim exactly-once transport or complete gap closure without evidence.
- Keep endpoints, subscription shape, recovery cadence, page size, buffer capacity, timeouts, and filters code-owned. The operator supplies only the existing News enablement and one OpenNews token secret.
- A missing or rejected token makes the OpenNews source unavailable/degraded while RSS continues. Never log, persist, return, or copy the token.
- Use existing News source/status surfaces for the latest connection and recovery outcome. Do not persist connection sessions, heartbeats, pages, or gap entities merely for history.

### Event admission and normalization

- `news.update` with `engineType=news` and a report payload is the only OpenNews event that may materialize a NewsItem.
- Translation payloads and `news.ai_update` may be stored as observations with an explicit observation kind, but they cannot create, win, or mutate a NewsItem and cannot enter Story clustering.
- Ignore `strategy.triggered` and unsupported engine types without creating a business row.
- Ignore `aiRating`, provider score, provider summary, and provider annotation for Story identity, classification, importance, source count, and Brief selection.
- Normalize OpenNews timestamps from supported ISO strings or millisecond values and reject invalid/future material through the existing admission boundary.
- Preserve raw provider payloads in observations after secret removal. The normalized payload fingerprint must exclude transport wrappers, receive time, connection ID, REST page, and fetch/session identity.

### Observation identity and deduplication

- Reuse `news_feed_observations`; the historical name does not justify a replacement table.
- Observation identity is the deterministic hash of acquisition provider key, provider record key, observation kind, and normalized payload hash.
- An identical report seen through WebSocket, REST recovery, reconnect, or a later poll performs zero observation writes.
- `fetch_id` becomes optional: RSS and REST observations may point to their bounded fetch receipt; WebSocket observations do not fabricate one.
- Remove the existing uniqueness rule based on `(fetch_id, source_item_key)` and replace it with semantic observation identity.
- Do not add `seen_count`, `last_seen`, receipt joins, or a sighting-history table. Fetch/recovery receipts already describe attempts; observations describe distinct content.

### NewsItem identity and current representation

- Reuse `news_items`; do not add Article, Revision, Dispatch, ProviderRecord, or Event entities.
- RSS identity remains source-scoped GUID/canonical-URL identity.
- OpenNews uses one hybrid source item key:
  - `url:<canonical_article_url>` when the URL identifies a specific article;
  - `dispatch:opennews:<provider_record_id>` when the link is missing, fragment-only, or only a publisher homepage.
- Tracking parameters are removed before URL identity. A homepage is retained as raw evidence but is not treated as an article URL.
- `canonical_url` and public representative URL become optional. Never invent a URL for linkless wires.
- Materialize one deterministic current report per NewsItem. Use one whole winning observation selected by a small deterministic precedence over reporting-origin quality, provider event time, content completeness, and observation identity; do not merge fields from several observations and do not add revision history.
- Reporting origin precedence is explicit provider origin, then article URL host, then one normalized acquisition/wrapper fallback. Persist the resulting normalized string on the NewsItem.
- A repeated observation or a losing observation writes zero NewsItem rows. A different winning report updates the existing NewsItem and its content fingerprint through the same sole writer.

### Exact current Story projection

- Replace incremental affected-component projection with one complete 96-hour calculation every 60 seconds. Run immediately after Worker startup; missed ticks coalesce.
- Load every enabled NewsItem whose publication time is within the 96-hour window in deterministic `(published_at, item_id)` order.
- Calculate outside a database transaction using the exact pinned WorldMonitor `clusterTexts` implementation. Do not add embeddings, entities, typed episodes, predicates, quantities, stages, conflict rules, pair exceptions, or admission constraints.
- Before publication, re-read the exact input fingerprint. If it changed, discard the stale result and retry on the next eligible turn; do not partially publish.
- Story ID is `SHA-256(normalizeTitle(earliest-published member title))`. Ties use normalized title and then stable NewsItem identity. Do not adopt a prior ID through membership overlap or alias lookup.
- When the canonical member exits the 96-hour window, the Story ID may change. Old Story IDs are not redirected and are not a compatibility contract.
- `source_count` is the count of distinct normalized `reporting_origin` values among members, with acquisition/wrapper fallback collapsing to one value. It is not the count of acquisition sources, source memberships, observations, translations, or provider events.
- Keep the pinned deterministic classification and importance calculation. Recompute it as part of the same batch; OpenNews AI fields never participate.
- Select representative and scoring items with the existing deterministic WorldMonitor-compatible ordering. Persist their separate identities and transparent factors.
- Publish the entire current closure: current Stories, memberships, Story/source facets, projection summary, and current Brief selection. Validate exactly one owner per active NewsItem and aggregate equality before commit.
- Story and membership tables are current read models. Remove rows absent from the new closure rather than archiving them or preserving inactive membership history.
- Compare state fingerprints and write only changed rows. Unchanged input may consume bounded read/CPU work but must write zero serving rows.
- Use one code-owned 25-second operation budget for the full Story turn. If the existing 10,000-row/4 MiB input envelope or operation budget is exceeded, preserve the last coherent projection and report Story degradation; never publish a partial window or invent partition semantics.

### Schema hard cut

- Start from the current `20260731_0233` schema and retain the existing material facts, bounded public caches, and Brief publication tables.
- Delete these five tables and every production read/write path to them:
  - `news_identity_features`;
  - `news_similarity_edges`;
  - `news_story_aliases`;
  - `news_story_input_state`;
  - `news_projection_frontiers`.
- The final News storage boundary is exactly these fourteen existing tables:
  - `news_sources`;
  - `news_source_memberships`;
  - `news_source_fetches`;
  - `news_feed_observations`;
  - `news_items`;
  - `news_stories`;
  - `news_story_members`;
  - `news_projection_summary`;
  - `news_story_facet_counts`;
  - `news_source_facet_counts`;
  - `news_brief_selection_current`;
  - `news_brief_runs`;
  - `news_brief_publications`;
  - `news_brief_current`.
- The facet, projection-summary, and Brief-selection tables are bounded serving caches refreshed by the one Story publication; they are not identity, audit, scheduling, or history entities.
- Make observation fetch linkage optional, add only the minimal observation-kind constraint needed to distinguish report/translation/provider annotation, and enforce semantic observation identity.
- Make NewsItem and Story representative URLs optional.
- Remove Story alias/archive semantics and obsolete membership-history fields when no retained consumer requires them.
- Consolidate existing repeated observations by the new semantic identity during migration. Preserve one deterministic immutable row per distinct normalized payload; fetch receipts retain polling-attempt history.
- Rebuild the complete current Story closure from NewsItems after migration. Do not migrate old alias ownership or preserve old Story IDs.
- Preserve immutable Brief publications and their embedded source evidence. Recompute current Brief selection from the new Stories; never rewrite a sealed publication.
- Regenerate current schema, grants, audit queries, OpenAPI, frontend types, CLI help, and generated documentation. A second generation must be clean.

### Worker and failure behavior

- Keep one Workers process and one NewsItem writer responsibility under `run_workers`.
- Replace the current News frontier candidate with the one 60-second fixed-period Story turn; do not add a new OS process, worker registry entry, generic scheduler, database wake plane, or durable queue.
- Keep all provider I/O outside database transactions. WebSocket frames enter a bounded in-memory handoff; each database publication is short and idempotent.
- Expected WebSocket/REST transport, rate-limit, authentication, and payload failures become bounded OpenNews source health outcomes. Unclassified code, SQL, invariant, or resource-overrun failures retain #33's fatal process semantics.
- Cancellation before publication leaves no partial Story closure. Graceful shutdown closes intake, drains only the already bounded buffer, finishes submitted writes within the shared deadline, and closes the client.
- Restart re-reads PostgreSQL facts, starts the WebSocket, performs immediate REST overlap recovery, and immediately rebuilds the current Story window. No frontier, alias, connection session, or in-memory cursor is a correctness authority.

### Public contracts and product behavior

- Retain exactly the five existing read-only routes: Feed, Story Detail, Brief, Sources, and Status.
- Upstream OpenNews WebSocket intake does not add an outbound News WebSocket, webhook, push API, item route, observation route, or provider-debug route.
- Feed ordering, filters, cursor behavior, ETag behavior, Story factor transparency, and Brief states remain stable except where this Issue explicitly changes Story identity, current-only detail, source-count meaning, and optional URL.
- Story Detail returns current Story evidence only. An expired or replaced Story ID may return not found; there is no redirect or archived Story compatibility promise.
- Sources and Status expose OpenNews availability, latest live connection outcome, latest recovery outcome, and whether recovery is currently known to be incomplete, without exposing secrets or session history.
- The browser must render linkless members and representatives without a broken link and must label reporting origin rather than treating OpenNews as independent corroboration.
- Brief behavior, provider chain, schema validation, immutable publication, last-known-good fallback, and model budget are unchanged. Only deterministic candidate Stories and fingerprints may change.

## Testing Decisions

### Primary seam

- Use one highest maintained seam: deterministic fake OpenNews WebSocket/REST input through the production `tracefold.news` Module and real PostgreSQL, followed by reads through the public News Interface/HTTP contract.
- The fake replaces only the external provider. Do not mock repositories, Story calculation, transactions, or public reads.
- Existing RSS inputs run through the same test seam to prove additive coexistence and single-writer behavior.
- Treat a real authenticated OpenNews connection as release evidence, not as a second architecture seam.

### External-behavior coverage

- A WebSocket `news.update` report produces one observation and one NewsItem; the next Story turn exposes it through Feed and Story Detail within the 60-second contract.
- The same normalized payload repeated on WebSocket, after reconnect, and through REST recovery produces zero additional observations, NewsItems, Stories, or memberships.
- A changed winning report updates one existing NewsItem and deterministically changes or preserves its Story on the next turn.
- Article URL identity merges the same OpenNews article across provider wrappers; linkless/homepage-only dispatches use provider-record identity and preserve an optional URL.
- Translation and `news.ai_update` produce observation-only behavior; `strategy.triggered`, unsupported engine types, and `aiRating` have zero product effect.
- Reporting-origin normalization causes RSS and OpenNews copies from the same outlet to count once; two independent reporting origins count twice.
- Startup, expected disconnect, reconnect, normal overlap, and buffer overflow invoke bounded REST recovery and converge to the same NewsItems.
- Missing/invalid token, rate limit, invalid payload, REST failure, and unclosed recovery gap degrade only the OpenNews ingest surface while RSS and last coherent Stories remain available.
- Graceful shutdown and restart prove bounded drain, no detached writer, immediate recovery, and deterministic convergence.
- Exact WorldMonitor golden pairs, CJK behavior, containment rescue, hot buckets, order independence, and known false merge/split cases remain frozen.
- A member-expiry test proves that the earliest-title Story ID changes and no alias preserves or redirects the old ID.
- A historical-chain fixture proves that prior membership and aliases cannot merge two current WorldMonitor components.
- A full projection followed by an unchanged turn writes zero Story, membership, facet, projection-summary, and Brief-selection rows.
- A concurrent input change between calculation and publication discards the stale result and never publishes a mixed closure.
- Real-PostgreSQL invariant tests prove one current owner per active NewsItem, aggregate equality, complete deletion closure, and transaction rollback on any violation.
- Migration tests begin with production-like repeated observations, alias-expanded Stories, active/inactive memberships, Brief state, and every five deleted tables; they end with semantic observations, exact current clusters, fourteen News tables, and preserved immutable Brief publications.
- HTTP contract tests cover current-only Story Detail, optional URL, new reporting-origin source count, unchanged cursor/ETag behavior, Sources/Status OpenNews state, and absence of new routes.
- Architecture tests prove one acquisition Module, one NewsItem publication path, one Story writer, no deleted schema owner, no retired imports, and no new generic entity.

### Release acceptance

- Record source commit, migration, redacted config path/token-present boolean, UTC/Shanghai cutoff, active NewsItem count, Story count, singleton/multi-member distribution, largest clusters, and observation deduplication counts.
- Replay the current production NewsItem corpus in isolated PostgreSQL using the exact new batch projection. Review every materially large multi-member cluster and highest-similarity non-merged pairs; compression ratio alone is not success.
- Measure current-corpus Story calculation and complete turn latency. The observed baseline is approximately 9.8 seconds for about 2,961 titles; the released turn must remain within the 25-second bound and 60-second freshness contract.
- Establish a real authenticated OpenNews WebSocket subscription, receive and persist a qualifying report, prove repeat delivery zero writes, exercise REST overlap recovery, and report connection/recovery status without exposing the token.
- Verify the five public News endpoints and the real browser Feed/Story experience, including one linkless dispatch and one cross-transport duplicate.
- Restart Workers with unchanged facts and prove deterministic convergence and zero serving-row writes.
- Run the repository's architecture, contract, focused real-PostgreSQL integration, WorldMonitor parity, generated-artifact, frontend, and Worker runtime gates affected by the change. Green tests alone do not replace the real connection and corpus review.
