## Release dependency rules
- No new team invites before workspace authorization and invitation security tests pass.
- No workspace AI before scoped retrieval and **post-search** canonical permission rechecks.
- No agent writes until immutable approval artifacts, execution-time policy enforcement, idempotency and audit pass.
- No legacy cleanup until backfill is reconciled and rollback + recovery are rehearsed.
- Do not conflate old copy-sharing with new canonical team note collaboration.
