### What happened?

I have 9697 tracks detected by MA for loudness analysis. When the background task was run for the analysis, it didn't complete during the 4 hours allotted to it. So I manually continued to run it until all tracks had been processed and the analysis was at 100%.

However, after coming back and checking MA the next day, HA had seemingly started the process again, showing that analysis is "21%" complete.

This happens repeatedly. Every time I check the stats after a day or two have passed, the process has started again and is stuck somewhere in the middle.

I have my local libraries in a mounted NFS folder, and I know they're being updated with the new ReplayGain data, because their file modification dates are different each time they're processed.

My understanding is that once loudness analysis is completed on a file, MA will see that the file already has the loudness metadata and it won't be checked again. So why is loudness analysis happening repeatedly?
