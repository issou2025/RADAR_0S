**What's happening**

The automated build-and-check pipeline for the Burton NDT showcase site has failed 3 out of its last 10 runs — roughly a 30% failure rate. There are no open pull requests and no Dependabot updates waiting, so this isn't a stuck dependency queue; it's the pipeline itself being unreliable.

**Why it matters**

Nothing here says the live site is broken. This is a workshop-hygiene problem rather than a client-facing outage. The risk is subtler: when a pipeline is red often enough, people stop reading it, and the one failure that actually matters gets waved through.

**Likely causes** (in rough order of probability)

- A flaky or timing-sensitive test that passes and fails at random.
- A build step relying on a missing or expired secret/environment variable.
- An external service or dependency fetch that intermittently times out.
- A genuinely broken check that has been ignored because the site still deploys fine.

**Confidence:** Moderate on the diagnosis being non-urgent (no open PRs, no deploy failure reported alongside it). Low on the specific root cause — we'd need to read the failed run logs to know which.
