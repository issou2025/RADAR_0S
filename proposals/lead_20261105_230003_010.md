You need assistance with: 
**UX: PROVY Brand-Welcome / animierter Einstieg für Proviant, Bordkasse und Abrechnung**

*Project description:*
## Owner-reported UX gap — 2026-11-05

There is **no real branded Provy welcome/intro screen** yet. Current `lib/main.dart:100-129` `AppStartupRecoveryLoadingScreen` only shows a spinner and app title while the mandatory safe startup/recovery gate runs; `lib/core/routing/app_router.dart:19-26` enters `StartScreen` (Meine Törns); `lib/features/home/ui/start_screen.dart:104-139` shows a basic welcome only when no active trip exists. Once a user has a trip, that brand introduction disappears.

**Owner request:** attractive, ideally subtly animated welcome/brand introduction describing the three main capabilities: provision planning, shared onboard/crew expenses (Bordkasse), and settlement/expense splitting (Abrechnung). **PROVY is currently the working name, not yet permanently approved.** Reuse central `AppConfig.appName`; do not hardcode irreversible brand strings everywhere.

### Copy exploration (NOT final brand approval)
- Wordmark: `PROVY`
- Headline: `Provision smarter. Sail lighter.`
- Supporting: `Plan provisions. Share crew costs. Settle with ease.`
- Also consider clearer German/English/localized wording; all supported 6 locales.
- UI ideas: gentle wordmark fade/slide-in and short provisioning → crew cost → settle sequence, 1–2 seconds at most if used as startup splash. Respect reduced motion setting. If this is onboarding rather than splash, provide clear `Continue` and `Skip`.

### Engineering and acceptance constraints
- Decide explicitly between **native splash**, **branded bootstrap splash**, and **one-time welcome/onboarding**; may be combined but not confused. Do not show blocking intro on every routine navigation or delay startup solely to animate.
- Preserve `appStartupRecoveryProvider` gate: **no router or data mutation before mandatory startup recovery is complete**, retain non-destructive error/retry UI. Absolutely no async/race bypass, login prompts, new auth or backend changes.
- Existing trips and account state must open normally; no forced onboarding on every restart; accessibility/readable text in Light/Dark/Nightwatch; Test Pixel 7 Pro and narrow screen; no third-party animation dependency unless justified.
- Keep this separate from flight-strip PR #60 and device flicker #61. No code initiated until owner selects the UX scope/wording, no automatic merge.
