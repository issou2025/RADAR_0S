# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**axiolid-model: ruled surface relation between two curves**

*Project description:*
**Need**

Revit stores ruled faces (`RuledSurf`) as two profile curves, each run linearly over its own parameter range: `p(u, v) = (1 − v)·c0(t0(u)) + v·c1(t1(u))`, u and v in [0, 1]. The profile curves are lines, arcs, ellipses or Hermite splines. openbimrs/revit (openbimrs/revit#108) emits face surfaces as exact `axiolid-surface` values and needs a way to carry such a surface exactly.

`SurfaceRelation` has `LinearExtrusion` and `Revolution` over a swept curve, but nothing that rules between two curves. A `BSplineSurface` covers the ruled surface only when both sides fit one spline form with the same parameterization: lines, points, equal-sweep conics, or Hermite splines over the same knots. It does not cover an arc against a line, two arcs of different sweeps, or two splines over different knots. In those cases the rational parameterization pairs the wrong points, and making the knots compatible means knot insertion, which is kernel work. On the Snowdon Towers Architectural 2026 sample, 96 of the 412 meshed ruled faces stay mesh-only for this reason; the other 316 go out as B-splines. The sample's ruled surfaces pair arcs with lines, arcs with arcs, ellipses with ellipses, Hermite splines with Hermite splines, and lines with lines.

**Proposal**

A ruled-surface relation in `axiolid-model` over two curve nodes, each with the parameter interval it runs over, and points allowed for an apex:

```rust
SurfaceRelation::Ruled {
    first: NodeId,          // Curve3 node, or a Point3 node for an apex
    first_interval: Interval,
    second: NodeId,
    second_interval: Interval,
}
```

Rulings pair `first(lerp(first_interval, u))` with `second(lerp(second_interval, u))`. Evaluation, bounds and tessellation would follow `LinearExtrusion`/`Revolution`.
