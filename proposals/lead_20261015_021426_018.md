2. **Enterprise-Grade Configuration & Security**: System-level configs (`/etc/pi/models.json`), MDM support, and secure provider delegation are recurring themes (#10895, #7547).

### **7. Developer Pain Points**

Recurring frustrations include:
- **Windows Usability Gaps**: Confusion over install/run methods and lack of unified documentation (#7547).
- **Session State Corruption**: Context overflow leading to permanent wedges and failed recovery (#9409, #10840).
- **Tool Call Parsing Inconsistencies**: Silent dropping of empty quoted args or malformed JSON, leading to unexpected behavior (#10867, #10944).
- **TUI Integration Friction**: OSC 52 lost in tmux, cursor jumps during selection (#10628, #10862).
- **Configuration Management Complexity**: Lack of hierarchical precedence and system-level override capability (#10895, #10958).

These highlight ongoing challenges in cross-platform reliability, robustness under edge cases, and developer experience for complex agent workflows.
