## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Seek bar sometimes seeks to 0:00 when clicked right after a track starts (Firefox)**

*Project description:*
### Music Assistant version

dev (server built from `dev` at 2026-10-26, frontend as bundled with it)

### Browser and operating system

Firefox on macOS

### What happened?

Clicking near the end of the seek bar sometimes seeks to 0:00 instead of the clicked position, so the track starts over. It only happens within the first few seconds after a track or a new stream starts, and not every time; a click on the same spot a few seconds later works.

The server receives the 0 from the frontend. A temporary log line in `players.cmd_seek` showed, for the same player:

| Time | Track (duration) | `position` received | Queue elapsed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10:16:14 | Crazy Mama (147 s) | 145 | 3 s |
| 10:16:20 | Home by the Sea (307 s) | 306 | 3 s |
| **10:16:31** | **Crazy Mama (147 s)** | **0** | 2 s |
| 10:16:34 | Crazy Mama (147 s) | 145 | 2 s |
| 10:16:41 | Home by the Sea (307 s) | 305 | 3 s |

The bar showed the right duration when looking at it (5:07 for Home by the Sea). It happened 4 times in about 20 clicks in Firefox. In 13 tries in a Chromium-based browser, right after a stream restart and right after a natural track change, every click sent the right position.

### How to reproduce

1. Play a queue in flow mode (seen on a Teufel Raumfeld player).
2. Within the first ~5 s after a track starts (after a track change or a seek), click near the end of the seek bar in Firefox.
3. Repeat a few times: sometimes the player seeks to 0:00.

### Screenshot or recording

Not available: it happens intermittently and only in Firefox; the server log above shows the position the frontend sent.

### Anything else?

Two guesses from reading `src/layouts/default/PlayerOSD/PlayerTimeline.vue`, not verified:

- `stopDragging()` (on `@value-commit`) sends `tempTime`, which is only set by the `v-model` setter and starts at `0`. If reka emits `value-commit` without an `update:modelValue` first (which could differ per browser in how pointer events arrive, e.g. on a freshly mounted timeline), the stale `tempTime` is sent. Since it only showed in Firefox, this looks the more likely one.
- `timelineDuration` falls back to `0` when `current_media.duration` is briefly missing while a new track or stream starts; `SliderRoot` then has `:max="0"`, so any click lands on 0.
