## Approved design direction
- Private company-scoped Report Content / Report User; accessible to authorized readers, including non-admin roles; no directory expansion or extra business-data access.
- Typed localized reasons: inappropriate content, harassment/abuse, privacy violation, illegal content, other; optional details. Server derives reporter, verifies target/tenant/authorization. Minimize evidence; preserve version/reference and lawful retention where needed.
- Independent Company Owner/Admin review; no self-review or conflicts. Direct platform routing for owner/moderator conflicts, unsafe company route, serious cases, or no independent reviewer; escalation for inadequate handling. Platform retains oversight. No automatic business deletion or suspension on submission.
- Separate lifecycle (Submitted/Under Review/Closed), level (Company/Platform), outcome (Pending/Violation Confirmed/No Violation/Insufficient Evidence), actions and immutable events; validate transitions and concurrency.
- My Reports exposes safe status/outcome only; separate company Reports & Moderation. Protect reporter identity from subject, not absolute anonymity.
- Platform review via scoped MFA-protected operator commands reusing #415/#358 foundations, no full #359 console dependency for this feature; preserve #359 standalone Flutter direction and any independent launch requirements. No blanket tenant access, service-role distribution or impersonation. Proposed review-start targets normal 48h, escalated 24h, serious 24h or sooner; document accountable staffing and actual feasibility before release.
- Versioned account-level terms acceptance, server timestamp, authoritative enforcement on relevant writes, existing/invited accounts and changed terms; preserve read access/account deletion. No local-only consent.
- EN/AR legal pages and verified data disclosures, no unsupported guarantees. Report data, diagnostics, IP-derived location, sharing/exemptions and SDK data inventory before final Data Safety.

## Architecture / security
SaaS multi-tenant, responsive/adaptive, Clean Architecture (Presentation → Domain ← Data), SOLID. DB migrations, RLS/grants, target-specific authorization, server-owned actor/audit, atomic transitions, anti-abuse, minimal evidence, retention/deletion, signed URL and cleanup compatibility. No broad audit leakage or raw backend errors. Preserve operational/financial records and existing membership authorization.
