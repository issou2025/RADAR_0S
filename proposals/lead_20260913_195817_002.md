## What this changes

Six distinct instructions, each with a consequence:

1. **The owner is not the technical reviewer.** "i'm not very technical … build out your engineering org and rigor on your own." Sessions own engineering rigor end to end — the standard, the tests, the review, the merge gate. Never hand the owner a technical judgement call dressed as a question. Their input is use cases, business plan, priorities.
2. **Donor-free is the product, not a goal.** "a bullet proof product that doesn't rely on donor files." Reaffirms and hardens S-2026-08-10-c.
3. **The deliverable is stated plainly: prompt → `.rfa`, and sometimes IFC → `.rfa`.** "fully generate an RFA file from a prompt or from an ifc input sometimes." That is the product; `.rvt` work serves it.
4. **Novelty is a requirement, not a preference.** "something that is novel and never done before … novel solutions that don't depend on existing software/paradigms/systems/files." No Revit install, no Autodesk seat, no APS, no donor `.rfa` — and, read strictly, no leaning on an existing file as the source of a law we could derive ourselves.
5. **Run continuously, self-testing.** "put yourself in a /loop and iterate forever, testing your own shit, until you have a perfect platform."
6. **Self-dispatch.** "do your own testing, merging, etc. and deplyoy/dispatch through ultracode and subagents."

## Tensions a future session must know about

- **(5) vs S-2026-08-10-b (#422)** — "nothing in normal operation may generate an approval or permission prompt", which explicitly said loop survival beyond the persistent session is *an owner-created routine, never something a session sets up*. The owner now asks the session to put itself in a loop. Read: the session may drive its own in-session loop (no Routine creation, no click-requiring spawn), and #422's no-prompt rule still binds. If a loop mechanism would prompt, it is not used.
- **(4) vs hard rule 3 and the mining practice.** We *mine laws* from Autodesk-born files (samples, templates) and author our own equivalents — that is how #516's category ids and the 0x0f3f footer law were found. "Novel, doesn't depend on existing files" cannot mean "stop reading specimens to learn the format", or the format becomes unknowable. Read as: **no donor bytes and no donor file in any shipped path or any product flow** — mining stays a development instrument, quarantined and git-ignored, exactly as rule 3 already says.
- **(1) vs hard rule 4.** Autodesk's reader remains the arbiter. The owner not being technical does not make a desktop-Revit verdict optional; it means the session must find ways to get evidence that do not route through the owner as a test instrument (steer #765 already said this).
