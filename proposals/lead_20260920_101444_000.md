# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding

**Signal modules: a company-keyed job corpus, technographics and buying intent (gap analysis vs TheirStack)**

*Project description:*
## Why

We already run three monitoring subsystems (price, mention, job) and a B2B research toolkit. Reviewing
[TheirStack](https://theirstack.com/en) against them shows the overlap is smaller than it looks: they are
not a fourth monitor, they are a **corpus**, and every signal product they sell is a `WHERE` clause over it.

TheirStack sells three datasets and one delivery mechanism:

| Dataset | Scale | Derived from |
|---|---|---|
| Job postings | 243M, 195 countries | crawled boards |
| Technographics | 33k technologies | **the text of the job postings** |
| Buying intent | use-case keyword sets | **the text of the job postings** |

Delivery = a saved filter that fires when something new matches, pushed to CRM / Slack / webhook, with
recruiting and consulting firms excluded automatically.

So "Signals" is not a fourth dataset. It is *a standing corpus + a saved filter + a webhook*. We have the
filter half and the webhook half three times over. We have no corpus, and we throw away the text the two
derived datasets are made of.

## Gap 1 — everything we collect is subject-scoped, so a market-wide signal is structurally impossible

`tracked_jobs` to `job_listings`, `tracked_mentions` to `mention_history`, `tracked_queries` to
`tracked_query_price_history`. The same shape three times: **nothing is collected unless someone already
asked for it.**

A signal that says *"this company just started hiring for X"* cannot exist in that shape. If nobody had
already tracked the company, we never fetched it. And `tracked_jobs` is candidate-side anyway —
`salary_min`, `seniority`, `excluded_companies`, `digest_hour_utc`, `digest_day_of_week` — it answers
"find me a job", not "who is hiring".

This is **not** a call to reopen #283. That issue closed COMPLETED with the correct finding: the three are
typed domain schemas sharing a *pattern*, and over-consolidating them into `tracked_targets` +
`observations(jsonb)` would be wrong. The corpus is a **fourth typed subsystem**, which is consistent with
that decision rather than a regression against it.

## Gap 2 — we discard the text both derived datasets are made of

`description_md` is NULL on all 159 rows. A 600-char excerpt is the title plus a paragraph of boilerplate;
the stack and the intent phrases live in the requirements block, roughly 2,000 characters down.

Cheapest item in this issue: the column already exists and is always NULL.

## Gap 3 — ATS enumeration: we pay to guess at a source that is keyless and complete

The boards already `promoted` in `job_board_index` expose public JSON. Probed live 2026-09-20:

```
greenhouse  boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{token}/jobs?content=true   200  5.1 MB (stripe)   full `content` HTML
lever       api.lever.co/v0/postings/{token}?mode=json                     200  684 KB (spotify)  full descriptions
ashby       api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{token}                  200  2.5 MB (ramp)     full jobs array
workable    apply.workable.com/api/v1/widget/accounts/{token}              endpoint valid, needs a real token
```

We currently reach those same companies through Google Jobs SERP + Perplexity + Firecrawl — metered,
per-query, and lossy enough that zero listings have ever scored `relevance='exact'`.

The 404s during probing were wrong **tokens**, not wrong endpoints, and that is the real gap: there is no
`company_domain` to `ats_token` map anywhere. Resolution is the work; fetching is free.

## Gap 4 — two dictionaries, both of which belong in the database

Per CLAUDE.md ("Prompts and extraction fields come from the DATABASE"), neither can be a constant in code.

- **Technologies.** We do not need 33k. For materials / construction / interiors plus the ERP buyer the
  useful list is 300-800: Softone, Entersoft, Epsilon, Pylon, SAP, Shopify / WooCommerce / Magento,
  AutoCAD / Revit / SketchUp / ArchiCAD / 3ds Max, the myDATA providers, PIM/DAM, accounting suites.
- **Intent phrases.** `(intent_key, phrases[], sector)` — "migrating to a new ERP", "own our myDATA
  compliance", "implementing SAP", "rolling out a new PIM".

The classifier already exists and is already verdict-cached (`job_classifier_verdict_cache`). We point it
at a different question; we do not build a second one.

## Gap 5 — a second technographics source we already own and do not read

TheirStack infers the stack from job ads because job ads are all they have. `crawl-user-website` already
fetches page HTML on a demand-driven queue. Script `src`, `meta generator`, cookie names and CNAME targets
are a Wappalyzer-grade fingerprint at **zero marginal fetch cost**, and it works on any domain we crawl —
including the 48 CRM companies that have a website today.

That is a detection surface a job-ads-only vendor structurally cannot match.

## These ship as MODULES, under `research`

Not under Finance. The natural family is `category = 'research'`, which already holds `job-research`,
`job-research-notifications` and `page-monitoring` — same lineage, same billing shape. Following the
established core-plus-satellites pattern (`commerce-core` + `commerce-shopify` / `commerce-skroutz` /
`commerce-woocommerce`, and every monitor having a separate `-notifications` module):

| Slug | Category | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `signals-core` | research | the corpus, `company_profile`, ATS enumeration, the dictionaries. The shared engine the others read |
| `signals-hiring` | research | who started hiring for what |
| `signals-technographics` | research | who uses / just adopted a technology |
| `signals-intent` | research | who is in market for a use case |
| `signals-notifications` | research | delivery, mirroring `price-monitoring-notifications` and friends |

`signals-core` is a dependency of the other three — express that with `modules.grants_slugs` on a bundle
row rather than by having each satellite re-check a second slug.

**The slug must name a real `public.modules` row.** A `moduleSlug` that names nothing is worse than no
gate at all: `finance` (the actual slug is `sales-finance`) made `enabledModules.includes()` false in every
workspace and hid the entire Expenses toolkit from everyone, operator included, permanently. Every toolkit
below carries one of the five slugs above, verbatim.

Each toolkit also needs a real `moduleGate(workspaceId, slug)` call inside the tool — `EntitlementGuard`
on a page is UX, and the tool reaches the same tables without passing it. Checking `modules.enabled`
instead is the trap that reads like a gate and refuses nobody: that flag is platform-wide and true for
everyone. The gate is `is_workspace_entitled`, and it fails closed.

## Schema sketch

**Tenancy split matters here and is easy to get wrong.** The corpus is operator-owned and global, the way
`job_board_index`, `mention_outlets` and `greek_registry_companies` already are. The *signals* are
workspace-scoped. Same split as global flows versus `workspace_flow_preferences`.

```
-- corpus (global, service-role writes, authenticated read) --------------------
company_ats_accounts
  company_domain text, provider text check (provider in
    ('greenhouse','lever','ashby','workable','recruitee','smartrecruiters','personio')),
  token text, status text,            -- active | dead | unresolved
  last_enumerated_at timestamptz, last_error text,
  primary key (company_domain, provider)

job_postings                          -- NO tracked_* FK. That is the whole point.
  id uuid, company_domain text, company_name text,
  canonical_url text, title text,
  description_md text not null,       -- the whole body, never truncated
  location text, country_code text, is_remote bool,
  seniority text, employment_type text,
  salary_min/max/currency/period,
  posted_at, first_seen_at, last_seen_at, closed_at,
  source text,                        -- greenhouse | lever | ashby | google_jobs | rss
  cache_status text,                  -- pipeline convention 2
  unique (company_domain, canonical_url)

company_technologies
  company_domain, technology_id, evidence text,   -- job_posting | website_html | dns
  evidence_url text, first_seen_at, last_seen_at, confidence numeric,
  primary key (company_domain, technology_id, evidence)

technology_dictionary                 -- the registry, not a code constant
  id, name, slug, category, aliases text[], sector text[],
  detect_phrases text[], detect_html jsonb    -- {script_src[], meta_generator[], cookies[]}

intent_definitions
  intent_key, label, phrases text[], sector text[], module_slug

company_intent_signals
  company_domain, intent_key, evidence_url, observed_at, confidence, posting_id

company_profile                       -- the market spine, distinct from crm_companies
  domain pk, name, country_code,
  is_recruiter bool, is_consultancy bool,     -- corpus-level, see traps
  employee_band text, industry text, registry_ref text

-- signals (workspace-scoped) --------------------------------------------------
signals
  id, workspace_id not null, created_by,
  kind text,                          -- hiring | technology | intent
  filter jsonb,                       -- the saved WHERE
  is_active bool, last_evaluated_at, cadence_hours

signal_matches
  signal_id, company_domain, matched_at, payload jsonb,
  delivered_at, dismissed_at
```

## Build order

0. **Seed the five `public.modules` rows** under `category='research'` first, so nothing downstream gates
   on a slug that does not exist yet.
1. **Stop truncating.** Populate `description_md` on the existing ingest paths. One column, already there.
2. **`company_ats_accounts` + the four enumerators.** Free corpus for every company on those boards.
3. **HTML fingerprinting** off `crawl-user-website` into `company_technologies`.
4. **The two dictionaries** as tables, seeded for our sector.
5. **`signals` + `signal_matches`**, evaluated on a cron, emitting `signal.matched` as a flow event.
6. **Three toolkit clusters** — `signals-hiring`, `signals-technographics`, `signals-intent` — each with
   its `moduleSlug` set and a `moduleGate` call inside every tool.

Items 1-3 are worth shipping on their own even if 5-6 slip: they make `crm-lead-score` an externally
informed score instead of a mirror of our own activity, and they give `company-enrich` something to read
that is not Apollo.

## Traps

- **Delivery is Flows, not a notification insert.** Emit `signal.matched` and seed a locked
  `system-default` flow. Hardcoding `user_notifications` here would be the fourth copy of alert dispatch
  after `job_alert_log` / `mention_alert_log` / tracked-query alerts, and CLAUDE.md forbids it outright.
  A new trigger also needs a `TriggerType` union entry, the exhaustive icon/label maps, a `paletteItems`
  entry, a `flow_area_registry` row — and a **workspace-stamping emitter**, or the fork path silently
  produces fewer notifications than the default did.
- **Silent zero is the dominant risk in this whole feature.** A board whose JSON shape changed returns
  `[]`, which is byte-identical to "this company is not hiring". Every enumerator needs `cache_status` and
  a `source_errors` column, plus a `dic_detect__ops_silent_zero` probe — `seo_domain_snapshots.source_errors`
  exists for exactly this reason, after all three DataForSEO calls were wrapped in `.catch(() => [])`.
  A signal product made of quiet zeros is worse than no signal product.
- **Recruiter / consultancy exclusion belongs on the corpus, not the subscriber.** TheirStack advertises
  it for a reason: a staffing agency posting "SAP consultant" for a client reads as *the agency* buying
  SAP. We have `excluded_companies` as per-subscriber free text on `tracked_jobs` — wrong layer, and it
  cannot be reused by the next subscriber who hits the same noise.
- **`crm_companies` is a relationship, not a market.** 54 rows, every one added by a human. Signals need a
  company spine that exists *before* the relationship. `greek_registry_companies` (176) is the nearest
  thing we have and it is registry-sourced, not activity-sourced.
- **Corpus tables are global; do not give them a `workspace_id`.** Reads are `authenticated`, writes are
  service-role only. `signals` / `signal_matches` are workspace-scoped with an
  `assert_workspace_member(workspace_id)` policy, written one policy per command — never `FOR ALL`.
- **Any new SECURITY DEFINER reader** gets `SET search_path = ''` and
  `REVOKE EXECUTE FROM anon, authenticated, PUBLIC` in the same migration, then a GRANT only to the roles
  that need it.
- **Politeness on the ATS endpoints.** They are public board APIs meant for embedding, but a single Stripe
  enumeration is 5.1 MB. Conditional requests, a per-provider rate limit, and `last_enumerated_at` as the
  due-gate — not a fixed cron over every token.
- **New tools need the full registration chain** or they are silently unreachable: `TOOLKITS` cluster
  entry, listed in at least one `AGENT_CONFIGS[agentId].tools`, a card branch (not an
  `AGENT_RESULT_TITLES` entry, which shadows it), `moduleGate` for the `moduleSlug`, and
  `npm run tools:manifest`.

## Explicitly not doing

- **The 243M postings.** That is a data-acquisition business and it is their moat. A roughly 50k corpus
  scoped to our sector and our countries, plus the HTML fingerprint they structurally cannot do, is worth
  more to our users than theirs is.
- **Wholesale contact data.** `contact_discovery` plus Apollo on demand already covers it.
- **Reopening #283.** The corpus is a fourth typed subsystem by design.
