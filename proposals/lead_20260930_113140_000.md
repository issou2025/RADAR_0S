*Project description:*
### What are you trying to do?

Currently, layouts are confined to the bottom horizontal bar of the window, which works well for small-scale layouts. However, as the number of layouts increases, this approach can become cumbersome.

I propose that a layout pane be created, similar to Rhino's layout panel, which resides on the right-hand side of the window. This pane could be toggled like a tab, allowing access to layer properties, among other features. The idea is to arrange the layouts in a vertical list, as is common in Rhino and most CAD software.

Taking this idea further, one thing that would fix something that has frustrated Rhino users for decades.
I suggest enabling nested layouts, essentially creating a "book" or "Layout Book" that contains multiple layouts, similar to Revit and Archicads set up. This would enable users to organize their layouts hierarchically and group them in whatver order and grouping made snese for their needs, making it easier to manage and print them individually or as a group.

This level of organization is essential for any serious documentation work, where multiple layouts need to be managed and printed simultaneously. And its lacking in rhino has continued to be one of th emany reasons rhino is not taken seriously after certain stages in 3D projects.

When Printing layouts there should be a page to define which layouts are to be printed and any available settings, much like in Rhino.
