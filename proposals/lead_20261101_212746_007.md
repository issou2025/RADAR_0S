- The additional Architecture library is examined for missing topics, insufficiently developed methods, missing alternatives and weak connections between disciplines. Selected content is integrated with page/section/time references and the same applicability checks. Actual review coverage and unresolved source-access gaps remain explicit.
- Each of the five passes produces substantive changes to architecture through multiple bounded iterations. Iteration counts and groupings are adjustable forecasts; pass acceptance depends on coverage, usable changes and evidence.
- The compact root, operation procedures, thematic references, normative catalog and project-owned facts have distinct responsibilities. A task loads the relevant material and critical conditions without reading the entire corpus.
- Representative architectural tasks demonstrate useful behavior, including an existing-layout revision, meaningful option comparison, requirement/source selection and incomplete or conflicting inputs. Evidence includes relevant reading/routing and the resulting design reasoning.
- Accepted modeling, format, source-authority, licensing and explicit-invocation boundaries remain intact. The result is a deepened design skill, with accurately stated coverage and limits.
