**Find how to detect events reliably in a noisy room, such as a busy road outside the window**

*Project description:*
## Question

The first real nights show two problems with the v1 loudness detector (rolling 20th-percentile baseline plus hysteresis):

- In a fairly quiet room it works, but with many false alarms.
- In a loud room with a four-lane road outside the window it marks practically every stretch as an event.

What are the options to make event detection reliable in such rooms, and which fit an on-device Android app?

- **Filtering steady and passing noise** such as road traffic without losing night sounds like snoring, breathing, speech, coughing: band-wise or spectral baselines, noise suppression, spectral novelty and similar signal-processing approaches.
- **Pretrained models** for sound event detection or audio tagging that run on the phone (size, speed, licence, maintenance, which classes they know, how well they handle night sounds).
- **Training our own model**: is it an option, what would it learn from (public datasets such as DreamCatcher, the local test nights, the owner's own labelled events, hidden events as negatives), how much data would it need, can it be personalised per room.
- How each option would be measured against the local test nights and real nights.
