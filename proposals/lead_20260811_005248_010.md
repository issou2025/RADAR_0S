Confirmed remaining problems
1. The merged self-healing fabric has a valid `control_plane_self_healing_recovery` route, but the ordinary Runner codegen path still turns a failed Codex child directly into terminal `runner:blocked`. Known recoverable codegen failures therefore do not automatically enter the existing recovery state machine.
