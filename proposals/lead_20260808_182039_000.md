## Four acceptance scenarios

### BET

BET uploads index C from the existing study card. Identity is explicit; B and C are compared; C becomes `latest_received`; B may remain `current_for_coordination` until review. Impact candidates do not rewrite dependent artifacts.

### Quote

A corrected quote becomes a new revision of one offer. An intentional technical variant becomes a sibling offer artifact. Analysis remains pinned to exact offer/CCTP/DPGF baselines.

### CCTP

Internal working revisions use optimistic concurrency. A later working revision does not silently replace the revision issued for consultation or contract.

### Knowledge

A new source edition creates a new source revision/edition. Hermes may propose Knowledge updates with source anchors. The Knowledge Item is not silently rewritten and historical applicability remains possible.
