# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Scorers: mesh_bodies_vs_ifc reads the tessellation of a round section as a difference of volume; Revit's export draws it as an inscribed polygon**

*Project description:*
## What

`tools/re/mesh_bodies_vs_ifc.py` compares each body drawn from a saved mesh with Revit's export by volume (and by vertices in both directions). On a round section the two disagree by construction: Revit's export draws a round section as a polygon inscribed in the circle with few corners, and the saved mesh draws the circle with more (RE-397 section 5.2).

- The BIM4LCA housing's 30 mm round rails: Revit's end face is 24 vertices, 6 of them the corners of a regular hexagon inscribed in the circle of rvt-rs's 32-gon (areas 0.00629 and 0.00754 ft2); every shell that differs reads 1.198 to 1.200 of Revit's volume, and every other shell of the railing is Revit's to 4 digits. 12 of 43 and 16 of 56 railings of the housing's large files read outside 1%.
- The office's `KAI-3` posts: Revit's cap is a quadrilateral inscribed in the saved cylinder's circle (4 corners at 0.164 ft, the saved radius, and 20 vertices on the chords); the posts read 1.59 of Revit's volume and the two railings 1.195 and 1.198.

The vertices bar (each export's vertices within 0.01 ft of the other's surface) holds on all of them; the volume bar does not, and a row pre-registered on #1636 (RE-397) was missed for that reason alone.

## Done when

The scorer says, per shell, whether Revit's end face is a polygon inscribed in rvt-rs's circle (its corners on the circle within 0.001 ft, and its area the inscribed polygon's) and reads such a shell's volume against that area ratio, counting it apart from the shells that differ for another reason, so that a body that is wrong still reads wrong. Its own row on #1636 is pre-registered before the next measurement, and the RE-397 rows of the housing are re-read with it.
