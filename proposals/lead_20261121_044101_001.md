## Summary

Auditing `Worksets` (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/Worksets/`) during [Platform Kernel, Feature Slice Seams & Legacy Allowlist Policy](https://github.com/amcgoey/Synthetic-Revit/issues/158) surfaced three latent functional defects (to be resolved and verified in the planned `Worksets` Direct-Slice vertical hardening spec):

1. **`WorksetSetFile.Execute` opens a Revit `Transaction` before modal UI dialogs and Excel COM interop, and exits without `RollBack()`** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/Worksets/Commands/WorksetSetFile.cs` lines 69–192):
   - Starts a `Transaction` before `FileOpenDialog.Show()` (line 141), `Excel.WorkSheetNames()` COM calls (lines 106, 158), and `ListByCheckboxView.ShowDialog()` (lines 116, 168); early `return Result.Failed` paths (lines 126, 178) and Cancel (`trans.Dispose()` at line 186) exit without explicit `RollBack()`.
2. **`ElementsOnWorksetReload` (`ElementsOnWorkset.MoveToWorkset`) swallows exceptions and throws `NotImplementedException`** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/Worksets/Models/ElementsOnWorkset.cs` lines 129–160):
   - Chains hidden helper transactions without a `TransactionGroup`, swallows exceptions at line 135, and hits `throw new NotImplementedException()` at line 160.
3. **Misplaced `else if` branch in `WorksetUtil._create` makes existing-workset lookup dead code** (`src/SyntheticShared/Modules/Worksets/Utilities/WorksetUtil.cs` lines 227–263):
   - Line 256 (`else if (wkset.Name != null)`) attaches to the outer `if (wkset.Name != null && wkset.Name != "")` instead of the inner `if (WorksetTable.IsWorksetNameUnique(doc, wkset.Name))` check (line 231), so existing worksets are never retrieved when `IsWorksetNameUnique` is `false` and empty names fall through to `GetByName("", doc)`.
