## Why this tranche is necessary

Three relevant 2026 research findings change the benchmark requirements:

1. Ersöz & Bosché, *Evaluating confidence in geometric matching between 3D point clouds and BIM models by integrating coverage, distance, and distribution metrics*, Automation in Construction 181 (2026), article 106636, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autcon.2025.106636. The method uses coverage, distribution and distance indices, accounts for inter-element occlusion when deriving theoretically visible surfaces, and was evaluated on 46 point-cloud/BIM pairs and 4,000+ IFC elements. Reproduce/adapt the published method with attribution and its upstream implementation where compatible; do not relabel the same indices as a Luminous innovation.
   - Paper: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0926580525006764
   - Author implementation: https://github.com/CyberbuildLab/pcd-bim-matching-confidence (Apache-2.0 repository; verify exact revision and dependency terms before use).

2. Jayasinghe & Brilakis, *Adapting occlusion completion methods for scan-to-BIM automation*, Automation in Construction 189 (September 2026), article 107052, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autcon.2026.107052. Complete-object bounds leaking into partial-input normalization can make completion scores misleading. Candidate-facing inputs must not contain reference-derived full-object bounds or equivalent target information. The paper's reported EMD/CD gains are study-specific and are not a benchmark target to copy without matched methods, data, and splits.
   - Paper/accepted version: https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/98958304-1013-47cc-a6e6-66a121a23e55

3. Patil et al., *Automatic Scan-to-BIM: The Impact of Semantic Segmentation Accuracy on Opening Detection*, ISPRS Annals XI-1-2026, 437–445, DOI: https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-annals-XI-1-2026-437-2026. Door and window detection rates differ materially in the tested S3DIS setting, and segmentation accuracy does not establish geometric opening accuracy. Report detection, dimensions, centroid error, 3D IoU and topology separately.
   - Paper: https://isprs-annals.copernicus.org/articles/XI-1-2026/437/2026/

## Scope and staged execution

### P0 — deterministic replay engine (synthetic, no external data needed)

Implement a standalone Rust package/CLI or a tightly scoped addition to the existing tooling. It must accept a declared manifest and candidate output, produce deterministic JSON/CSV results, and never need evaluator labels to execute the candidate pipeline.

The manifest must bind:
- dataset and scene identifiers, building/capture/trajectory group IDs, source revision and access/usage constraints;
- source-file hashes recorded locally (hashes/metadata only in repository; no restricted bytes);
- coordinate frame, units, tolerance, sampling, distance cutoff, meshing/surface sampling and normalization parameters;
- candidate pipeline code/build digest, command line, model/configuration digests and output digests;
- reference/evaluator artifact identity, held by the evaluator side;
- metric definition and version, excluded/unknown/visible masks, sample counts and denominators.

Add frozen synthetic fixtures for: leaked full-object bounds; partial-only normalization; self-occluded surfaces; sparse/nonuniform sampling; duplicate points; disconnected and non-Manhattan structures; hidden openings; false positives on unobserved regions; correlated capture derivatives; stale references; and deliberately wrong units/frames.

### P1 — faithful ISPRS historical replay

ISPRS Indoor Modelling has six heterogeneous scenes. Preserve the historical wall-only protocol independently of all Luminous extensions:
- completeness = intersecting reference surface area / total reference surface area;
- correctness = intersecting source surface area / total source surface area;
- accuracy = median absolute reference-to-source surface distance under the declared cutoff;
- retain the original buffer/cutoff curves and explicitly report the 10 cm table values.

The official results page is the source of historical tabulated values: https://isprs.org/resources/datasets/benchmarks/IndoorModeling/results.aspx

Reproduction must identify the exact sampling, surface construction, thresholds, exclusions and aggregation implementation. A result that merely uses similarly named metrics is not a faithful replay. Compare reproduced values against published values within their displayed rounding precision; record any unresolved discrepancy rather than adjusting thresholds to fit.

Group TUB1 and TUB2 under the same building-level holdout. They are two acquisitions/sensors of the same physical building, not independent unseen buildings. UoM hidden windows remain unobserved; do not count them as detector misses or successful hallucinated reconstructions.

The official submission portal is closed. Research downloads are subject to research-only use and no redistribution: https://www.isprs.org/resources/datasets/benchmarks/IndoorModeling/Default.aspx. Never commit source scans, reference BIMs, derived restricted geometry, or dataset-bearing CI artifacts.

### P2 — visibility-aware element evaluation and BIMNet

Reproduce the published coverage/distribution/distance confidence indices as a separately identified metric track. Track:
- theoretically visible versus self-occluded versus externally occluded versus not captured/unknown surface;
- element-level visible coverage and point distribution;
- distance distributions and signed/unsigned residuals;
- component correspondence and identity ambiguity;
- opening detection versus opening geometry;
- topology and IFC relationship correctness.

BIMNet is a follow-on, not an assumed freely downloadable corpus. Its access instructions require Matterport3D access approval and a separate request to the BIMNet maintainers. The BIMNet website states that part of its dataset is MIT-licensed while original Matterport3D material remains under its terms of use. Enforce per-file/per-subset rights and do not assume the complete package is redistributable:
https://thucbims.github.io/bimnet/

### P3 — matched operational comparison

Compare, under disclosed inputs and labor:
- reproducible academic baselines and published historical results;
- a documented expert/manual workflow;
- available commercial workflows such as Leica Cyclone 3DR, Trimble RealWorks and Autodesk ReCap Pro/Revit;
- low-cost/mobile acquisition and terrestrial scanning where the same building/checkpoints are available.

Record acquisition time, processing time, expert correction person-minutes, equipment class, scan density, coverage, and rework separately. Build quality-vs-cost/time Pareto curves; do not claim an efficiency advantage from unmatched studies or vendor feature pages.

## Benchmark outputs — keep tracks separate

1. **Historical geometry:** official ISPRS completeness, correctness and accuracy curves.
2. **Element geometry:** visible-surface coverage, distance median/p95/p99, centroid/dimension error, independent-checkpoint error and 3D IoU where suitable.
3. **Semantic:** per-class precision/recall/F1 and opening-level detection.
4. **Topology/identity:** graph-edge precision/recall, opening boundary consistency, persistent correspondence, split/merge/replacement/unknown correctness.
5. **Epistemic quality:** uncertainty calibration/coverage, selective risk versus abstention, unsupported hidden-surface claims, and confidence conditioned on actual visibility.
6. **Admission/lifecycle:** false model mutations per independently accepted change, rejected unsafe mutations, rework and time-to-accepted-change.
7. **Acquisition efficiency:** quality and decision risk as a function of acquisition/processing/review cost.

Do not collapse these into one weighted score. In particular, never trade false mutations or overconfident hidden-surface claims against a small improvement in geometric distance.

## Leakage-resistant evaluation and statistics

- Candidate and evaluator are separate processes/identities. Candidate access is restricted to partial observations, declared prior model state and explicitly permitted metadata.
- Reference geometry, labels, reference-derived boxes, holdout membership and evaluator-only correspondence stay outside candidate access.
- Split by building, scene, capture session and source lineage. All views/derivatives from one capture remain in the same group.
- TUB1/TUB2 share one building group. Do not treat point samples or repeated scans as independent buildings.
- Use building/scene-clustered uncertainty estimates. With only five independent ISPRS building groups after grouping TUB1/TUB2, any generalization interval must disclose the small effective sample size and be described as exploratory; millions of points do not create millions of independent buildings.
- Record macro-by-building and macro-by-scene results alongside pooled descriptive totals. Never hide a difficult scene in a point-weighted mean.
- Include negative controls for reference-bound leakage, hidden windows, label exposure, source-lineage leakage and post-hoc parameter tuning.
- Freeze protocol, candidate artifact digest and split custody before evaluator reveal; reuse #1226 receipts rather than inventing another blind-run chain.
