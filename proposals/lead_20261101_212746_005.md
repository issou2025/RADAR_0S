## Curator interpretation

The repository should increase executable conformance where verification can legitimately be abstracted or automated **without converting professional judgment, contractual authority, project facts, or human governance decisions into machine-created truth**.

A complete conformance chain is not necessarily required for every governed requirement. The appropriate endpoint depends on the requirement's verification class and authority boundary.

## Workstream A — ATS completeness

1. Review S1 and S2 normative requirements for abstract-test coverage.
2. Add ATS where an objective conformance question can be expressed even if final verification remains human.
3. Review the 9 instructions without direct ATS and determine whether each requires direct ATS, is covered indirectly by a governing ATS, or legitimately requires no separate ATS.
4. Preserve traceability from ATS to governing object / requirement where supported.

Priority examples include questions such as whether an authority assignment is explicit and traceable, or whether required project information declarations are present. ATS must not invent the missing assignment or declaration.

## Workstream B — existing ETS implementation

For the seven already-specified ETS:

1. verify each ETS is still current and correctly linked to its ATS/governing object;
2. define or complete callable implementation where automation is appropriate;
3. provide controlled fixtures / expected outcomes where needed;
4. validate positive, negative and unresolved/error cases as applicable;
5. update generated conformance coverage from repository truth rather than hand-editing coverage metrics.

## Workstream C — ATS → ETS suitability review

Review ATS currently lacking ETS and classify their intended verification endpoint rather than treating all 25 as automation debt.

Suggested dispositions:

- `automation_candidate` — objective rule suitable for executable testing;
- `machine_assisted` — automation may collect/test evidence but a governed human decision remains necessary;
- `human_verifiable` — professional, contractual, authority, applicability or contextual judgment should remain human-governed;
- `not_applicable_for_ets` — an ETS would not add legitimate verification value.

Use existing repository verification-class semantics where they already provide an equivalent governed representation; do not create redundant taxonomy merely to satisfy this issue.

## Governance boundaries

- Do not create an ETS merely to maximize coverage percentage.
- Do not treat absence of ATS/ETS as automatic nonconformance.
- Do not allow automation to assign contractual, professional, acceptance or governance authority.
- Do not encode unresolved project facts as enterprise defaults.
- Do not turn machine-detectable evidence into a machine-made professional decision.
- Conformance maturity metrics are visibility mechanisms; they do not create normative authority.

## Completion condition

This issue may close when:

1. S1/S2 and instruction-level ATS coverage have explicit dispositions;
2. the seven currently specified-but-unimplemented ETS have been implemented, superseded, rejected, or otherwise explicitly dispositioned;
3. ATS without ETS have an explicit suitability/verification-endpoint disposition sufficient to distinguish genuine automation backlog from intentionally human-governed verification; and
4. the generated conformance coverage inventory reflects those governed states and passes repository validation.
