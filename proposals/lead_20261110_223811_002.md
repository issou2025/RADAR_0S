# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Railings are written as record boxes, about a thousand times Revit's volume, and now sit inside their stairs**

*Project description:*
## What

rvt-rs writes a railing as its record box where the railing's saved mesh is not whole (RE-303, #1247). Revit's railing is a thin body, so the box is far too large: in Measure 42688171578 (head `a43f517d`, 34 models with a box scorer file) the 109 railings written as boxes across 11 models have a median fill (Revit's volume divided by the box's) of 0.001. No other class is near that: sanitary terminals 0.198, building element proxies 0.144, windows 0.119, doors 0.254, furniture 0.062; members, columns and plates are 1.0 (the box is the body).

Since #1626 the railings of a stair are parts of its `IfcStair`, so the stair's volume as the sum of its parts now carries the boxes: on the BIM4LCA office, 13 stairs go from within 1 percent of Revit's volume to 2 to 127 times it (stair 2598347: 100.9 ft3, 707.6 with the boxes, Revit 101.6; 12 others about 110 to 117 ft3 against 3,600 to 4,100). A viewer shows the same boxes as before, inside the stair that owns them.

## Done when

A railing's body is Revit's, or the railing writes no body, where the saved mesh is not whole. Which of the two is the open question: RE-303 section 3 does not say why a railing's saved mesh lacks parts of Revit's body (rails and balusters may be drawn by other elements' graphics).
