**Verified live vs. source:**
- A fresh third-party live fetch returned both pages, original expected titles, index/follow robots, self-canonical metadata and the expected phone **909-375-6685**; no non-working canonical redirect was observed by this fetch.
- Repo main HTML: the dedicated laundry page has **one relevant content-appliance image plus logo**, **no HTML `<form>`**, and a set of prominent direct links to `/rancho-cucamonga-appliance-pickup/#request`. The general city page does contain the one shared Formspree form, and its appliance selector explicitly includes Washer, Dryer and Washer and Dryer; dedicated-page links are not broken. The shared-form architecture is intentional and must not be overturned casually.
- The main city page links back to the specialist laundry page; the California and Inland Empire hubs also link to the specialist page. Both appear in the existing 726-URL sitemap with accurate 2026-11-11 modification dates.
- The City of Rancho Cucamonga public bulky-waste alternative is documented separately on the laundry page (not represented as our service).
- The specialist page has appeared in Google query results: **this is a ranking/relevance and possible UX-funnel question**, not evidence that Google completely cannot index the page.
- Query volume for the specialist page is small (only 10 impressions for this exact query over 31 days); it cannot support confident causal conclusions by itself.
- A recent page-source search-result snippet showed older photo-gallery text, while live/source checks show the current single hero. Treat search snippets as possibly stale rather than evidence the deployment failed.

**Evidence-backed conclusion, not a diagnosis of a penalty:** the general page received more impressions and a better average position for this specific laundry query; the specialist page currently requires users to leave the page to reach the shared form. These are measurable findings; they do **not** by themselves establish why Google selected either URL or prove content cannibalization, a technical penalty, or a CTR remedy.

**Blocking verification step:** Use Search Console URL Inspection for **both exact URLs**, recording (a) indexed status/reason, (b) last crawl, (c) user-declared canonical, (d) Google-selected canonical, and (e) indexed/live HTML or screenshot where available. Google's live test does not reveal what canonical it *will* select; the indexed report does. Official docs: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9012289/ and https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/urlInspection.index/inspect.

**First experiment gate:** Only after inspection, choose ONE page and ONE reversible variable (e.g., a single approved internal-intent or conversion-flow improvement). Measure query/page performance in later settled windows before expanding.
