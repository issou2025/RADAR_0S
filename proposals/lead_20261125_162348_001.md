# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**IFC import: beams and columns lose their material (IfcMaterialProfileSetUsage), concrete without a class in its name gets no E, slab openings dropped**

*Project description:*
Found while preparing #120: I imported a small reference house (IFC4 written by IfcOpenShell, physical model only) into `pnpm dev` at 23a44658 on Linux, then ran `analysis.defaultCases`, `analysis.addMissingCases` and `analysis.run` through `lapinbim`. The model, its generator, the test copies below and the script that runs it all are attached (`reference-house-lapin.zip`, CC0).

Everything comes in (10 walls, 19 columns, 233 beams, 13 slabs, 7 footings, 8 rooms), but the analysis does not start. I found four causes by changing one thing at a time in test copies of the file:

1. **Beams and columns lose their material.** `MaterialOf` in `engine/src/Platform/Lapin.Ifc/IfcImport.Recognition.cs` reads layer sets, `IfcMaterial`, material lists and constituent sets, but not `IfcMaterialProfileSetUsage` / `IfcMaterialProfileSet`. That is how IfcOpenShell, Revit and Tekla write the material of a beam or column. The material comes back empty, `ForeignElements` falls back to „Reinforced concrete”, which has no E, and all 502 bars are refused. This may well be what #115 hits too. (Test copy: `reference-house-plain-bar-materials.ifc`, the same material as a plain `IfcMaterial`.)
2. **A concrete with its strength but no class in its name gets no E.** The material arrives with `Pset_MaterialConcrete.CompressiveStrength` = 25 MPa, and that is imported correctly. But `StructuralMaterials` derives Ecm from fck only when the kind is concrete, and the kind comes from the name (a C25/30 class, "concrete", "beton") or a layer category. `IfcMaterial.Category` = "concrete" is not imported, and having a `Pset_MaterialConcrete` is not taken as a sign either. Spanish names such as `HA-25/B/20/IIa` fall through: bars are refused, and slabs and walls made of it quietly become „Slabs not of concrete … 45° rule”. (Test copy: `reference-house-plain-bar-materials-classes.ifc`, "C25/30" appended to the names.)
3. **Openings in slabs are not carried over.** Both floors have a stair opening (`IfcOpeningElement` voiding the slab). The fidelity check measures the native floor without it (+8.7 % volume) and brings the floor in as a reference, so the floor drops out of the analysis. The rooms' imposed load then "finds nothing under it" and the beams around the stair become a mechanism. `ForeignElements` does add `prism.Holes` as openings, so I suppose voids are simply not part of the prism the recognition reads. (Test copy: `reference-house-probe-no-openings.ifc`, slab openings removed.)
4. **The default „Reinforced concrete” has no E of its own**, so every fallback ends in a refusal rather than an indicative value.

With 1 to 3 worked around (`reference-house-probe-no-openings.ifc`), the model builds (334 nodes, 500 bars, 3727 shell elements) but meets a mechanism at the first floor's edge beam, at `(7.05, 9.9, 2.825)`, where the stair wall line meets it. I stopped there: that is how the analytical model joins eccentric beams, walls and slabs, and I'd rather not guess at it from outside.

A smaller one: the two basement stair walls come in as references ("box 150 mm at the base"). They stand on the footing and pass through the basement slab, and Lapin seems to want them on top of it.

**Repro:** `pnpm dev`, then `python import_and_analyse.py reference-house.ifc out/` from the zip. It saves every answer (survey, import, census, cases, loads, preview, run, results, checks) as JSON.

I'm happy to make PRs for 1, 2 and 4, with tests built from small IFC fixtures, before the NDP work in #120, if you agree. Without them no IFC from another program reaches the checks. For 3 I'd need your guidance on where the voids should enter the recognition.
