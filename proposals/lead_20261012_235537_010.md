# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Sign in without a password by approving a push notification**

*Project description:*
## Problem

Users without a passkey still type a password, or wait for a code or link over email or SMS. They can't sign in by approving a notification on their phone.

However this has the following concerns.

- **Passwords can be phished or reused across sites.** Removing them removes that risk for these users.
- Codes and links depend on email or SMS delivery, and SMS has a cost.
- Organizations with their own mobile app can't use it to sign users in on other devices.

Users need to be able to register their phone in the organization's mobile app. Then they enter their username and sign in by approving a push notification. The notification shows which application and device is signing in. Builds on push provider configuration (#5754).

### Who is it for

- End users: sign in with one tap, without a password.
- Application developers: passwordless sign-in through their own mobile app.
- Security teams: fewer passwords to phish, and protection against repeated requests.

### Why now

- Value to persona: passwordless sign-in for users who can't or don't use passkeys.
- Value to org: completes passwordless options next to passkeys and magic links. Needed before push as a second factor (#5756) and registration during sign-in or sign-up (#5757).

## Scope

In scope:

- Registering a device from the organization's mobile app by scanning a QR code, through the self-service APIs.
- Push approval as the only sign-in step after the username, with approve and deny.
- Showing the application, IP address, browser and operating system in the notification.
- Number matching, on by default.
- Request timeout, resend interval and resend limit.
- Limiting repeated denied or unanswered requests.
- Multiple devices per user, with a maximum.
- Sending each request to all of the user's devices, or to one device the user picks, configurable.
- Notifying the user by email or push when a new device is registered.
- Not revealing whether the username exists or has a device, before approval.
- Listing and removing registered devices, through the self-service APIs and from the user's view in the Console.
- Adding passwordless push in the flow builder.
- Offering passwordless push as a sign-in option when creating an application.
- Device registration and responding to requests in the mobile SDKs (Android, iOS, Flutter, React Native).
- A sample mobile app showing device registration and approval.
- Rendering push sign-in across the SDKs.

Out of scope:

- Push approval as a second factor (#5756).
- Signing in without a username. Ex: scanning a QR code on the sign-in page.
- Registering a device during sign-in or sign-up (#5757).
- A ThunderID authenticator app.
- Falling back to an app-generated code.
- Device integrity or attestation checks.
