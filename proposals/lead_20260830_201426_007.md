# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**[RoomVPN][Mobile][Subscription] "Get Started" cannot be tapped — no plan can be bought from the app**

*Project description:*
Filing this because it was found by the developer rather than reported, and QA should re-test the purchase path.

**Fixed and released as 1.0.23+30.** It was broken in 1.0.22+29.

## What was wrong

On the subscription screen the three plan cards rendered normally and **not one of them could be tapped**. Tapping "Get Started" did nothing at all — not intermittently, always. The app's entire purchase path was dead.

## Cause

A regression from the accessibility fix that made the card `Flexible` so it could yield at a large system font. That put it in competition with the `Spacer(flex: 1)` above and `Spacer(flex: 2)` below it, and `Flex` shares free space in proportion to `flex` with no notion of priority — so the card won a quarter of it, about 288 logical pixels, where its content needs ~452.

It overflowed its box by 164 pixels (`A RenderFlex overflowed by 164 pixels on the bottom`, once per card). Flutter does not hit-test a point outside a `RenderBox`, so the button was painted 164 pixels below its parent's bounds: it looked completely normal and the tap fell straight through to the scrim behind it.

Measured on device — the ancestors stop containing the button's centre exactly at the `RenderFlex`:

```
RenderSemanticsGestureHandler rect=75,593 262x53  containsButtonCentre=true
RenderFlex                    rect=75,426 262x56  containsButtonCentre=false
```

Nothing was disabled, which is what made it look like a mystery: every `IgnorePointer` read `ignoring=false`, the `AbsorbPointer` `absorbing=false`, every `FadeTransition` opacity `1.0`.

## The fix

The block below the header is centred rather than padded with `Spacer`s, which leaves the card the only flexible child. It takes what it needs up to `cardHeight` and the leftover is split above and below it. It still shrinks at a large font — what the original change was for — and no longer shrinks when there is room.

## For QA

Please check on a real device, at **both** normal and large system font (Settings → Display → Font size):

1. Open the subscription screen and tap **Get Started** on the centre card — it should respond.
2. Swipe to each of the other plans and tap theirs too.
3. Confirm no yellow-and-black striped overflow bar appears at the bottom of the card.
4. Confirm the card still looks centred and uncramped at both font sizes.

Worth testing on a short screen as well: the card is sized at 55% of screen height, so a small phone is where it has least room.
