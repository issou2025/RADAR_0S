*Project description:*
## Why
Some things a web app cannot do, or not on every phone: receive a picture or file from the share sheet (#148, open on iPhone), notify about a new note in a feed, and be found in the app stores.

## What to decide first
- **What the apps are for.** A first version could be small: the feeds this phone follows, reading notes, posting text, pictures and files, and receiving a share from other apps. Notifications are a second step, because they need a push service and something on the server that knows which phone follows which feed, which notefeed does not store today.
- **How to build them.** One codebase for both (React Native or Flutter), a thin native shell around the web UI (Capacitor) with a native share extension, or two native apps. The share extension and notifications are native code in every variant.
- **Which instance.** The apps must work with any notefeed instance, not only notefeed.me: the instance's address is a setting, and the app talks to the versioned REST API and nothing else.
- **Cost and upkeep.** Developer accounts at Apple and Google, the stores' review rules for apps that show user-posted content, signing and releases in CI, and who answers store reviews.

## Not decided here
- Whether the apps are free, and how they relate to licensed feeds (#42).
