# What MudCrab can learn from spud

**spud's most useful lesson is consistency across the whole engine.** That is our assessment after studying chasmlol's implementation. Its renderer carries source-shaped materials, weather, ambient light, fog and image space through a purpose-built pipeline. Its importer and runtime also cover actors, scripts, quests, menus, audio and saves. Similar-looking outdoor views are therefore only one part of the project.

Credit belongs to **chasmlol and [spud](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud)**. It is an MIT-licensed C++ engine that imports the player's own Skyrim Special Edition data. One headphone-filter dataset has its own credited license. This study covers spud **e5f2de4** and MudCrab upstream main **6c887918**. Upstream spud still pointed to the studied commit when checked. We read source and reused earlier measurements. We ran no game, GPU test or new import. Code support below does not mean completed retail parity. [spud's description and license](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/README.md#L3-L9), [dataset credit](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/README.md#L46-L48).

## Importing is already a strength in both projects

**spud offers a useful linking design; MudCrab already has substantial conversion safeguards.** spud compiles plugins into independently cached units, then resolves load-order-dependent references and winning overrides when linking them. Reordering unchanged plugins can reuse their units. However, the full import also constructs a separate legacy world database. This is not evidence that every plugin read disappears. [Unit keys and linking](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/importer/records/forms_import.cpp#L95-L192), [second database](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/importer/pipeline.cpp#L895-L910).

MudCrab already has database reuse, dependency-aware ordering, stable owning-plugin identities and verified staged outputs. It also reads BA2 archives; no BA2 path was found in spud. Both retain more records than their runtimes necessarily understand. Record retention, decoded fields and live gameplay support are different measures. Our assessment is to evaluate per-plugin compilation without weakening MudCrab's recovery or publication guarantees. [Identity/order](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/converter/src/esm/load_order.rs#L324-L390), [cache checks](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/converter/src/cache.rs#L38-L57), [archive dispatch](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/converter/src/archive/mod.rs#L525-L551).

Our earlier scoped spud import reported **661.7 seconds**, with eight threads, Tamriel only, no interiors and no audio. The retained cache inventory was about **22.59 GB**, including routed runtime caches. Those are our historical measurements, not whole-game figures or a speed/size comparison with MudCrab. The appendix preserves the original receipts and missing-data qualifications.

**Different formats bring different maintenance costs.** spud's common chunk header makes its native contracts easy to follow across subsystems. MudCrab's standard interchange assets fit Bevy's tooling, while its custom caches and database serve world queries. Neither design removes the need for versioning and invalidation. spud's asset freshness key can depend on a whole archive, and its cached file hashes trust unchanged size and modification time. MudCrab verifies staged output hashes. We would test full-versus-incremental equivalence before changing either policy. [Native container](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/formats/container.hpp#L29-L45), [source granularity](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/importer/legacy/data_source.cpp#L95-L103), [hash shortcut](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/importer/records/forms_import.cpp#L39-L52).

The projects also have different platform boundaries. spud documents a Windows build and uses Windows-specific platform and audio services; Linux and Steam Deck remain later roadmap items. MudCrab's current parity scope includes Windows, Linux and macOS. That broader target constrains any renderer or audio adoption, but this study supplies no new cross-platform test result. Language and graphics API alone do not establish portability or speed. [spud platforms](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/ROADMAP.md#L21-L26), [MudCrab scope](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/milestone/44).

The import figures above, named city outcomes below and grass counts are reused local evidence (see the source ledger in the comments below), with their source revisions and measurement scope retained.

## World fidelity includes deliberate differences

**spud deliberately replaces retail distant LOD.** Level of detail (LOD) reduces geometry as objects recede. spud generates simplified meshes, regional proxies and normal-bearing tree impostors from full-detail data. Its terrain selects detail by projected error and morphs between levels. Regional proxies omit several material categories and small objects. These are concrete trade-offs, not evidence that they satisfy MudCrab's stricter every-reference E2 acceptance bar. [Design intent](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/VISION.md#L26-L35), [proxy eligibility](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/importer/far/far_proxies.cpp#L294-L342), [terrain selection](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/render/terrain.cpp#L702-L815).

City merging is also more than batching terrain. spud selects child cities using records and paired-door distances, then removes parent stand-ins and duplicate geometry. Our earlier import log reports MarkarthWorld and WhiterunWorld merges. That supports those outcomes for that dataset, not universal city-mod compatibility. Seamless interiors require multiple spaces, transforms and shared state; MudCrab's current safe fade-and-load transition is a different architectural choice.

**Grass placement work should be reused, not restarted.** With deliberately matched terrain and water inputs, our retained CPU control checked 217 cells and 816,988 exactly matched source placements before obstruction; packed roots remain quantized. This is not a claim of production-cache or rendered parity. That work is in the grass PR stack (#282 → #539 → #540 → #541 → #611 → #612, with placement in #612); pinned upstream main has grass export but no grass runtime yet. Rendering remains a separate question: spud uses scalar landscape brightness, mesh-alpha-driven wind, distance fading and a direct-light shadow floor. Its grass fragment discards low alpha and writes opaque alpha for accepted pixels, so a general alpha-to-coverage description alone does not establish smooth grass coverage. [Grass shaders](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/shaders/grass.vert#L25-L58), [fragment behavior](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/shaders/grass.frag#L20-L34). Uploads also truncate each generated cell to 49,152 instances, a real submission limit to retain in any completeness assessment. [Upload cap](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/engine/grass.cpp#L468-L479).

## The visual gap is a pipeline question

**Our assessment is to resolve the light and colour contract before tuning isolated multipliers.** spud deliberately avoids automatic sRGB texture decoding and describes its half-float scene as gamma-space. MudCrab publishes diffuse textures as sRGB and approximates source gloss with physically based rendering roughness. A final tone curve cannot generally undo different arithmetic throughout a frame. [Texture views](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/render/textures.cpp#L22-L47), [scene contract](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/shaders/common.glsl#L1-L3), [MudCrab conversion](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/converter/src/material.rs#L500-L530).

spud evaluates six directional ambient lighting colours, blends weather and image spaces, and distinguishes visible sun position from light direction. MudCrab retains much of this data, but preserving a field is not the same as consuming it. The same distinction applies to interior lighting templates, environment maps and parallax. Some spud settings are hardcoded values attributed by its comments to captures/settings dumps; we do not claim arbitrary user overrides already propagate everywhere. [Ambient and image spaces](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/engine/weather.cpp#L1106-L1160), [fixed settings](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/render/renderer_post.cpp#L18-L39).

Both engines have sun shadows, custom skin/hair handling, effect-material work and reflected water. The differences are in equations, feature coverage and integration. spud also has local-light shadows, spatial screen-space ambient occlusion, layered sky and depth-aware water. MudCrab's current converted point lights disable shadows and skip negative lights. Its fog intentionally uses a different model. These should become controlled feature comparisons, with existing work credited. [Point lights](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/engine/src/lights.rs#L153-L199), [fog choice](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/docs/specs/engine/sky.md#L66-L93).

## Breadth does not prove completion

**spud has considerably broader live gameplay code, with explicit remaining gaps.** Its VM handles events, waits and saved continuations. Quests dispatch script fragments; AI connects packages to navigation. Native saves capture multiple running systems. However, unimplemented natives can warn and return defaults, unknown animation nodes become null nodes, and some menus still use captured data where live host values are unavailable. These are reasons to qualify completeness, not dismiss the implementation. [Native stand-ins](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/script/natives.cpp#L485-L520), [unknown graph nodes](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/anim/graph.cpp#L524-L535), [UI host boundary](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/src/ui/ui.hpp#L370-L378).

Physics is not a blank in MudCrab: it already has Rapier walking, collision and dynamic clutter. spud uses Jolt and more extensive movement/gameplay integration. Switching libraries would not itself reproduce movement semantics. Audio has a similar distinction: Bevy's audio capability is not Skyrim's authored mixing rules. MudCrab's current E1 selects Seedling/Firewheel; spud's XAudio2 backend should inform comparisons rather than override that decision. [MudCrab physics](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/blob/6c8879186727df9bb574111ac56b7783dfc7bb7c/crates/engine/src/physics.rs#L269-L390), [E1](https://github.com/Mudcrab-Team/mudcrab/milestone/5).

## What the evidence says about matching

**spud explicitly asks for comparisons with the real game.** Its vision makes that the verification standard. Its tools expose held clocks, drawn-camera state, material/form/UV views, HDR and shadow exports, contact traces and comparable record dumps. That supports a measurement-driven explanation of its results. It does not establish an exhaustive public parity pass or reveal every method the author used. We make no further inference about those methods. [Verification standard](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/VISION.md#L8-L24), [diagnostic controls](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/AUTOMATION.md#L23-L50), [record dumps](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/apps/skytool/cmd_gamedump.cpp#L1-L2).

Its published performance note reports 375–378 average frames per second for three historical tours at a named earlier revision, with hardware/presentation caveats and unresolved stalls. We did not reproduce that result or compare MudCrab under matching conditions. The useful lesson is to measure cold loads, frame-time tails, late ticks and presentation costs. [Published measurements](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/PERF_CHANGES.md#L5-L28), [limits](https://github.com/chasmlol/spud/blob/e5f2de4/docs/PERF_CHANGES.md#L139-L155).

## Our adoption priorities

**Start with evidence and shared visual foundations.** Our ranked proposals are: a uniform diagnostic/state contract; an explicit parity colour pipeline; effective weather/interior/directional ambient; authored terrain tests; complete image-space ordering; authored fog; local-light semantics; cold-load/frame-tail budgets; a measured per-plugin linker prototype; and safe object/tree LOD handoff with an impostor prototype. These map principally to E4–E7 and G1–G4. They are proposals, not accepted milestones.
