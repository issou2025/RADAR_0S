# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Revit 2027 rac_advanced: 79 curtain members get no IsExternal where the 2025 and 2026 saves write it**

*Project description:*
## What

On Autodesk's `rac_advanced` sample saved by Revit 2027, 79 curtain members get no `Pset_MemberCommon.IsExternal`, where the same project's 2025 save writes one. The same project's 2026 save writes all 3,192, as the 2025 save does.

Found by #1861's unfiltered Measure ([43336468164](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/actions/runs/43336468164)), which reads a wall's type Function on 2026 and 2027 (#1860). `readers_across_saves.py` on the 2027 save counts `IfcMember Pset_MemberCommon.IsExternal: equal 3113, differ 0, only here 0, only there 79`. On 2026 it counts `equal 3192 ... only there 0`. Nothing is written wrong: the 79 are left out.

**The lead.** The 2027 export's diagnostics differ from the 2026 export's in one line that touches curtain walls:
- `exported.wall_cut_openings_left_out.curtain_wall_box_not_read` is 3 on 2027 and absent on 2026.
- On 2026, `curtain_wall_within_half_foot_of_wall_end` is 3 instead.

So on 2027 three curtain walls' boxes are not read. The 2027 export's `parameter_value_count` is exactly 79 lower than 2026's (20,956 against 21,035). Whether the 79 members are those three curtain walls' is the first question.

## Done when

- The 79 members are named, with the curtain wall and the wall each takes `IsExternal` from.
- What 2027 does not read for them is found: the curtain wall's box, its host wall, or another link.
- The read is fixed fail closed, or stated as a measured negative. Either way it is held, as #1861 was, to the 2025 save element by element.
