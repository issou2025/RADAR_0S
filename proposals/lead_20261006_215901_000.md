## Project Understanding

*Project description:*
## Why
Steer #1026 asked for a test of agentic spooling: a rack of 7 × 3" conduits, 10 ft long, built to constructability, fabrication and electrical-code standards. The session built it as a one-off: a 245-part generic model, VALID with 0 errors, provenance ok, 2026 and 2025.

It was composed by a scratch script from the strut-trapeze archetype plus round runs. Doing that again needs a session. The test also showed what a product lane needs:
- **The prompt router cannot build it.** "a rack of 7 3 in conduits 10 ft" resolves to a single conduit run.
- **The spool's numbers are not checked:** conduit spacing, support spacing and strut length come from no rule.
- **No connectors:** the conduit ends carry none, because the writer hosts connectors on box faces only (see the second issue below).
- **No drives:** Width, Depth and Height are reported only, because the multipart path's drives need a rectangular first part.

## DONE
1. **Archetype.** A `conduit_rack` archetype or route, prompt-reachable: "rack / bank / spool of N × S in <EMT|IMC|RMC|PVC> conduit, L ft". Its parameters:
   - conduit count, trade size, type, length, tiers;
   - centre-to-centre spacing;
   - support spacing and overhang;
   - strut size and rod size.
2. **Every number has a stated source:**
   - **Conduit and coupling ODs:** a sourced table per type, cited to the standard. A value not held is `nominal` and said.
   - **Spacing:** derived from the clamp and coupling ODs plus a stated clearance, so clamps and couplings never touch. Add a test for that.
   - **Support spacing:** the NEC support rule for the chosen conduit type, cited by article and table. For EMT that is 358.30(A); for RMC, 344.30(B) and its table. Never NFPA text, and unchecked values reported as unchecked (S-2026-09-23-a).
   - **Overhang:** keeps a field joint between spools within that spacing.
3. **Fabrication data as parameters:** Spool ID, conduit type and size, count, spacing, support spacing, strut and rod. Also the end convention: couplings on one end, the other end receiving.
4. **Conduit connectors at both ends of every run**, once round-end connectors exist (the second issue below), so conduit can route to the spool in Revit.
5. **Tests:**
   - the 7 × 3 in × 10 ft case and a 4 × 2 in × 20 ft RMC case;
   - clearances between clamps, couplings and rods;
   - support spacing within the cited rule;
   - VALID with 0 errors on 2024, 2025 and 2026.
6. No "works in Revit" claim without a desktop verdict (hard rule 4).
