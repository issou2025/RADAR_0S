Fresh operator evidence 2026-08-10
- Operator commonly browses/selects content in the native YouTube app on the phone while Home Edge is the playback target.
- Starting playback from that phone YouTube app produces video audio on Home Edge, but the visual screensaver remains on the display.
- Therefore current screensaver suppression/wake logic is coupled to a local Home/Skeleton UI action or otherwise fails to observe externally initiated active playback.
- No private video title, account, URL, device address or session identifier may enter GitHub/tests/logs.

Correct product contract
Screensaver ownership must depend on authoritative media/display state, not on which UI originated Play. Any confirmed active video playback session targeting the Home Edge display must immediately take display ownership, stop/hide the visual screensaver, ensure the display is awake as required by current power policy, and expose the video. This must work equally for playback started from Home UI, direct remote, native YouTube app on the phone, Cast/control handoff, or another approved media control surface.
