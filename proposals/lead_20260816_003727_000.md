# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Imported meshes look rounded: RecalculateNormals smooths 90-degree edges**

*Project description:*
## Context

Headset 2026-08-16: «на некоторых моделях есть smooth нормалей, например на квадратных
брусках, а ощущение что там чуть ли не цилиндр».

`ImportController` builds every baked element with a plain
`mesh.RecalculateNormals()` (`Assets/RoomPlanner/Scripts/Import/ImportController.cs`).
That averages the normals of ALL faces meeting at a vertex — and extruded solids
(`EmitExtruded` in `IfcImporter`) share the ring vertices between adjacent side quads, so
a 90° corner of a square bar is averaged into a smooth gradient. The bar reads as a
cylinder.

Flat-shading everything is not the answer either: Revit tessellates curved surfaces into
Breps, and those need the smoothing (a lamp shade must not become a faceted drum).

## Expected

- Angle-based smoothing in `RoomPlanner.Core` (pure, unit-testable):
  faces meeting at a vertex are grouped into smoothing clusters; an edge sharper than
  ~40° splits the vertex, anything softer keeps the averaged normal.
- Triangle ORDER and COUNT must survive unchanged — `MepPart.TriStart/TriCount`
  (design/29) are ranges over that list.
- Applied in `ImportController` for both fresh imports and loaded projects, before the
  box UVs are computed (each split vertex then belongs to one face, so the projection
  gets sharper too).
- Watch the vertex count: log the before/after totals of an import.

## Tests

- EditMode: a cube whose vertices are shared between faces comes out with 6 distinct
  face normals; a tessellated cylinder keeps ONE smooth normal per ring vertex.
- Triangle count and order unchanged; part ranges still address the same triangles.
