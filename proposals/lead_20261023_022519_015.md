# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Publish the immutable VcsRef revision-1 artifact contract**

*Project description:*
## Impact

Runtime and VCS consumers gain an authoritative immutable artifact contract for exact published revisions and recorded publishing principals. This contract-only deliverable lets their typed boundaries proceed without waiting for the other built-in artifact definitions; it does not implement publication or artifact storage.

## Problem

Parent #138 covers four built-ins. VcsRef independently blocks Runtime.finalize, PublicationOutcome.ref, CheckoutRequest.rev and Vcs.head_sha in #156, followed by #159 and #195–#197. No immutable schema/behavior revision defines their shared payload.

## Desired outcome

Publish revision 1 of VcsRef: authoritative JSON Schema, immutable validation/digest-selection/summary descriptors, examples and exact canonical-byte vectors. Reconcile the affected specifications and owner-reviewed docs. Keep #138 open for CommandResult, AgentSession and StructuredOutput. Typed models and boundary validation will land in a separate work-item-2 issue/PR.

## Affected

[IR](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/blob/master/knowledge/specs/ir.md), [VCS/artifact protocols](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/blob/master/knowledge/specs/protocols.md), [artifact identity/history](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/blob/master/knowledge/specs/data-model.md), [worker uploads](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/blob/master/knowledge/specs/api-and-agent-surface.md), FR-4.2 and the implementation plan; ADR-0008/0016/0018/0023. Consume #137 role names and #140 publication scope without redefining them.

## Constraints and open decisions

No provisional type, graph/node models, shared diagnostics, storage provider, runtime or executor implementation. Preserve trusted provenance, late authorization and recovery barriers. PrincipalRef will have one shared lower-layer definition. The owner approved the independently reviewed [implementation plan and exact docs wording](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353251683); [approval is recorded here](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353268608). The owner also approved the [revision-5 amendment and exact docs changes](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353558178), recorded in [this approval](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353614983). The owner approved the [URL/upload/enrichment boundary amendment](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353981360), recorded in [this approval](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6353992496). Full-lifecycle and merge permission is granted.

The approved plan fixes the field list, whole-payload digest selection, current status outside the artifact, canonical VcsRef upload bytes and this deliverable split. PR #211 implements the contract; #209 owns the typed-model implementation. The [review clarifications](https://github.com/realkarych/kora/issues/208#issuecomment-6354411050) preserve the approved payload/schema/summary/retention decisions while making existing untrusted-presentation obligations explicit. Inherited resumable grant-history and bounded read-projection gaps are prerequisites for their future consumers in #226 and #227; neither is claimed implemented by this contract. PR #220 must merge and be integrated before #211 freezes revision 1.
