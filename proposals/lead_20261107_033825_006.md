## Summary
Move the Fox codebase from PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 to MIT, and keep the brand assets proprietary.

## Why
The project is being relaunched as free software on both stores. PolyForm Noncommercial is source-available, not open source (GitHub reports it as `NOASSERTION`), which undercuts the whole showcase. MIT is the most recognised open-source licence, has near-zero friction for contributors, is fully App Store compatible, and is what most Flutter developers expect. The trade-off is no explicit patent grant; that is acceptable for an app of this size and keeps the licence one paragraph long.
