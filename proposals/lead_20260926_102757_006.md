# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Mac app: one unified title bar instead of a grey bar above the app**

*Project description:*
## Observed

The macOS app shows the system's standard title bar — grey, with the window title "covey" and a separator line — above the app's own top bar, which carries the covey signet and the person's initials. Two bars stacked, where native Mac apps (Finder, Mail, many chat and office apps) have one: the traffic lights sit in the app's own bar.

A development build is also signed ad hoc. macOS keys permissions such as Accessibility (needed for dictate-anywhere, #355) to the code signature, so an ad hoc build loses them with every rebuild; launched from a terminal, the permission request is attributed to the terminal, and the app does not appear in the list at all.

## Change

- The window gets a transparent, hidden title bar over full-size content and an empty unified toolbar, so the bar is as tall as Finder's. The app's top bars (the space's bar, each page's bar, the wide window's sidebar) take that height, leave the traffic lights their corner and put the signet or the back control right of them. Their empty parts move the window and zoom it on a double click, as the title bar did — the Flutter view receives those clicks, so the window is moved from there.
- The macOS app reads a developer's signing team from a git-ignored `Runner/Configs/Signing.xcconfig`, as the iOS app does (#345); without it the build stays ad hoc.
