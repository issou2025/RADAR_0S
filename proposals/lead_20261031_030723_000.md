Carry the wording over, and add **Built from the bundle** to `GLOSSARY.md` with _Avoid_: projected, projection. `ReceivedVersion.v3_route` is public API, `Literal["server", "projected"]`, and the example and the tests compare against `"projected"`, matching the `v3_route` column of the bucket's `speckle-v3-objects.parquet`.
