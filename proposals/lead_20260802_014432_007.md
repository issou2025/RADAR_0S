<design_system>
### Style
- **Flat geometric SVG.** No photorealism, no gradients, no blur filters.
- **2.5D perspective.** Figures/vehicles face right, slightly toward the viewer. Not top-down, not full side-on.
- **Warmth.** Earthy, hand-made feel. Ink line `#1f1a14` holds everything together.

### Line Weights
- Major outlines: `stroke="#1f1a14"` `strokeWidth="1"`
- Interior detail: `strokeWidth="0.5"–"0.8"`
- Thin highlights: same color, `opacity="0.3–0.5"`

### Material Palette (`MATERIAL_PALETTE` in `sprite-system.tsx`, aliased as `P`)
```
skin:   warm=#d4a373  cool=#b08968  deep=#8a5a3c
cloth:  tunic=#c19a6b  linen=#e6dcc6  wool=#7a6e5b  dye=#5b4a7a
metal:  iron=#5a6068  steel=#8a929b  bronze=#b8895a  gold=#d4a13c  shine=#e8edf2
wood:   light=#c19a6b  mid=#8a6a3a  dark=#5e3f24
stone:  light=#c4b8a4  mid=#9a8e78  dark=#6a5e4a
thatch: straw=#d6b46a  shadow=#8a6a3a
ground: grass=#7ea860  dirt=#a08260  sand=#d8c896  water=#3a6e94
ink:    line=#1f1a14   soft=#3a3228
hud:    food=#7bb850  prod=#c98a3a  gold=#e8c64a  sci=#5fb4d4  cult=#c46db4  mil=#c4413a  esp=#7a5ec4
```

### Faction Color (`FactionPalette`)
```typescript
{ dark: string; mid: string; bright: string; trim: string }
```
Generated by `derivePalette(civColor)`. Every unit receives a `palette` prop — never hardcode a faction name or civ color.
- `palette.mid` → primary cloth/hull-trim/paint fill
- `palette.dark` → belt/shadow/outline accent
- `palette.bright` → gem, glow, highlight dot
- `palette.trim` → heraldic accent, flag/ensign

### Unit Sprite Contract
```typescript
export function FooSprite({ palette, svgOnly = false }: UnitSpriteProps): string
```
- **ViewBox**: `0 0 128 128`
- **Wrapper**: `<SpriteFrame svgOnly={svgOnly}>` (naval units also pass `hexTint={P.ground.water}`, matching `TriremeSprite`) — never a raw `<svg>`
- **Required**: `<Shadow />`
- **data-kind on outermost group**: `naval` for `frigate`/`destroyer` (matches every existing ship in this file); `civilian` for `merchant_wagon` (matches `caravan`'s own data-kind convention)
- **Faction pennant**: `<Banner x={…} y={…} palette={palette} scale={…} />`
- **Animation**: CSS class names only — relevant hooks for this batch: `cq-sail` (sail billow), `cq-cape` (flag/ensign flutter), `cq-smoke[--b][--c]` (funnel smoke), `cq-deliver` (trade-goods glint, already used by `CaravanSprite` — reuse for `merchant_wagon` to keep the "delivering" motif consistent across the trade-unit line)
</design_system>

## SPRITE 1 — FrigateSprite (Unit)

**Insert into**: `src/renderer/sprites/units.tsx`, immediately after `TriremeSprite`
**Catalog entry**: `frigate: withMotion('frigate', FrigateSprite),` (replaces the current
`withMotion('frigate', TriremeSprite)` line and its two-line placeholder comment in
`sprite-catalog.ts`)
**data-kind**: naval

### Concept
An era-6 age-of-sail frigate — a three-masted wooden warship with a full broadside of cannon,
square-rigged sails catching the wind, and a raised quarterdeck at the stern. This is the
mechanical and historical successor to the ancient oared Trireme, so it must read as a real
sailing warship, not a recolored galley: no oars, no single low sail — tall masts, painted
gunport stripe, and visible cannon.

### Key requirements
- Three masts (fore, main, mizzen) with square-rigged sails in `P.cloth.linen`, mainmast tallest —
  a noticeably taller, busier rig than Trireme's single mast
- A painted gunport stripe along the hull (alternating dark squares in `P.wood.dark`/`P.ink.line`)
  with at least 4–5 visible cannon barrel tips (`P.metal.iron`) poking through — the frigate's
  firepower is its whole visual identity, and Trireme has none
- Bowsprit spar projecting forward from the bow — a silhouette element Trireme does not have
- Raised quarterdeck at the stern, higher than the main deck, with the faction `<Banner>` mounted there
- Hull in `P.wood.mid`/`P.wood.dark`, waterline curve reading as a real sailing hull, not an oared
  galley — no oar rows, no oar blades
- `palette.mid` for the hull trim/gunport-stripe accent, `palette.trim` for the ensign at the stern
- Animated element: `cq-sail` class on the sail shapes (billow), `cq-cape` on the ensign (flutter)
- Tone: proud, wind-driven, Age of Sail

## SPRITE 2 — DestroyerSprite (Unit)

**Insert into**: `src/renderer/sprites/units.tsx`, immediately after `IroncladSprite`
**Catalog entry**: `destroyer: withMotion('destroyer', DestroyerSprite),` (replaces the current
`withMotion('destroyer', IroncladSprite)` line)
**data-kind**: naval

### Concept
An era-10 modern destroyer — a long, low, lean grey-steel warship built for speed: a forward deck
gun turret, a radar lattice mast, angled torpedo tubes, and a thin raked funnel venting turbine
exhaust. This must read as decades more advanced than Ironclad's boxy riveted hull and single
blocky smokestack — smooth welded plating, a sleeker silhouette, and modern sensor/weapon fittings
Ironclad never had.

### Key requirements
- Long, low, lean hull in `P.metal.steel`/`P.metal.iron` — noticeably longer and lower-profile than
  Ironclad's short boxy hull; smooth plating with no rivet lines (Ironclad's riveted-plate lines
  must NOT be reused here)
- A single thin, raked funnel (not Ironclad's blocky vertical smokestack) with a faint smoke wisp
- Radar lattice mast amidships — a thin cross-hatched tower structure, a fitting Ironclad has no equivalent of
- Small enclosed forward deck gun turret (not Ironclad's open cannon ports)
- A cluster of angled torpedo tubes amidships
- `palette.mid` as a hull pennant-number stripe, `palette.trim` on a small stern ensign
- Animated element: `cq-smoke` class puff from the funnel top
- Tone: fast, lean, purposeful, industrial-modern

## SPRITE 3 — MerchantWagonSprite (Unit)

**Insert into**: `src/renderer/sprites/units.tsx`, immediately after `CaravanSprite`
**Catalog entry**: `merchant_wagon: withMotion('merchant_wagon', MerchantWagonSprite),` (replaces
the current `withMotion('merchant_wagon', CaravanSprite)` line and its two-line placeholder comment)
**data-kind**: civilian

### Concept
An era-6 four-wheeled merchant wagon: a draft animal pulling a cart loaded with crates and
barrels, with a driver seated up front holding the reins. This is the mechanical successor to the
walking pack-donkey Caravan, so the wheels are the single most important differentiator — Caravan
has no wheels at all, and this unit's whole silhouette should read as a rolling cart, not a
person leading an animal on foot.

### Key requirements
- Two large spoked wooden wheels (`P.wood.dark` rims) — the defining silhouette difference from
  Caravan, which has none
- A draft animal (horse or ox) in harness, positioned ahead of the cart body pulling it — a
  distinctly different pose from Caravan's donkey being led on foot beside a walking merchant
- A driver figure seated on a raised bench at the front of the wagon, holding the reins — not
  walking alongside like Caravan's merchant
- An open or canvas-topped cargo bed behind the driver's seat, with at least 3 distinct stacked
  cargo shapes (crates in `P.wood.light`, sacks/bundles in `P.cloth.linen`)
- A visible wooden axle/undercarriage in `P.wood.dark` connecting the wheels to the cart body
- Faction pennant on a short staff at the driver's seat or wagon rear, `palette.mid`/`palette.trim`
- Animated element: reuse the `cq-deliver` class (coin/gold glint at the cargo bed) — the same
  trade-unit "delivering goods" motif `CaravanSprite` already uses, for continuity across the
  trade-unit line
- Tone: sturdy, prosperous, wheeled commerce
