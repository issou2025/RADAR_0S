 The Opportunity
 



 We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to help build and scale the platform that powers a growing SaaS business. This role combines infrastructure ownership, cloud architecture, and backend engineering. Initially, you'll take the lead on establishing core platform capabilities including AWS architecture, CI/CD automation, observability, and reliability. Over time, as those foundations mature, you'll shift more of your focus toward backend and product development.
 



 This is an ideal opportunity for an engineer who enjoys working across disciplines rather than staying narrowly focused in one area. We're not looking for a pure DevOps engineer. We're looking for a well\-rounded software engineer who can build scalable infrastructure, accelerate developer productivity, and contribute directly to product and backend systems as business needs evolve.
 



 Our technology roadmap is centered on modern cloud\-native architecture and TypeScript\-based services. You'll play a key role in shaping the technical direction of both our platform and application stack.
 



 What You'll Do
 


* Lead the evolution of our AWS infrastructure, cloud architecture, and engineering platform.
* Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines that improve deployment speed, reliability, and developer experience.
* Establish and refine infrastructure\-as\-code practices and deployment automation.
* Improve monitoring, observability, alerting, and operational readiness across production systems.
* Build and maintain backend services, APIs, and distributed systems.
* Modernize legacy applications and services into scalable cloud\-native architectures.
* Partner with engineers across the organization to improve performance, security, scalability, and reliability.
* Evaluate and implement technologies involving containerization, orchestration, deployment automation, and cloud services.
* Contribute to long\-term platform and infrastructure strategy.
* Mentor and support engineers on software architecture, cloud\-native development, and operational best practices.



  





 What We're Looking For
 


* 7\+ years of professional software engineering experience.
* Strong backend engineering experience building and maintaining production systems.
* Hands\-on experience designing, deploying, and operating cloud infrastructure in AWS.
* Experience building CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
* Experience with Infrastructure\-as\-Code tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar technologies.
* Experience working with containerized platforms such as Docker and Kubernetes.
* Ability to move fluidly between platform, infrastructure, and backend development work.
* Strong understanding of distributed systems, networking, security, and reliability principles.
* Experience supporting production environments and participating in incident response.
* Excellent communication skills with the ability to influence technical direction and collaborate across teams.
* Demonstrated ability to learn new technologies and adapt to changing business needs.



  





 Preferred Qualifications
 


* Experience modernizing legacy applications and architectures.
* Experience with TypeScript and Node.js in production environments.
* Experience with microservices, event\-driven systems, and cloud\-native architectures.
* Experience with observability, logging, monitoring, and operational tooling.
* Track record of improving engineering productivity, deployment processes, and platform reliability.
* Experience working in SaaS, cloud software, or data\-intensive environments.
