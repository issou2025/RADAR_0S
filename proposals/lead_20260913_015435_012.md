## Why it matters

The test's own comment says what it covers:

> NOTHING JOINED THE WEAPON TO A HIT BEFORE THIS. `FCataclysmIncomingHit` carries
> `bIsSlashing`, `bIsMagic` and now `bIsPiercing`, and
> `UCataclysmDamageCalculation::Resolve` applies all three correctly. None was ever set, so
> slashing, magic and piercing all did nothing whatever weapon was held.

So this is the test standing over the behaviour that the equipped weapon decides a hit's
sub-type, from issue [#639](https://github.com/sdubois777/Cataclysm/issues/639). **Reading
the source says that behaviour is covered. It is not.** The body of the test has never
executed, so nobody knows whether it passes.

**And nothing reports this.** The run's line — "1689 tests performed, 1689 succeeded, 0
failed" — is the same whether a declared test ran or silently did not.
