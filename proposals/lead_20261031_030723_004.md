# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**W2, W6, W7: geometry and properties on Revit 2015 to 2023, 2026 and 2027 projects, beyond each element's box**

*Project description:*
## Summary

Revit 2024 and 2025 projects get geometry beyond each element's bounding box (walls cut at their joins and drawn as their layers, roofs as their planes, stairs as their steps, openings from their symbols' cut outlines) and many properties and base quantities. On the other releases in the 1.0 range, most of this is not read yet or not measured. W2, W6 and W7 of the [1.0 release criteria](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/blob/main/docs/release-1.0-criteria.md) need it on every release from 2015 to 2027.

## Where things stand

From the [supported profile](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/blob/main/docs/supported-profile.md#by-input):

- **Revit 2023:** each element's bounding box; rooms take their outline. Not read: geometry beyond the box, wall joins and beam cuts, layered or angled joins, design options and IFC export overrides.
- **Revit 2026 (experimental):** the bounding box, and walls cut back at their joins. Measured on one project against Revit's own export (RE-124).
- **Revit 2027 and 2015 to 2022 (experimental):** as on 2026 and 2023 respectively, measured against the same sample projects saved by a neighbouring release rather than against a Revit export of their own (RE-179 to RE-181).

The readers built on native records are tracked in #1158. Revit exports to measure against are tracked in #421 and #408.

## Done when

For each release from 2015 to 2023, 2026 and 2027, each capability that 2024 and 2025 have is either read and measured against a Revit export of that release, or listed as not read with the evidence why. The supported profile and the support matrix carry the result per release.

## Where to start

The geometry readers are under `src/research/partition_*.rs` and `src/research/element_record_*.rs`. Most are gated to 2024 and 2025 by the per-release constants they read. A useful first slice is one capability on one release, measured against the oracle its report names.
