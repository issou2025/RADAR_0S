Issue Description: Need for BimCraft

The AEC industry relies on costly proprietary tools such as Revit, Navisworks, Archicad, Autodesk Forma, and Civil 3D, leading to vendor lock-in and interoperability challenges. BimCraft is needed as an open, IFC-native BIM platform that provides an affordable, integrated alternative for modeling, coordination, analysis, and collaboration while promoting OpenBIM standards and AI-driven workflows.
