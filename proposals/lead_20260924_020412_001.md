# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Stair flights not yet drawn: monolithic (end-with-riser flag), riserless, spiral, and nosing profiles**

*Project description:*
RE-52 draws 34 of Snowdon Towers' 43 stair flights as their treads and risers. This tracks the other 9 and the residuals, with what is measured so far.

## Monolithic runs: geometry measured, one flag missing

Revit's IFC4 geometry of run 2061630 (type 168815, "3/4" Nosing", 2.5 in structural depth), as `[along, up]` from the run's sketch origin:
- Each riser leans from the step's inner corner `(u_{k-1} + N, (k-1)h)` up to its nosing `(u_{k-1}, kh)`, the slanted flag of the run type.
- Each tread runs from its nosing to the next inner corner.
- The underside is one straight line. It runs parallel to the line through the inner corners, one structural depth below it measured square to the pitch: 0.2225 ft vertically at h = 0.375, tread 1 ft, which is 0.2083 ft (2.5 in) square to the pitch.
- Type 951855 ("Solid Base") closes the profile down to the floor instead.

The missing piece is Revit's "End with Riser" run setting:
- 2061630 has 17 riser lines, and its flight riser count is 17. Revit draws 16 risers and ends the run with a tread at the 17th line.
- 2234674 (13 lines, 12 risers) and 1563804 (3 lines, 2 risers) end with a tread too.
- The 34 drawn runs all end with a riser.

No byte at a fixed offset from the run's data header or from its riser-count anchor separates the two groups, so the setting is stored elsewhere. Until it is read, these runs keep their box.

## Other residuals

- **Upright-riser nosing (4 flights).** Revit shapes the nosing with the type's nosing profile, a family. The tread's front is 0.0625 ft thick and chamfers back. rvt-rs draws it square.
- **First riser on a landing (run 1240916).** Revit carries it one tread thickness below the landing top.
- **Riserless runs, type 1557288 (3 flights).** Treads overhang the run's sides by 0.083 ft, and no read field gives that. The byte at +0xb9 of the run type is 3 on this type only. Separate treads also need a multi-solid body.
- **Spiral runs (2 flights).** Their sketch is arcs, not the straight lines `run_sketch` reads.

Oracles: Snowdon Towers with Revit's IFC4 export (local only). Measured with the RE-52 probe and scoring script.
