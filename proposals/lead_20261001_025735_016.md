## Metrics

### Primary — quality / safety

Measure per fresh task:

- independent verifier-confirmed semantic usefulness/correctness;
- material false-safe / materially wrong conclusion;
- correct abstain / escalation when the local candidate cannot safely finish;
- instruction and schema compliance;
- tool-call correctness where the frozen shadow contract includes tools;
- evidence preservation / source binding;
- recovery / truthful completion signaling.

Any material false-safe or unsafe authoritative-looking output that would cause regression under the frozen hypothetical placement policy is a stop condition.

### Secondary — decision delta

Only after quality/safety survives, measure:

- complete shadow task wall time;
- TTFT p50/p95 where sample size permits;
- cold prefill vs resumed-turn latency;
- prompt/cache reuse;
- decode throughput;
- input/output token usage when exposed;
- strong-Online call-avoidance **potential** under the frozen verifier policy;
- peak memory;
- retries / protocol failures;
- concurrency only if it becomes part of the frozen hypothesis.

Do not promote the candidate from tok/s alone.
