## Binding surface

1. **Only the actual final-click seam is submission authority.** Consolidate semantic submit authorization in **one code-owned pre-submit function** inside `src/tools/browser_skill_apply.py`, invoked by every path capable of issuing the final `bsk.Session.call('click', submit_selector)` IPC (including native/formless direct facade calls). It obtains fresh current tracked-row/duplicate/approval/seed/hold evidence, final page and form evidence, then makes a single accept/refuse decision immediately before the click. There must be no bypass through `execute()`, `_EntrySession`, an alternate model verb or a direct session call; simple IPC wrappers may *enforce* that one result but must not own competing control- or ATS-specific certificates. Existing submission-hold durability, no-auto-retry on possible click, and positive *new* employer confirmation remain obligatory. No prompt, model decision, UI heuristic, site quirk or copied authorization state may substitute for the code boundary. **Application raw-IPC ownership:** The privileged producer is `bsk.Session.call` in `kind='apply'`, narrowly mediated by the existing `browser_skill_apply.py` controller at actual effectful IPC (raw click, Enter/press, choice and upload where commitment is possible). Direct/raw unmediated application `Session.call('click',...)` must produce zero CLI commands, including direct facade escapes. The transport checks only that the same code-owned final Submit decision authorized any committing effect (or that the existing action controller proved a noncommitting effect); no duplicate verifier, re-usable authority token or prompt exception. JD/discovery callers are untouched.
2. **Reversible operations progress.** Same employer-origin and explicitly associated ATS-origin navigation (including a JavaScript Apply button leading to that ATS), opening pickers, filling with an existing approved value, choosing options, uploading the staged row PDF and re-reading after asynchronous DOM mutations are permissible preparation. Bounded retry/re-observe/skip may recover absent controls without converting the inability to prove shape, stable root identity, an iframe or a known widget library into a terminal per-step human gate. The existing *source* authority for approved values, initial dispatch permission, safe origin allowlist, login/CAPTCHA/manual authentication, and prohibition on model-supplied executable JS remain in code; they are **not** shape-based submission admission. Never click an unknown cross-origin target or treat an intermediate action shown to have an actual submit effect as merely reversible. Classify the real irreversible effect at the IPC producer, not by an optimistic "Next", "Apply" or "Continue" label; if its noncommitting nature cannot be established, it cannot bypass the one Submit decision. **Binding surface 2(a)-(d) names existing non-overridable restrictions for the agent-review path:** (a) approved-answer/source and initial-dispatch authority; (b) the safe employer/explicitly associated ATS origin and no unknown cross-origin click; (c) login/CAPTCHA/manual authentication and no model-supplied executable JS; (d) no unproven irreversible intermediate action, with committing effect classified at the IPC producer. This grouping introduces no second guard and no new proof gate for reversible steps. Under ARCH 21:58, #139 removes existing `_configured_alias` / `_alias_current` early tenant/provenance refusal from otherwise authorized reversible preparation by moving their authority to the final guard; this is not permission to navigate an unsafe/unapproved origin. #138 makes no such application-entry change. **Origin-before-disclosure:** Safe employer-origin preparation remains allowed. An ATS-origin cross-tenant navigation, form write or résumé upload first requires independently configured watchlist ATS host+board association plus canonical tracked-row evidence, or authoritative persisted board/feed membership; a page-provided board hint or missing/stale row pin alone is not tenant proof. Without that association, refuse the cross-origin disclosure and name the missing evidence, while continuing safe employer-only steps. This preserves an existing source/origin boundary, **not** the full `_configured_alias`/`_alias_current` pre-browser same-job admission moved to final guard. **Actual effects:** The existing application IPC producer must classify action effects using evidence supported on the production path **before** IPC. Role, label, button type, `href`, IPC verb and post-click redirect do not prove arbitrary JS cannot call `fetch`/`requestSubmit`. No general such witness is established for opaque JS Apply/Continue; absent proof, refuse before click and suggest independently authorized direct navigation/operator inspection. A committing candidate must pass the SAME final guard before raw IPC; if the required final form/job/answers are missing, refuse. Also treat Enter, checkbox/option selection and upload/change event side effects conservatively; possibly issued unknown committing effects are uncertain/held, not safe reversible progress.
3. **Final tracked-job identity and origin.** Re-read the currently dispatched canonical row/immutable job identity and the final browser location. Final origin must be the exact authorized employer origin or the correct explicitly configured ATS origin/tenant for that row (no lookalike, unrelated tenant, arbitrary redirect or page-supplied board hint). A matching *company and exact job ID*, or the **same company and exact tracked job title** on that final origin, is sufficient where the final page exposes no usable ID; a page-exposed *contradictory* job ID rejects even if title matches. Exact title means code-comparable full text (only innocuous edge whitespace normalization, no fuzzy, substring, similarity or inferred synonym). Prove the association from tracked row and independently read final-page identity rather than trusting the model's statement; current pinned ATS data is admissible evidence, but its **absence alone** cannot block preparation. Missing/ambiguous identity at the final check produces `wrong/missing final job identity` and a safe next action, no Submit. **#139, and not #138, relocates `_configured_alias` / `_alias_current` into this single final code decision**, evaluating their fresh tenant/row provenance alongside independently read same-job and final-origin evidence. The owner explicitly keeps the fallback; this is knowingly **non-unique identity evidence**, not proof the target row is unique.
