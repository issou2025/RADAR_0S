### Goal

Obtain, or prove unobtainable, a small set of **legally usable** architectural plans carrying **independently verified** measurements and known quantities, and write the dataset contract that any later reference value must satisfy. This gates the whole experiment and therefore the pivot.

**BLOCKED is a legitimate and valuable outcome.** The directive is explicit: if suitable ground truth cannot be obtained, report BLOCKED. Do not substitute synthetic fixtures and claim real-world accuracy, and never manufacture ground truth from BuildSmart's own calculations.

### Current behavior

The repository holds 19 real ResPlan plans (CC BY 4.0) as vector room polygons. They carry **no verified quantities, no wall heights, no materials and no finishes**, and their metric scale is *inferred* from the dataset's own stated gross area rather than measured — #257 verified that. So they are plans without ground truth, which is precisely what this Issue may not substitute.

Nothing in the repository has ever compared a computed quantity to a surveyed one.

### Required behavior

Write the **dataset contract** first: what a reference project must supply before it counts. At minimum, per project — the plan itself and its licence; per-room floor areas as measured, with the method; room perimeters or enough geometry to derive them; **door and window widths** (baseboard runs break at openings, and wall paint deducts them); and for wall paintable area, **the wall heights and the opening areas, stated by the source**, because the directive forbids inferring them. Per reference value: source, measurement method, units, revision and reliability. State explicitly what a project missing any of these can and cannot be used for, since a plan without heights still supports three of the four first materials.

Then attempt acquisition, and keep a log of what was tried and what each source actually offers. Candidate avenues, to be assessed rather than assumed: open government or municipal as-built archives; university or research datasets published with measured quantities; open BIM/IFC models whose quantities are derivable and independently stated; published quantity-surveying worked examples or textbook case studies with full dimensions; standards bodies' worked examples; and anyone the owner can ask for a real project with a professional takeoff. Web research is in scope. **No paid acquisition without the owner's approval** — if the only viable sources are paid, name them, their cost and their licence, and stop there.

For every candidate, record the licence and whether it permits the use, and reject anything whose terms do not. A dataset that cannot be used legally is not a dataset.

Finish with a plain verdict: **ACQUIRED** with the projects and their provenance, **PARTIAL** with what is covered and what is missing, or **BLOCKED** with what was tried, what each source lacked, and what the owner could supply that no public source can.

### Acceptance Criteria

AC-1: the dataset contract states the required fields per project and per reference value, including source, measurement method, units, revision and reliability
AC-2: the contract says what a project missing heights or openings can still be used for
AC-3: every candidate source is recorded with its licence and a usable-or-not verdict
AC-4: no ground-truth value is derived from BuildSmart's own calculation and no synthetic fixture is offered as real-world evidence
AC-5: a verdict of ACQUIRED, PARTIAL or BLOCKED is stated, with the acquisition log behind it
AC-6: no paid acquisition was made, and any paid-only option is named with cost and licence for the owner to decide
AC-7: no production file is changed

### Out of scope

Building the schema, the formulas or the harness, which are #264. Computing any quantity. Any production change. Paying for anything. Marketplace or supplier work.
