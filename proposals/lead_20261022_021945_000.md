# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Feature request: Add Revit-like BIM modeling capabilities to support a full CAD → BIM → construction → digital twin workflow**

*Project description:*
This is a feature request, not a bug. I strongly suggest adding Revit-like BIM modeling capabilities so users can do actual BIM authoring and development work, not just CAD drawing.

CAD and BIM are complementary. Together they form a complete workflow:

**CAD drawings → BIM modeling → construction → digital twin → simulation**

Revit and its ecosystem are commercially monopolized and expensive. An open-source alternative that connects CAD and BIM would be extremely valuable for the AEC industry.
