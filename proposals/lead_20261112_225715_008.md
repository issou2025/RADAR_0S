# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Mobile layout system: page templates and shared components**

*Project description:*
Mobile (Flutter) layout and organisation review, 2026-11-12. Five read-only reviewers each took one area of `apps/mobile_android` at `origin/main` `b1c58ff07c`: Home + recording, the Training/Fitness hub, Social, You + Settings, and the shared design-system primitives (with a web comparison and a benchmark against Strava, Nike Run Club, Garmin Connect, Apple Fitness and Material 3). Each recorded every screen with the same anatomy (how you reach it, container, app bar, header, controls, primary action, body, states, spacing). Their full reports, with file:line evidence, are the comments below; this body is the merged plan.

Everything was read from code; nothing was run. Points about how screens look rendered (off-screen tabs, content jumps, Save below the fold) are inferred and flagged as such in each report.

The owner's direction: **reusable, shared components**, so the layout is owned by widgets rather than by rules each screen follows by hand.

## What's wrong, in one paragraph

The values are mostly fine: #666 already gave the app radius, icon, colour and type scales with guard tests, and the page gutter is 16 at 89 of 115 sites. What drifts is **page structure**. 125 screens build their own `Scaffold` and 118 their own `AppBar`, and nothing checks where the title, controls, primary action and states go. So the four bottom-nav roots have four different top bars, the two hubs follow opposite chrome contracts, every Fitness tab puts its main action somewhere different, and links between tabs often push a second copy of a screen instead of selecting its tab.

## Findings that showed up in more than one area

1. **Two hubs, two contracts.** The Fitness hub hides its own toolbar and lets every tab bring its own `Scaffold`, `AppBar` and FAB (`fitness_hub_screen.dart:287`); each tab's app bar repeats or renames the tab ("Plans" shows "Training plans", "Segments" shows "Famous segments"). Social's tabs are embedded and the hub hoists one FAB (`social_screen.dart:126-195`). Reported by training-hub, social and system.
2. **Links push second copies instead of selecting a tab.** Home's gym, nutrition and feed links (`dashboard_screen.dart:713-718, 784-792, 1586-1593`), links into Social clubs/challenges (`home_screen.dart:543-546, 710-715`), and `SocialScreen.initialTab` has no caller. Same bug class as § 1654 / § 2437 and the Segments → Park courses link fixed in open PR #1373. Reported by home-record, social, training-hub.
3. **Primary action has no fixed home.** Fitness tabs: FAB on Runs/Routes/Races/Gym (on Races it is the rarely used "Submit race"), inline on Plans, `+` in the app bar on Nutrition, none on Segments/History. Editors save in three places; `add_run_screen` has two Save buttons (`:312`, `:501`). Settings: instant apply, app-bar Save, or inline button.
4. **No shared search/filter bar.** 20 hand-built search fields in about 6 styles, 11 without a clear button; 6 filter idioms across the Fitness tabs; Runs, the longest list, has no search; Discover opens with search, chips, three dropdowns and price chips before any result.
5. **Shared state widgets exist but aren't used.** About 23 empty states are bare centred text and about 14 loaders bare spinners; none of the five Social tabs uses `EmptyState`.
6. **Section headings come in three or four styles,** even within Settings; ui_kit's `SectionHeader` is the small-caps eyebrow, and an in-body section title is hand-built about 18 times in the Training hub alone.
7. **Buried or misplaced entry points.**
   - Messages: only an unlabelled chat icon on the You header (`you_screen.dart:282-300`); no unread badge on any nav item; no "new message" action. Web has Messages at top level.
   - Notifications open from Home, messages from You.
   - Home's toolbar has Feed (duplicates Social) and a Profile icon right above the You tab.
   - Run heatmap only from Routes → Discover; Season plan only from inside New plan; plan calendar feed only from Settings → Integrations; Your shares and Follow requests only from Settings.
   - Park courses after up to 60 race cards (fixed in open PR #1373).
8. **"Start run" doesn't start a run.** The centre button and Home's Start open the recorder, where you press START again (`home_screen.dart:1256-1311`, `run_screen.dart:5363`).
9. **Home is one long scroll** of up to 18 blocks, today's items mixed with every stat; it also duplicates the idle Run page (today's workout, next event, last run, its own Start).
10. **Preferences is one page of about 65 rows in 10 groups** (`settings_preferences_screen.dart:1703-2509`); web splits the same content into six pages (`apps/web/src/lib/settings/preferences_ia.ts`). Privacy zones are 4 taps plus a deep scroll. No settings search. Your own profile has no Edit.
11. **Every upgrade prompt is a dead end:** "upgrade in Settings" text with no link to Pro.
12. **The bottom nav is hand-rolled.** Selection is colour only; the Material 3 `NavigationBar` theme in `app_theme.dart:247` is unused; the tablet rail marks selection differently.

## Proposal: a component-owned layout system

### Page archetypes

Every screen becomes one of these, each with a fixed anatomy owned by one scaffold widget:

| Archetype | Used for | Scaffold | Anatomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hub tab | Fitness and Social tab bodies | `HubTabBody` | no app bar of its own (the hub owns it); one `FilterBar` row (search + Filters sheet + removable chips); list; the hub hoists the tab's one primary action as the FAB; `AsyncView` states |
| Root page | Home, You | `PageHeader` + body | large in-body title, one toolbar row max |
| Detail page | run, route, race, park, course, club, event, challenge, profile, plan | `DetailPage` | small app bar, at most Share + Edit visible, Report/destructive in overflow; hero with a parent chip (event → club, course → park) and a full-width primary button; sections with `SectionHeader`; tabs only for club and profile |
| Settings list | You, Settings, every settings page | `SettingsList` | grouped `ListRow`s (nav / switch / value), group headers, optional search at the top |
| Editor | add run, new plan, route builder, gym session, body metrics, privacy zones, the 9 full-screen form sheets | `EditorPage` | small app bar with close, fields, Save pinned above the keyboard, unsaved-changes guard |
| Map-first | route builder, explore, live spectator, heatmaps | `MapFirstPage` | full-bleed map, floating controls, draggable sheet |
| Sheet | the 55 bottom sheets | `SheetScaffold` | handle, title, body, pinned actions |

### Cross-cutting rules, each enforced by a component

- A screen that is a bottom-nav or hub tab is never pushed; links select the tab (and its view) through one shared channel.
- One primary action per screen, placed by its scaffold: the hub's FAB on hub tabs, pinned Save on editors, the hero button on detail pages.
- A chip filters and never navigates.
- Every page has a title: large in-body on a root page, small app bar on a pushed page; a tab's label is its surface's name.
- One page gutter (16), one section gap (24, 8 within a group), from the token file.
- Nav selection is never shown by colour alone; badges show on the nav item that hosts them.
- Every long-press or hidden gesture has a visible alternative.
- Every upsell opens Pro.

### Guard tests

Eight, beside the #666 guards, modelled on web's `empty_state_guard.test.ts`; each exemption list may only shrink. They fail on a hand-built empty or loading state, a hand-built search field, an eyebrow label outside `SectionHeader`, a `Card` with its own `Padding` outside `ListCard`, a FAB or bottom bar outside the scaffolds, and spacing values off the scale. Details in the system report, § 5.7.

### Build order (biggest win per screen touched)

1. `HubTabBody` + `FilterBar` + `AppSearchField` + `PrimaryAction` on the 7 Fitness and 5 Social tabs (fixes findings 1, 3, 4 on the most-used screens). Lands together with the hub decisions below.
2. `AsyncView` / state widgets move to ui_kit: mechanical, about 25 files.
3. `AppSpace` / `AppInsets` tokens plus a codemod of the off-grid gaps.
4. `EditorPage` with pinned Save (9 sheets at once via `showFullScreenForm`), then the 6 editor screens.
5. `SectionHeader` title + `SettingsList` / `ListRow` across Settings and You, with the Preferences split.
6. `ListCard` across about 65 files (largest, mostly mechanical once 1-5 have shrunk the screens).
7. `DetailPage`, `MapFirstPage`, `SheetScaffold` per feature as screens are next touched.
8. Material 3 `NavigationBar`, and the link-selects-tab channel for Home and Social.

Web in parallel: `PageHeader.svelte`, `FilterBar.svelte`, `SectionHeader.svelte`, `ListCard` / `ListRow`, so both platforms use the same names and slots.

## Search and filters: one `FilterBar` everywhere

Today every list screen builds its own: Feed has wrapping chips + a raw dropdown + caption (`feed_screen.dart:406-460`); Clubs stacks chips, a full-width button and a search field in three rows (`clubs_screen.dart:224-280`); Discover opens with a search field, 5 wrapping chips, 3 dropdowns and price chips before any result (`discover_screen.dart:185-305`); Runs, the longest list, has no search. 20 hand-built search fields in about 6 styles, 11 without a clear button; 6 filter idioms across the Fitness tabs.

Planned control row, the same on every list:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [🔍 Search courses            ✕]  ⋮ │  search, always has clear
│ (All) (5K) (10K) (Half) (Mar…  [⚙ 2] │  ONE line, scrolls sideways
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- At most one search field, always with a clear button.
- At most one row of quick chips, for the screen's most-used filter. **Chips never wrap to a second line**; extras scroll sideways. A chip only filters; it never opens another screen.
- Everything else (place, date, price, cadence, sort) goes in a Filters sheet, and the button shows a count, "Filters (2)". Inside the sheet, chips may wrap.
- Active filters show as removable chips in the same one-line row, so clearing one is one tap.
- API (ui_kit): `FilterBar({SearchFieldSpec? search, chips, selected, onChip, int activeFilterCount, VoidCallback? onOpenFilters, Widget? sort})` + `AppSearchField`. Web twin: new `FilterBar.svelte`, absorbing `ChipDropdown` and the 10 `type="search"` inputs.
- Guard: a hand-built search `TextField` or a `Wrap` of filter chips outside `FilterBar` / the filter sheet fails CI.

Web note: `/races`'s filter panel shows a hint paragraph under every control, which `control_hints_guard.test.ts` requires for every control group on that page. Under this plan the hints would move into the Filters sheet (or a "How this works" link) rather than sit under each control; that changes what the guard enforces (decision below).

## System bars: content must clear Samsung's 3-button navigation bar

The app targets SDK 36 (`android/app/build.gradle.kts:78`), so Android 15+ draws it edge to edge, under the navigation bar, and the opt-out is gone. Each screen must keep its bottom content clear of the bar. The 3-button bar is about 48 dp tall versus a thin gesture handle, so screens that ignore the inset look fine with gestures and overlap with buttons, which is what shows on Samsung phones.

Handling is per screen today:
- Bottom-pinned controls: some wrap in `SafeArea` (`route_builder_screen.dart:1369`, `routes_heatmap_screen.dart:1038`), others sit at a fixed `bottom: 12` with no inset (`coach_screen.dart:1821`, `run_detail_screen.dart:1483`; whether those two actually overlap needs a device check).
- Bottom sheets: 55 call sites, 45 pass `useSafeArea: true`.
- Pinned bottom buttons: only `plan_new_screen` has a real `bottomNavigationBar`; other Save buttons sit at the end of a scroll or are hand-placed.

Durable fix:
1. **The templates own the bottom inset.** `EditorPage` (pinned Save), `SheetScaffold`, `HubTabBody` (FAB + list padding) and `MapFirstPage` (floating controls) add the system bar's height through one shared `BottomActionBar`; screens never place bottom content by hand.
2. **Guard test:** `Positioned(bottom: …)`, a hand-built bottom bar, or a sheet without `useSafeArea` outside those templates fails CI; the exemption list may only shrink.
3. **Simulated 3-button bar in widget tests:** a shared helper renders a screen with `MediaQuery` `viewPadding.bottom = 48` (3-button) and with gesture-nav padding, and fails if any tappable widget's rect intersects the system-bar area. Catches this class in CI without a Samsung.
4. Fix the pages the owner has seen first, in their own PR, then fold the rest in as the templates land.

## Rules for the CLAUDE files

Short rules that point at the shared components and the guards that enforce them, not lists of components or colours (those go stale; see the `ui-polisher` note in the root `CLAUDE.md`). Each rule lands in the same PR as the component and guard that make it true, in `apps/mobile_android/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/architecture/conventions.md`:

- Every new screen is one of the page templates; no raw `Scaffold` with its own `AppBar`.
- A link to a bottom-nav or hub tab selects that tab and its view, through the shared channel; never push a copy.
- One primary action, placed by the template.
- Lists use `FilterBar`: one search field with clear, one line of chips that filter only, the rest in the Filters sheet with a count.
- Loading, empty and error states use the shared widgets; an empty state offers a next step; a failed read is never shown as empty or as a default value (round 2, FS-*).
- No hand-placed bottom content: the templates own the system-bar and keyboard insets, sheets included (round 2, IK-1).
- Spacing and colours come from the tokens; error text uses the AA-passing danger colour (round 2, A11Y-1).
- Every new screen has a widget test at text scale 2.0 on a 320 dp surface, in the longest locale, and with the simulated 3-button bar (round 2, TO-6).
- Same component names on web and mobile.

The cross-project versions of these (UI/UX, frontend, backend, testing) are proposed for every repo in templates PRs Absence0760/templates#21 (files on `base`, read by the review agents) and Absence0760/templates#22 (sync registration).
