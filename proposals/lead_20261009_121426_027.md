## Work packages
- **A01 — Official-result authority (critical):** Join 25 classifications, positions, final lap totals, tie handling and podium to authoritative published results; distinguish official classifications from the last observed snapshot; preserve conflicts and source provenance.
- **A02 — Heat sheet/grid versioning (high):** Exhaustively compare two captured heat sheets, starting-position uniqueness, car numbers/transponders and accepted version authority. Independently recompute `start_position - final_position`. Keep Grid-to-Finish distinct from First Observed-to-Finish.
- **A03 — Driver/team/car identity (high):** Produce a 25-row identifier matrix (car, transponder, entry identifier, source driver/team labels, canonical team), compare both entry-list versions, preserve diacritics and report all ambiguous joins rather than assuming name normalisation establishes identity.
- **A04 — Snapshot coverage (high):** Quantify timestamps, distinct cars, duplicate/absent records, interval distribution, maximum gaps, capture duration, opening gap and analytical coverage per metric; do not reconstruct unobserved race events.
- **A05 — Car 73 anomaly (critical):** Reconcile distinct Ostrov Team lap regressions **98 → 0 at 11:11:19 UTC** and **154 → 141 at 11:20:07 UTC**, plus restoration/recovery and downstream dependencies. Preserve raw data; evaluate whether pace, delay, battles, ranking traces and scores exclude/qualify affected windows. Cause remains unverified unless independently evidenced.
- **A06 — Inferred analytics (high):** Trace inputs/formulas, confidence and constraints for movement, pace, lead changes, pit/delay estimates, head-to-head, report cards and grades; distinguish derived, inferred and official claims; RaceIQ grades are storytelling aids, not official rankings.
- **A07 — Validation/UI integrity (high):** Inspect `tools/validate_static_data.py` and JSON contracts, path resolution, invalid/missing values, incident/caveat visibility and safe HTML rendering; perform genuinely available validation only, explicitly flag browser checks if not run.
