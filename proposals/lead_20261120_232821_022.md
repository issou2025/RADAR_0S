# Project Proposal — Python Automation

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**console: looking forward: the sessions ahead, today's conditions, the scorecard (phase 5 of 5, analysis plan)**

*Project description:*
Phase 5 of 5 of the console analysis plan: arm pages, changes, and a forward read.

Every forward-looking card today is an operational next step: the next buy, the next cycle. None says what to expect, or what is coming that will touch an arm's gates. This phase adds that, using the calendar stores and the regime cuts already on disk.

**Steps (one PR each)**
- [ ] **16. The sessions ahead (Python).** A pure function takes an arm's date-based gates (VIX expiry, FOMC, half days, earnings, holidays) plus `core.calendar`, `core.events` and `core.symbolevents`, and lists the next 10 sessions with what each gate will do there.
  - A value gate, such as a VIX level, reads "depends on VIX at the time".
  - It adds the arm's own record on that kind of day, only where a regime cut has enough sessions.
  - The arm page's forward section renders it.
- [ ] **17. Today's conditions in the arm's record.** Each module's regime-cuts writer also stores today's bucket per dimension. The arm page shows the arm's net in today's bucket, only for cuts that are not underpowered.
- [ ] **18. The scorecard.** A first tab on Compare with one row per arm and automation in the suite: research verdict, the vs-backtest reading, the vs-control reading, sample progress, net, drawdown, and the reason it needs a look. Built on `core.compare` and the references from phases 1 and 3.
