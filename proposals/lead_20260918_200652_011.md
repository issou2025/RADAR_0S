## What is wrong with docking today

Admission is decided by `ASSGameMode::Tick`: the ship must be within 1200 cm of the dock, with `Dot(forward, toDock) > .45`, and `ASSStation::CanAssistDocking` must find a clear swept path. Those rules encode **"come in level, centred, through the hangar mouth"** — a corridor approach. They are automatic, not player-initiated, and they are tied to one station rather than to a pad.

The whole automation suite around it (`SpaceSurvival.Integration.DockingAdmission`) pins that model with eight reject cases — roof dive, rear wall, side entry, lane edge, below deck, outside the 12 m band, facing away, aimed-but-not-inbound. On an open pad about half of those stop meaning anything: a vertical descent onto a landing pad is a landing, not an illegal roof dive. **That suite has to be re-derived, not patched**, which is why the current change deliberately left admission alone.

## Target sequence

1. **Proximity, not corridor.** Near a pad, from any direction or attitude → a prompt appears.
2. **Player presses a button** to engage. Docking is never automatic.
3. **Slow down** to a hover above the pad.
4. **Lower onto the pad** while the landing gear opens.
5. **On contact the back door opens.**
6. **The character stands up**, and the player has control to walk out of the ship.

Launch is the same sequence in reverse, and both must be identical at every pad in the game, now and later.

## What already exists to build on

- `Landing_On` (2.067 s, 31 bones) drops the gear **and opens the rear ramp in one motion** — `Cargo_Door_Bone` swings 83.7° alongside `Foot_Bone` at 90.3°. Steps 4 and 5 are already authored. `Landing_Off` is its mirror for launch.
- `FSSHullDefinition` already carries `LandingDeployClipPath` and `LandingStowClipPath`.
- `ASSShip::HoldBody` stops the physics body for a scripted move, so the same landing motion drives both the kinematic classic hull and the simulating Phoenix.
- `ASSStation` now has a measured pad with `PadDockPosition()`, `PadWalkSpawn()` and a `WalkableLocal` envelope.

## The architectural ask

"A landing pad anywhere in the game, ever" means the pad cannot stay a set of constants on `ASSStation`. It needs to become a **thing** — a pad actor or component carrying its own dock transform, approach volume and services — with one dock routine that takes a pad rather than a station. `ASSStation` then owns *a* pad instead of *being* the pad.

## Not ShipCore's autopilot

`UAutopilotManagerComp::DockAtTransform` flies a straight line and judges completion on the actor origin within 30 cm and 30 cm/s, with **no timeout and no abort** — a miss leaves it stuck in DOCKING forever. It has no landing concept at all: no ground trace, no contact, no settle, no gear hook, no attach. It disables its own tick on arrival, so it cannot hold a ship on a pad. It also ships an ungated per-frame on-screen debug message. Its `ComputeSafeApproachSpeed_V2` is worth borrowing for step 3; the rest is not.

## Blocked sub-step

Step 6's "walk out of ship" needs interior collision, which does not exist — see the walk-aboard issue. Until then the hero can be set down beside the ship on the pad.
