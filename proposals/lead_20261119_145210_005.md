## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Heron cannot save the family in front - a save-helper model is needed**

*Project description:*
**Seen:** 2026-11-19, Revit 2020, building two fixed-size families in the Family Editor through `revit_change`.

**What happened:** Heron cannot save the family document that is in front while it works in it (SAVE_DOCUMENT refuses the pinned document). To save after each group, the agent had to make an empty scratch family (`save-helper.rfa` in `%TEMP%\heron-work`), then ACTIVATE it, pin it with `revit_use_this_model`, SAVE_DOCUMENT the real family from there, ACTIVATE the family again, and pin it again. That is 5 calls per save, plus an extra document the modeller sees open in Revit and asks about.

**Expected:** one call saves the family in front, or Heron keeps its own hidden helper and the modeller never sees an extra model.
