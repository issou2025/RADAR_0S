## Why it cannot start

**R4 (#71)** settled that the **user selects** the tie topology and §6.1
**validates** it rather than generating it. What the control *is* remains open:

> **Q10 (owner, 2026-09-14):** *"for stirrups shape it is very hard to give you
> one final answer."*

There is no closed catalogue of tie shapes, and there may never be one.
Hardcoding a list of Figure 13-3 sections would be inventing one — exactly
what `CONTEXT.md` forbids.

## Q11 — the actual question

- **(a) Named template picker.** A catalogue of typical sections. Familiar,
  but the catalogue does not exist and cannot be completed.
- **(b) Direct subset entry.** The user states subsets in §6.2's own terms —
  initial bar index + count of enclosed bars — and §6.1 plus **A1** validate
  whatever they build. Needs no catalogue, cannot go stale, asks more of the
  user.

## What is NOT blocked by this

Everything downstream is shape-agnostic and can be built first: §6.1's tier
validator, **A1**'s buildability test, subset→rectangle geometry, the
per-level mirror map, the placer.

## What the control must enforce once it exists

- **A1:** a closed loop where buildable, a cross-tie **only** where the bend
  fails (`narrow dimension >= tie bend diameter + tie diameter`, bend diameter
  read from the `RebarBarType`). Tested **before** the API call — Revit's
  refusal is a modal dialog that blocks the whole session, not a catchable
  exception.
- **§6.1 tiers** as a validator: `x <= 150` alternate · `150 < x <= 250` every
  bar tied · never more than 300 mm between tie branches.
- ⚠️ **Q13:** `x` is computed from bar positions, but per **R15** corner bars
  sit ~3.5 mm inboard of their idealised coordinates once a tie exists. Whether
  §6.1 validates idealised or as-built positions is unresolved, and it changes
  which bars the tool says need restraint.

Do not implement either option until Q11 is answered.
