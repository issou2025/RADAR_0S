## 4. Tip jar

[Tip jar…](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1534) · [In-app tips research](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1533) and its [verification](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1533#issuecomment-6704406619), which **overrides** the research note where they differ.

**Products:**

| Name | Base price (Germany) | Type | **(proposal)** ID, same on both stores |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small tip | €1.99 | Consumable / one-time, unlocks nothing | `tip_small` |
| Medium tip | €4.99 | Consumable / one-time, unlocks nothing | `tip_medium` |
| Large tip | €9.99 | Consumable / one-time, unlocks nothing | `tip_large` |

Other storefronts use the stores' generated prices. Show only the store-formatted `ProductDetails.price`, never a hard-coded amount. (The research note adds: product IDs can't be reused on either store, even after deletion — pick them once.)

**Wording:** "tip" everywhere — row, products, store metadata, review notes — never "donate" (App Review 3.1.1 permits tips; 3.2.2(iv) bars in-app fundraising for non-nonprofits).

**States (decided):**
- **Loading:** a 20 px spinner where the chips go.
- **Failed** (no network, the store returned nothing, or a build without store access — develop flavour, sideloaded or GitHub APK, TestFlight without sandbox setup): the chips are replaced by "Couldn't load tips. Tips work in the App Store and Google Play versions of the app." and a **Retry** button. The rest of the page is unaffected.
- **Purchased:** dialog "Thank you!" / "Your {tipName} ({price}) helps keep OpenNutriTracker free." with OK. Then a quiet line under the chips, "You left a tip — thank you!". The chips stay.

**States (proposal):** *pending* (Play) — say the payment is waiting and don't thank yet; *error* — a short message; *canceled* — nothing; *partial load* — show only the tips that loaded.

**After a tip:** the "you left a tip" line is on-device only, cleared by Settings → *Reset profile data* (`settingsDeleteAllDataLabel`, `DeleteAllUserDataUsecase`), never sent anywhere. It must stay **text only** — any badge, theme or icon would be an unlock under 3.1.1 and change the product type. Note: `variant_a.dart` draws a heart icon before this line; drop it.
- **(proposal)** Store the flag as a new `@HiveField` on `ConfigDBO` in the global `AppConfigBox` (a tip belongs to the device, not a profile), regenerate with `just build` and commit `config_dbo.g.dart`. `DeleteAllUserDataUsecase.deleteAll()` leaves that box alone by design, so reset the flag there explicitly (like `_aiCredentials.clearAll()`), with a unit test.

**Code — decided:**
- [ ] Add `in_app_purchase` and make sure the lock file resolves **`in_app_purchase_storekit` ≥ 0.4.17** (iOS 27 fix; `in_app_purchase` 3.3.1 only asks for `^0.4.0`). `in_app_purchase_android` 0.5.5 needs Flutter ≥ 3.44 — the pinned 3.44.6 is fine.
- [ ] **Android:** consume any tip in the purchase-stream handler, **and** at startup call `restorePurchases()` and consume every tip that comes back. The plugin's auto-consume only remembers purchases from the current process, so a tip that completes after a restart is otherwise never consumed (and is refunded after three days).
- [ ] No Restore button and no receipt validation — consumables aren't restorable and nothing is unlocked.
- [ ] Keep purchase details out of `logging` and Sentry.

**Code — guidance from the research note:** subscribe to `purchaseStream` at app start (so unfinished transactions from an earlier session are handled); `buyConsumable` with the default `autoConsume: true`; always `completePurchase` when `pendingCompletePurchase` is true; sort tips by `rawPrice`.

## 6. Privacy and README

- [ ] **App Store privacy label:** no change, as long as nothing about a purchase leaves the device through the app.
- [ ] **Play Data safety:** **don't answer "no change" yet.** Google lists "uses Google Play's Billing Library" as a sign of Financial-info collection, and Billing 9.1.0 pulls in `datatransport`, `play-services-base` and `play-services-location`. Do a network capture of a release build first, then update `docs/play-data-safety.md`.
- [ ] Check the Android merged manifest after adding the plugin: expect `com.android.vending.BILLING`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, billing `<queries>`, `ProxyBillingActivity`, a datatransport JobService and receiver, `GoogleApiActivity`.
- [ ] **README (decided):** add `BILLING` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` to the permissions line (README.md:177); re-check "zero advertising or analytics SDKs" (README.md:84) against the capture; add one line to the contributing/support section pointing to Settings → About → Support OpenNutriTracker ([#1535](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1535)).
- [ ] **README (proposal):** add tips to "What leaves your device" (the payment goes to Apple or Google; the app sees only the product ID, status and a token it doesn't store) and update that section's lead-in ("the last four only if you turn them on", README.md:129); re-check "No location, contacts, or microphone access" (README.md:177) given `play-services-location`, and "no analytics" (README.md:127).
