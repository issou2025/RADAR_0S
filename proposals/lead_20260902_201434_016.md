## Problem

The Settings page explains the product using internal implementation terms such as “harness,” “worker,” “dispatched webhook session,” “pinned,” “environment variable,” and “in-flight.” This makes routine actions harder to understand for non-developers and obscures the consequences of disruptive controls.

The **Update box** card also says the action “rebuilds the system.” On a distro deployment, the documented behavior is a Nix profile swap followed by configuration rendering and affected service restarts—not a system rebuild.
