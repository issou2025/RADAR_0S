### Stage B — Implement reusable entitlement and purchase framework (after Stage A approval)

**Architecture**
- One Flutter app; a central typed **Entitlement Manager** resolves Free baseline + verified non-expiring purchases + active subscriptions. No separate Free/Pro app binaries.
- Central product catalog with stable feature IDs and explicit product-to-capability mappings; support Pro, separately owned add-ons, and future connected services without scattered `isPro` flags.
- Guard paid features at the **application/use-case boundary**, with consistent presentation/upsell states; never rely on hiding UI alone.
- Independent storage for verified license records / secure proof vs local finance database; do **not** transfer a license via financial backup/restore or user-owned cloud synchronization.

**Store purchase and entitlement lifecycle**
- Apple StoreKit for iPhone/iPad/macOS App Store distribution; Google Play Billing for supported Android release; future direct Mac distribution is a separate decision.
- Free requires no Butlerly account. For Apple/Google store purchases, leverage purchase history/restore, not collection of names, emails, card numbers or personal finance data.
- Multiple compatible Apple devices using the same purchasing Apple Account must restore Pro without paying per device, subject to product/store availability. Distinguish purchase portability from financial-data synchronization.
- Verify transactions/receipts/token status through suitable platform SDK or secure backend. Google Play verification generally requires a secure backend for robust handling. Treat cancelled, pending, unverified, refunded, revoked and failed purchases correctly.
- Cache verifiable permanent entitlements for offline access; restore on reinstall/new device when online. Define a bounded, verified subscription grace/expiry policy. Subscription expiry must not disable Pro, erase data or block Free operations.
- Design for idempotent purchase events, acknowledgement, restore retry, multi-device refresh and fraud/replay resistance.
- Do not promise cross-store entitlement portability unless we separately implement secure identity/license bridging and approve related legal terms.

**UI**
- Settings/More → **Plans & Purchases** displaying Free/Pro/owned add-ons, price from store, product details, Restore Purchases, active subscriptions and Manage Subscription, pending/error/offline states.
- Use existing design system and English/Simplified Chinese/Spanish localization; mobile/tablet/desktop responsiveness and accessibility.
- Show clear one-time vs recurring language; publish current price, cancellation terms and feature list.

**Legal, privacy and release**
- Update LEG-0001 Terms of Use, LEG-0002 Privacy Policy, LEG-0003 Software License/Third-Party Notices and LEG-0004 AI Disclosures according to **actually launched** services, with legal review before billing.
- Preserve existing Free rights, published supported-version maintenance policy and non-expiring purchased offline capabilities. Future modules may be separately sold only if genuinely distinct.
- Purchase receipts/identifiers and any optional service account should be minimal and separate from financial data. No Butlerly account needed for the store-specific Pro baseline.
- Build/test purchase state simulation first; then implement sandbox purchases. Do not activate production payments, subscriptions or cloud services without explicit approval.

**Deliverable B:** A technical ADR, stable catalog and entitlement schema/API, purchase adapters, Plans & Purchases UI, complete tests, and a draft implementation PR. Defer actual subscriptions/add-on sales until their features and provider economics are ready.

## Advanced feature deferred — Cross-platform identity and universal licensing

**Product decision (2026-10-29): DEFER for a later advanced-feature phase.** A universal Butlerly ID, Apple/Google account linking, cross-platform recognition/portability of purchased Pro licenses, and the associated identity/licensing backend are **out of scope for the initial Free/Pro purchase framework**. Do not implement them as part of the next post-Survey monetization milestone.

Initial licensing should remain **store-specific**: Apple App Store purchases restored through Apple's supported purchase system across compatible Apple devices; Google Play purchases restored through Google Play on supported Android devices. **Do not assume or promise that an Apple purchase activates Pro on Android, or vice versa.** Maintain one extensible, capability-based Entitlement Manager so optional cross-platform identity and licensing can be evaluated later without redesigning feature gates. The user has not authorized implementation of the advanced feature.

Keep the proposed user-owned encrypted sync workspace/device pairing concept distinct from identity for purchases (see #375); neither establishes cross-store purchase ownership.


### Exact same-store vs cross-store boundary

- **Apple → Apple (iPhone, iPad, compatible Mac App Store releases):** Apple's verified StoreKit entitlements / Restore Purchases. No QR or Butlerly user identity; same purchasing Apple account, supported App Store availability.
- **Google → Google (Android phones/tablets):** Google's verified Play Billing purchase records / restoration using the purchasing Play account. No QR or Butlerly user identity.
- **Apple → Google or Google → Apple:** separately deferred cross-ecosystem license recognition, with optional QR-assisted pairing and appropriate verified purchase evidence. No cross-store sharing is promised in V1.
- QR is **not** a portable bearer receipt that simply unlocks Pro. The issuing side proves original purchase, a short-lived challenge pairs the destination, and the licensing service verifies and issues a scoped device entitlement; platform billing-policy review is mandatory.
- **Explicit exclusion:** this is a licensing feature, not a financial-data synchronization or cloud-storage feature.

## Future advanced design option — QR-assisted portable license pairing

**Clarified product scope October 29, 2026 — deferred, NOT approved for implementation.** A user who purchases Pro on **Apple** may wish to activate it on **Google Play / Android**, or vice versa, by pairing the two ecosystems with a QR-assisted license-verification workflow **without a conventional Butlerly account**. **Only Apple ↔ Google portability requires this advanced mechanism.** Purchases among devices within the same Apple account must use StoreKit restore/current entitlements; purchases among devices within the same purchasing Google Play account must use Google Play Billing purchase retrieval. Never make users scan QR codes for same-store device installation/restore.

- Do not put an unsigned `paid=true` flag or a raw Apple/Google purchase token into the QR; a copied/forged QR cannot be considered proof of ownership.
- Candidate: purchase provider verification → minimal Butlerly licensing service issues a short-lived, one-time, challenge-bound pairing token in QR → destination device registers its public key and retrieves a signed device-bound entitlement → central issuer handles revocation/refunds/device limits. Backend would need only pseudonymous license, purchase, redemption and device identifiers, **not financial transactions**.
- A signed QR credential without redemption control is still transferable/replayable by anyone who copies it. Device pairing, activation counts, expiry, recovery and anti-replay require explicit security design.
- **App Store compliance risk:** Apple App Review Guideline 3.1.1 explicitly restricts apps using their own mechanisms such as QR codes to unlock functionality. Guideline 3.1.3(b) permits qualifying multiplatform services to recognize features acquired on other platforms when also offered as IAP. Therefore **QR as a direct standalone iOS unlock is not an approved design**. Any QR-assisted account/device linking and subsequent verified entitlement authorization would require platform-specific policy review before delivery. See https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ .
- Google Play purchases within the Play-distributed app must respect applicable Play Billing policy; purchase-token verification should use Google Play Developer API: https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security .
- Cross-platform portability, recovery after losing the source device, family/device-sharing limits, refunds, and license transfer rights remain product decisions. QR proves possession/authorization, not automatically the buyer's identity.
- Preserve simpler store-specific purchase/restore approach for initial commercial milestone. **Do not start QR activation implementation or promise universal purchase portability until explicitly authorized.**

- **Separately priced Family Pro plan:** advanced backlog [#378](https://github.com/Jack84283/Butlerly/issues/378) covers Apple-only, Android-only and mixed-account households. **Individual Pro and Family Pro must have distinct paid SKUs and different prices; exact Family pricing TBD.** Apple-native Family Sharing should apply to the eligible Family SKU only, not Individual; Google Play Family Library does not share in-app purchases. Do not implement family entitlements or enable live Family billing in the initial purchase framework.

## Product proposal — Cross-store access belongs to Family Pro

**Packaging proposal:** Cross-store purchase recognition (Apple ↔ Android), **even for the same person**, should be an entitlement of the separately priced **Family Pro** plan in [#378](https://github.com/Jack84283/Butlerly/issues/378), rather than a separate Universal Individual Pro license. Initial Individual Pro remains strictly store-specific; same Apple account and same Google Play purchasing account each rely on their native storefront for multi-device restoration. Family Pro may provide additional same-ecosystem family sharing and cross-platform pairing, subject to explicit scope, price, seat policy, security and App Store/Google Play compliance approval. Do not implement universal licensing in initial M1/M2 or assume that the QR alone authorizes an App Store feature.
