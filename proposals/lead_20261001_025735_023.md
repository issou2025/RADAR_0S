# Project Proposal — Unknown

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Fresh install without myhome.yaml: lights are never queried, and discovery follow-ups cut status replies short**

*Project description:*
**Environment:** MH201 (firmware 3.6.27, OPEN password), Home Assistant 2026.9.2, MyHOME 2.0.0b14, OWNd 2.0.0b9.

On my MH201 test box I removed the integration, disabled `myhome.yaml`, cleared the deleted-entity records and added the gateway again, to see what a new user gets from discovery alone. After setup: 10 of 24 WHO 1 addresses and 4 of 7 covers.

### 1. `*#1*0##` is never sent
`initial_discovery()` only asks WHO 2, 4 and 16. `sweep_bus` doesn't send `*#1*0##` either, although its description says it covers lighting. So lights only appear once something else makes them talk.

The request was removed in e7c209f (#242) because an F454 NACKed it and the NACK was logged as ERROR. On the MH201, `*#1*0##` sent through `myhome.send_message` is accepted and returns the state of all 24 WHO 1 addresses in about 2 s. @xtimmy86x pointed out in #242 that it is listed in the Legrand documentation. Since #406/#407, a NACK on a startup status request is no longer an ERROR, so the reason for dropping it is gone.

Proposal: send `*#1*0##` again in `initial_discovery()` (gated by the profile like the others) and in `sweep_bus`.

### 2. Follow-up status requests interrupt the general reply
Every entity created by discovery immediately queues its own status request (`*#1*16##`, `*#2*0112##`, …) while the gateway is still streaming the reply to the general request. The MH201 has a single command session, and replies get lost:

- first `*#1*0##`, with many unknown addresses: 19 of 24 lights came back;
- second `*#1*0##` a few minutes later, nothing new to discover: 24 of 24;
- the 5 missing lights (02, 04, 06, 09, 0013) answered normally when asked one by one.

The frame that creates the entity already carries its state, so the follow-up request adds nothing when the frame is itself a status reply. Proposal: skip it in that case, or hold it until the general request is acknowledged.

Side note: the MH201 mixes subsystems in these replies. `*#2*0##` also returned WHO 1 states, and `*#1*0##` returned WHO 2 states.

### Steps to reproduce
1. Remove the MyHOME integration and rename `myhome.yaml`.
2. Add the integration again.
3. Count the entities: lights are missing, some covers too.
4. `myhome.send_message` with `*#2*0##`, then `*#1*0##`: most devices appear, 5 lights still missing.
5. Ask the 5 missing ones one by one (`*#1*02##`, …): they answer.
6. `*#1*0##` again: all 24 answer.
