# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**volume_readings: a round bar cut at a slant has elliptical end faces, which the shell reading leaves as another reason**

*Project description:*
## What

`tools/re/volume_readings.py` reads a shell as explained when each round end face of rvt-rs's (at least 12 vertices on one radius, `circle_of`) has a polygon of Revit's inscribed in it. A round bar cut at a slant, a mitred pipe end, has an elliptical end face, so its shell has "no round end face" and reads `other`, though Revit's export draws it as the same inscribed polygon prism.

## Measured

Snowdon Towers Architectural, RE-426 (Measure 43002827683, head `ea1f01fd`): of the 145 shells of the 22 railings outside 1% that the scorer does not read, 72 give "no round end face in rvt-rs's shell". On railing 622056 they are 6 bars 3.09 ft long cut at a slant at both ends: 130 vertices and 236 triangles in rvt-rs's mesh against 8 and 12 in Revit's, volume ratio 1.4970. All 8 of Revit's vertices are vertices of rvt-rs's mesh (distance to the nearest 0). The end faces of these bars also come out of `caps()` as 4 to 8 caps of 5 to 12 vertices, not one cap each; the cause is not found.

## Done when

- A cap whose vertices lie on an ellipse in one plane (within `CIRCLE`) is read by mapping it, and Revit's cap, to the circle of the ellipse's minor semi-axis (an affine map of the plane scales both areas alike), then as a circle is read now.
- The 6 bars of 622056 read explained, and no body that reads `other` today reads `explained` without a polygon of Revit's inscribed in the mapped circle, so a body that is wrong still reads wrong.
- Measure's `shell readings:` line before and after, on the 2024 samples and the BIM4LCA files, is given on the PR.
