# Project Proposal — Quantity Takeoff

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Retire Graphite from the review stack after cancellation and refund**

*Project description:*
## Background
Graphite's AI review was a supplemental reviewer on the CodeRabbit path, alongside CodeAnt. Across 620 PRs (Aug 21 – Nov 1, 2026) it reviewed 30, versus 484–620 for CodeAnt, CodeRabbit and BugBot. On 2026-11-03 Graphite support (ticket T-J15030) cancelled the Team plan effective immediately and issued a pro-rated $450 refund. Graphite is no longer part of the review stack.

## Problem
The rules, reference docs, skills and scripts still treat Graphite as a live supplemental reviewer: they name `graphite-app[bot]` among the bots to poll and reply to, describe the CodeAnt + Graphite supplemental pair, list Graphite in the pricing and paid-levers docs, and carry Graphite-specific handling in review scripts. That costs auto-loaded rule budget, sends agents looking for a reviewer that will never answer, and leaves the cost docs wrong about what we pay for.

## Proposed solution
Graphite is gone from the review workflow everywhere it is described or handled. Agents stop polling, triggering, or waiting for it; the supplemental reviewer on the CR path is CodeAnt alone; the pricing docs record that Graphite was cancelled and refunded, and why. History stays readable — decision records keep a dated "retired" note instead of being rewritten.
