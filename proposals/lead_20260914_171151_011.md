## Purpose

v3.5-en (PR #6, branch `prompt-budget-lint`) refactored the skill around the economy doctrine and the prompting-technique menu (A Caption Spine · B block structure · C Master Style Block). The rules are now tight, but most copy-ready instances are synthetic (the LIGHTCAT set, the rain-alley woman in ch. 21). This issue collects **verified, published example prompts** from vendor docs, platform guides, practitioners, social media and blogs. Each entry has its source, the reason it was selected, and the place in the skill it is meant for.

**Research only — nothing is implemented.** Every candidate still has to pass the skill's own lint before it goes in, and many entries say what must change. The doctrine findings below should be settled before any examples are added.

## Method and selection bar

Research ran on 2026-09-14 in eleven parallel passes. Seven covered vendor and platform material by topic; four covered X, Reddit, YouTube/LinkedIn and blogs/newsletters. Every quoted prompt comes from a page that was actually fetched. Nothing is paraphrased and presented as verbatim.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Vendor-official, with the generated result published **next to the exact prompt** (docs, guide, cookbook, gallery, official blog/project page) |
| **Tier 2** | Platform-official for a third-party model (fal, Higgsfield for Seedance, Runware, Replicate), **or** a practitioner who published prompt and result together |
| **Tier 3** | The same prompt or prompt shape reproduced by ≥ 2 independent sources with results |
| *doc-only* | Official wording with no rendered output. Usable as 🟢 syntax, 🟡 result |

**Rejected:** SEO "best prompts" lists without results; prompts with no visible output; gated prompts (Skool, Patreon, "comment PROMPT"); aggregators re-hosting other people's posts; anything not actually fetched.

**Caveats:**
- The clips were **not** watched frame by frame. "Verified" means the result is published next to the prompt (checked in the page HTML), not that it was judged against the skill's six review passes.
- Several Runway GIF filenames differ from the displayed prompt (see D8/D10).
- Higgsfield's short-film blog media did not load in one scrape (E13).

Each entry carries one **Fit** verdict:
- **conforms** — passes the v3.5 lint as is, or with trivial trims
- **needs adaptation** — the entry says what to change
- **counter-example** — valuable because it shows a failure class the skill warns about

## ⚠️ Doctrine findings raised by the research (settle before adding examples)

1. **The Seedance bracket grammar in 12b contradicts ByteDance's own table.** The official `sd25-pe` prompt skill v0.1.1 ("Sound, Dialogue, and Text") and the ModelArk Seedance 2.0 guide ("Special character standards") both define `()` = **music**, `<>` = **sound effect**, `{}` = **dialogue**, `【】` = **subtitle**. Neither has a delivery bracket. The skill uses `( )` = spoken line, `< >` = delivery, `{ }` = SFX, `【 】` = ambience **and the no-music exclusion**. Under the vendor table, `【no music, no BGM…】` sits in the *subtitle* bracket, lines in `( )` read as music, and SFX in `{ }` read as dialogue. This was verified in the downloaded sd25-pe file and the 2.0 guide text (A14). Two practitioner guides give the same vendor mapping: Theoretically Media (SY3 block) and OpenArt's Seedance 2.5 guide (S3 note). A viral 2.5 dialogue prompt puts its lines in `{ }` (SX3). The skill's grammar may come from a Higgsfield/CS source, so trace its origin, then decide per surface. This touches 12b, the ch. 12 filled example, worked-example §3 and the post-audio-legal P32 note.
2. **The quoted "official worked example" for time-scoped edits was not found.** "Only from 4–7 seconds, change the cool blue light on the right wall to warm orange" (ch. 14 edit format) is in none of the current official sources: the 2.5 guide, 2.5 tutorial, 2.0 guide or sd25-pe. The current official form is doc-only, "…from 4-6 seconds in Video 1, and leave the rest of the content unchanged". The only time-scoped edit found with a shown result is Runware's (Tier 2). See A8 and the note under A14.
3. **Veo text/subtitle leak: Google's documented fix is syntax, not a negative keyword.** Put a colon after the speaker and no quotation marks (B5). ch. 21 currently makes `subtitles` in the negative field the default entry. Also: the negative field exists **only on Vertex**, not in the Gemini API Veo 3.1 parameters (B6). Google's own pages contradict each other on quotation marks (B2/B4/B7 use them).
4. **Kling 3.0 Omni puts `@Element` names in the prompt body** in its official examples (B14). ch. 21 says "no @tags in the body". Split the profile into VIDEO 3.0 and 3.0 Omni.
5. **"Grok Imagine: style qualifier first" (12g, ch. 21) is not supported by xAI's own examples.** They all open with camera or subject (D14). The rule's source is [W], so re-verify it.
6. **Cully Hill Boys / Hell Grind briefs are public.** sources.md P24 says "requires login". The pages render with JavaScript but need no login, and they hold the original wording of the height ruler, off-screen lock, speech-count lock and phonetic accent (C1–C3). The skill's `"Pull it, Oli."` example appears to derive from Cully scene 102F, so make the attribution explicit.
7. **13b quotes an "official zero-motion / rack-focus block" as one text.** At the Higgsfield Academy these are **two separate entries** (C4).
8. **Casting collective ranges do bind for extras.** ByteDance's shown-output concert maps `@Images 6 to 10` to the orchestra (A3). ch. 14 says collective mappings "bind nothing". Refine to: individual mapping for characters, ranges admissible for crowds.
9. **Extension ceiling.** ByteDance claims "multiple rounds" to "multi-minute" length. The skill's ≈60-s chain ceiling is stricter, so label it as house caution. Every shown-output extension uses **no** boundary-frame wording; that wording exists only as sd25-pe templates (A10).
10. **Vendor corroboration of the economy doctrine**, independent of [PP 2026-09-14]:
    - Runway: over-specified multi-paragraph prompts "paradoxically lead to unexpected or unnatural results"; JSON prompts are a "placebo" (D7/D10).
    - Vertex Veo: "Prompt for motion only… Not recommended: Re-describe the character" (B9).
    - sd25-pe: "Do Not Add Unrequested Constraints".
    - Higgsfield: "Genre and lighting settings do more work than most of the prompt text itself" (C9).
    - Kling: don't set a voice tone again that the element already binds (B12).
11. **Seedance 2.0 timing:** the 2.5 guide states that "Seedance 2.0 does not respond to timestamps and only responds to shot numbers". The skill prompts 2.0 and 2.5 identically ("Seedance 2.0 = 2.5"), which this contradicts.
12. **Speech-count lock, lower bound.** Replicate's Veo 3 tests show too few words for the clip length produce "AI gibberish" filler (G6). The lock needs a minimum as well as the exact count.
13. **New failure classes, each seen with a result, that the skill doesn't name yet.** Candidates for renderability §2/§3, ch. 14/14b and post-audio-legal ch. 19:
    - **First/last-frame dissolve:** pinning two frames with a slow move yields a cross-fade. Fix: write camera travel and parallax that cover the distance between the frames (SX1, corroborated by SR5, SB8).
    - **Start-image re-staging:** the model "infers a before" and replays how the start frame came about. Fix: declare that the visible state has *already happened* (SX1).
    - **Object class drifting across hard cuts:** a closed-cockpit racer becomes an open-wheel car. Fix: restate the class in every beat (SR7). Same mechanism as Kling's "no hidden state between shots" (SB9).
    - **Face flush at the emotional peak on Seedance 2.5** (SR12).
    - **Characters walking backwards or sideways** (SX10).
    - **A name written inside a reference image gets printed into the frame** (SB4). Supports the existing "no mapping labels inside reference images".
    - **GPT Image 2.5 quietly declines an angle it was never shown; Nano Banana 2 invents one** (SB11). Fix: add a reference, not words.
    - **Aleph edits drift on logos and letterforms without a first-frame anchor** (SB10).
    - **Effect before cause:** a flame lights before the lighter clicks (SR8).
14. **Evidence to re-measure.** Higgsfield's official X post runs percent coordinates on GPT Image 2.5 (SX12). style-control §2 [PP] says canvas fractions fail silently, but that was measured on `gpt-image-2`. Runware shows Flare vs. Sunburst edit locality with results (SB12), which is the first shown evidence for a claim the skill still labels 🔴.
15. **Reproduced prompts (Tier 3) are all long, over-instructed templates** (SX6 ≈900 words, SX7 ≈1,250 words, SX15). They spread because they are shareable, not because they are economical. When they are used, they belong in the skill as the counter-example next to a trimmed version, never as the default.

## Gaps (no verifiable example found)

- **"Exactly N words" count lock** with a shown result from any vendor (closest: Cully "three words", Higgsfield "exactly two scripted spoken lines", Seedance 1.5 "remains silent").

## Rejected sources (summary; per-block lists in the comments)

- SEO "best prompts" lists and prompt farms without results (atlabs, invideo, krea "10 best", queststudio, evolink, youmind, seedance2prompt …).
- Aggregators that re-host other people's clips.
- Gated prompts: Skool, Patreon, Gumroad PDFs, "comment WORD for the prompt".
- Pages whose outputs could not be tied to the prompt: ChatGPT Images 2.5 announcement, OpenArt Seedance 2.5 guide, Grok docs demo players.
- Posts where the prompt was promised but never published.
- Coordinated sponsored campaigns.
- Posts relying on real-person likeness or IP characters. Where a *mechanism* is still worth keeping (SX5, SR1, SR2), the entry says so and the characters must not be reused.
