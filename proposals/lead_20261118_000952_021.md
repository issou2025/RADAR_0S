# SFP AI Production Agent — Master Brief v2 (17 November 2026)

## Owner direction
Build a **cloud-first, script-first, eventually fully autonomous video production agent** for Stay Focused Productions. The owner directs and approves. Formats: long-form documentaries, music videos, public interviews, event behind-the-scenes, YouTube, promos, podcasts, Shorts. Later support footage-first documentary storytelling. Script-first means format-appropriate creative plans, not necessarily spoken dialogue.

## End-to-end production
1. Accept brief, topic, script, music, raw footage, optional B-roll, separate audio, brand assets, references.
2. Research with source tracking, verify factual claims, create treatment, script/visual treatment, interview questions, storyboards, shot lists.
3. Ingest and preserve originals; proxy, index, transcribe, diarise where feasible, analyse scenes and footage.
4. Match real footage to storyboard/shot plan; identify missing shots.
5. Where authorised, generate original images, B-roll, animation, illustrative reconstructions, graphics and VFX. Prefer authentic footage, label synthetic documentary material appropriately, record provenance and rights, and never pass generated footage off as documentary evidence.
6. Produce source-linked paper edit and structured, reproducible timeline; maintain timecode mapping and edit decisions.
7. Rough/fine cut, dialogue cleanup, licensed music, captions, lower thirds, colour, audio mix, graphics, export.
8. Automated technical and editorial checks; provide preview and timestamped feedback loop; owner approval required before final delivery/publication.

## Proposed modular architecture (evaluate, don't assume)
Creative director / production brief parser; research and evidence; script/treatment; shot planning; media ingest and analysis; editorial planning; FFmpeg execution; optional Remotion; AI visual generation adapters; QC; persistent jobs, storage and owner review. Codex is a development tool, not assumed to be the permanent video rendering service.

## Constraints
- Samsung Galaxy tablet control, cloud-first, minimal cost; no paid API or service without explicit approval.
- Keep project separate from Yard Crew.
- Preserve source footage and protected branches; no main-branch changes, merges, pushes or PRs without approval.
- No real client media in early testing; synthetic or expressly authorised footage only.
- Never fabricate interview statements, source citations or documentary evidence.
- Track model outputs, source timecodes, generation provenance, commercial rights and licensing.
- Require human sign-off on research/script, rough cut and final publication where relevant.
- Review security of third-party dependencies, privacy and licensing before integration.
