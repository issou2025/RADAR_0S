## 1. Naming — recommendation: **HERALD**

"Marketing Agent" is generic (every SaaS has one), implies autonomy over messaging (the opposite of this system's doctrine), and is invisible in a crowded namespace. The name should encode the role's defining property: **it speaks loudly and decides nothing.**

**Recommended: HERALD** (German: *Herold*). Reasons:

1. **Doctrinally exact.** A herald historically carries the sovereign's message in the sovereign's name, with zero personal authority. That is precisely the boundary this seat needs: public contribution ≠ canonical authority, execution success ≠ compliance, publication ≠ approval.
2. **Pairs with STAR-T-GATE.** The system's front door is a *gate* (inbound); its public voice is a *herald* (outbound). Symmetric, memorable, in the system's own naming spirit (USG, ARPL, ZEN, STAR-T-GATE).
3. **Fits the seat taxonomy.** The system already uses role words, not tool words: *curator*, *observer*. *Herald* joins the family naturally and stays model-independent.
4. **Optional backronym** if the acronym style is preferred: **H**onest **E**vidence-**R**eporting with **A**uthority-**L**imited **D**isclosure — which is the doctrine in five words.

**Alternatives considered:**

| Name | Appeal | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| VOX | Short like ZEN; "the system's voice" | Implies speaking *as* the system; collides with a major media brand |
| BEACON | Top-of-funnel signal guiding the right audience in | Generic; crowded namespace in construction tech |
| AMBASSADOR | Represents without deciding | Diplomatic/corporate connotation; long |
| RELAY | Carries messages faithfully | Too passive; doesn't imply the judgment the role needs |
| MARKETING AGENT | Says what it is | Says nothing about its limits; invites autonomy assumptions |

## 2. The core doctrine: claims are evidence-bounded

This is the single most important design decision, and it is what makes the seat *TIVERIT*: every public claim must trace to a verifiable governed artifact (object id + version, curation record, ledger entry, release record), and claim strength is bounded by the **weakest evidence state in the system** (network review N-05).

The Herald must therefore maintain a **Claim Ledger** — a versioned inventory of every public claim: the claim text, its evidence source, the current evidence state, the gates that must close before it may be used, and its ready/not-ready status. Claims decay: when evidence changes (a pack is reproduced, a release is published), the ledger updates and old claim wording retires.

## 4. What the Herald actually does

### Positioning (from PM Report 001, unchanged)
**ZEN is the evidence engine of a governed open system** — not "another AI tool." The repositories are the proof of work; the Herald makes that work legible to the outside world.

### Content pipeline — the system already writes its own marketing
The Herald should rarely invent content; it **curates true stories the system produces anyway**:

- **ARPL**: the 89 mm stud story; the crash-forensics incident; "we keep failed attempts on purpose" — the most distinctive content in the ecosystem
- **USG**: "Need first, geometry last"; the mutation-test discipline; Building-002 sketch → concept-model loop (the network's one proven working interface)
- **STANDARDS**: honest changelog entries; "we don't claim authority we haven't released"; the no-wildcard forward-declaration discipline
- **BPL**: a weekly "from the observatory" column — audits, network seam repairs, horizon notes

### Launch sequence (gated)
1. **Gate 0 (prerequisite):** USG license (N-01) + ZEN registration in the S9 automation registry (N-06) + BPL founding push
2. Wire Marketplace links into all repo READMEs (currently missing — PM Report 001 P1)
3. LinkedIn launch series (governance-first thesis)
4. Flagship demo write-up (session-001 ZEN→Standards registry projection PoC)
5. Sustained cadence from the content pipeline above

### Timing driver (HZN-001)
Position governance-first **before** Autodesk Assistant's 2027 standalone cross-product rollout sets the industry's agent narrative. The window is now-ish; the tone toward Autodesk stays respectful — they just approved ZEN for their Marketplace.

### Metrics — funnel quality, not vanity
- GitHub: stars → watchers → issues → inbox submissions → **ARPL usage reports** (the truest top-funnel signal)
- Ecosystem: first external reproduction (the moment ARPL's evidence pyramid grows its second layer), STAR-T-GATE intake quality
- Commercial: ZEN trials and conversions traced back to which narrative produced them
- Audience: engagement of BIM decision-makers, not raw impressions
- Persona funnel: 3XFF newsletter members → personal-post readers → TIVERIT page followers → repo visitors → ZEN trials

## 5. Reality frame — the world outside the walls

The Herald does not live in a TIVERIT vacuum. Every publication needs an external hook — a live debate, a news event, a trend the audience already cares about — and must speak the audience's language, not the system's. The seat therefore carries a **reality frame**: a versioned model of the outside world it speaks into.
