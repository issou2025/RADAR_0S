- Relevant operations have reproducible checks and representative real artifacts or runtime evidence. Static inspection, mocked checks, native execution, calculation and visual inspection are reported separately. Unsupported or untested environments are not advertised as verified.
- `README.md`, its migration/adoption map and the project-owned context map describe the resulting package accurately. Shared-source use and explicitly chosen local-copy use have working resource paths and explicit dependency requirements.
