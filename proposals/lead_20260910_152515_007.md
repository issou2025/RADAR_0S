# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**PRD: Pesaje destination, daily kg, and weekly Promedio**

*Project description:*
## Problem Statement

I can log Comidas and see Cumplimiento for the month, but I cannot record what I weigh. I want a place to note one kg reading per Día (ideally in the morning, fasting), see those readings on a month calendar like the one I already have, and see the Promedio of that Semana (Monday–Sunday) with whether it went up or down versus the Semana before — and by how much.

## Solution

A third destination, **Pesaje**, next to Día and Cumplimiento, sharing the same selected Día. On that destination: the Promedio of the selected Día’s Semana at the top (green arrow if the Promedio dropped vs the previous Semana, red if it rose, no arrow on a tie or when the previous Semana has no Promedio), a kg field in the center with a morning-fasting hint and an explicit Guardar, and the month calendar at the bottom whose cells show the kg (empty if none). One Pesaje per Día, in kilograms, greater than zero, to at most one decimal; saving again replaces. No delete. Past, today, and future Días are allowed. A Pesaje does not create Cumplimiento. The Promedio is the mean of recorded Pesajes in that Monday–Sunday (gaps omitted, not zero), including days that sit in the other month when the Semana spans two months.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Glossary (keep / write into CONTEXT.md):**
  - **Pesaje:** a recorded body mass in kilograms for one Día, greater than zero, to one decimal. A Día has at most one; recording again replaces it. Once a Día has a Pesaje, it is not cleared — only replaced. The Día may be today, past, or future. Independent of Comidas: a Día may have a Pesaje with no Comida, and vice versa. It is not Macros and not a Meta. Also the destination that shows those readings: month cells are the mass, not a Cumplimiento color. The Promedio there is of the Semana that contains the selected Día.
  - **Semana:** Monday through Sunday in the user’s local timezone — the same week the month calendar uses. It may span two months; a Promedio is not clipped to the displayed month. Never a rolling seven Días ending today.
  - **Promedio:** the arithmetic mean of the Pesajes recorded in one Semana. A Semana with no Pesaje has none. Días without a Pesaje are omitted, not zero. Compared to the previous Semana only when both have a Promedio: a drop is down (green), a rise is up (red), a tie has no arrow. There is no Meta of weight. Do not invent an arrow when the previous Semana has no Promedio.
  - UI copy for destinations is **Día**, **Cumplimiento**, and **Pesaje**. Avoid: progreso, anotar, menú as screen names; pounds; coloring a Pesaje day as Cumplimiento; ayuno as a tracked state; deleting a Pesaje back to none; treating a future Pesaje as a Meta.
- **ADR-0003:** body weight was out of MVP. This spec reopens **only Pesaje**. Fasting as a product, photo, voice, barcode, meal plans, recipes, health-platform sync, accounts, and micros stay out. Implementer adds a short ADR (next free number after the existing 0009s, i.e. **0010**) that records that trade-off and points at this issue. Do not silently edit 0003 without the supersede trail.
- **ADR-0002:** Pesajes live on-device next to Alimentos, Comidas, and Meta. No account, no NutriMezzo server.
- **Do not reopen** ADR-0006/0008/0009 for this work. Live Meta and one Comida per Momento stay as they are. Pesaje does not participate in Cumplimiento.
- **ADR-0007:** no new motion stack. Saving a Pesaje may snap the Promedio and the cell; do not add a controller, Hero, Lottie, or a chart package. `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf` already covers the app.
- **Seams:** none new. Pesaje write/read, replace, rejection of invalid kg, and Promedio (including comparison and month-spanning Semana) live on **Diario**, same as `dia()` / `cumplimientoDe`. Navigation, field, Guardar, calendar cells, and arrows are specified through the existing Flutter widget tests that pump the app. Persistence is the existing diary-store callback, extended to also write Pesajes (not a second database).
- **Diario API (names may vary; one module):**
  - Construct with an iterable of Pesajes (fecha + kg).
  - `guardarPesaje(fecha, kg)`: normalize fecha to local calendar date; upsert the single Pesaje for that Día; persist. Reject `kg <= 0` and kg with more than one decimal digit (`ArgumentError`, same spirit as invalid alimento). Integers and one decimal are valid (72 and 72.4). Do not silently round 72.45 to 72.5.
  - No borrar/clear.
  - Query Pesaje for a fecha (absent if none).
  - Query Promedio for the Semana containing a fecha: mean of present Pesajes in that Monday–Sunday, or none. Previous Semana is the seven Días immediately before that Monday. Comparison: if both have a Promedio, expose the signed difference (this minus previous) so the UI can show drop/rise/tie; if the previous has none, expose no delta.
  - `cumplimientoDe` stays a function of Comidas + current Meta only.
  - `Dia` may expose an optional Pesaje for convenience, but a missing Pesaje must not look like Cumplimiento ninguno for the wrong reason — Cumplimiento ignores it.
- **Schema:** persist Pesajes as a list of `{fecha, kg}` (or equivalent) in the existing on-device diary JSON alongside Alimentos and Comidas. Do not persist Promedio, arrows, selected destination, or a weight Meta. Extend the diary persist callback that today receives Alimentos and Comidas so it also receives Pesajes; update existing persist stubs.
- **App shell:** three destinations, default Día. Shared shown fecha. Cumplimiento tap → set fecha and switch to Día (unchanged). Pesaje tap → set fecha, stay on Pesaje. Month chevrons change the displayed month only. Catálogo and Ajustes remain pushed routes; pop returns to Pesaje if opened from there. App bar title on this destination: **Pesaje**.
- **Pesaje layout:** Promedio (value + optional arrow + optional delta in kg, one decimal) at the top; kg field vertically centered with fasting-hint help and Guardar; month calendar below. No FAB. Reuse the month grid: Monday start, month label and chevrons, selection ring. Parameterize the cell: Cumplimiento keeps color+day-number; Pesaje shows kg or empty, no Cumplimiento colors.
- **Guardar UX:** field bound to selected Día. Guardar disabled or no-ops when empty, ≤ 0, or more than one decimal. Locale is already `es`; accept the decimal separator the field already uses. After a successful save, cell and Promedio refresh. Same-value Guardar may persist as a no-visible-change replace.
- **Copy:** Spanish. Fasting line is a recommendation only (e.g. weigh in the morning, fasting). Do not introduce “Progreso”, “Anotar”, or “menú”.
- **Packages:** no new calendar, chart, or health SDK.

## Testing Decisions

Good tests assert **external behavior** at the existing seams: Diario results, what a person sees after pumping the app, and what comes back from the on-device store. Do not specify private widget state, GlobalKeys, hex colors, or CustomPainter internals. No new test seam. No real network. No goldens.

### Seam 1 — Diario (prior art: Diario tests for Día macros, Cumplimiento, live Alimento, persistirDiario)

- No Pesaje for a fecha until guardarPesaje; query is absent, not 0.
- guardarPesaje then query returns that kg; second save on the same fecha replaces.
- There is no API that clears a Pesaje; replacing is the only mutation.
- kg ≤ 0 or more than one decimal is rejected; the previous Pesaje (if any) remains.
- 72 and 72.4 are accepted.
- Future and past fechas save like today.
- A Pesaje on a Día with no Comida: `cumplimientoDe` is still `ninguno`.
- Comidas without a Pesaje: Cumplimiento unchanged from today.
- Promedio of a Semana with no Pesajes: none.
- Promedio of Mon 72.4 and Wed 71.8 only: 72.1; Thu–Sun omitted, not zero.
- Selected Thursday 1 Oct: Promedio includes Pesajes on Mon 29 Sep and Tue 30 Sep in that Semana.
- Comparison uses the previous Monday–Sunday only; a rolling 7-day window must not be used (seed a counterexample if needed).
- Previous Semana empty: Promedio present, no delta.
- Both Semanas have Promedio: delta is this minus previous (drop negative / rise positive — UI maps to green/red; tests may assert the numbers).
- Tie: delta 0 (UI: no arrow).
- Promedio is not persisted through the diary callback — only the Pesajes list.

### Seam 2 — App widgets (prior art: Cumplimiento destination tests, `desactivarAnimaciones`, HomeDia fecha sharing)

- Pumping the app still shows Día first, with Comida FAB; Pesaje is a third destination.
- Pesaje has no Comida FAB and no Día chevrons; Catálogo and Ajustes remain; opening them from Pesaje returns to Pesaje.
- Shared fecha: Día on Monday → Pesaje field/Promedio/calendar selection are that Monday; tapping a Thursday on Pesaje stays on Pesaje with Thursday selected; switching to Día then shows Thursday.
- Cumplimiento tap still opens Día (regression).
- Layout order is observable: Promedio copy above the field, calendar below; field is the centered kg entry with fasting hint and Guardar.
- Empty selected Día: field empty; Guardar does not create a Pesaje.
- Type 72.4, Guardar: cell shows 72.4; Promedio updates; Diario has that Pesaje.
- Type a new kg and Guardar: cell and Promedio show the replacement, not both values.
- 0, negative, 72.45, empty: Guardar does not change Diario.
- Future day selectable and saveable from the month grid.
- Cells with Pesaje show kg, not Cumplimiento colors; empty days show no kg.
- Promedio hidden or absent-copy when the Semana has no Pesajes.
- Green drop / red rise / no arrow on tie / no arrow without previous Promedio — assert via semantics, tooltip, or visible text (e.g. the delta), not raw Color.
- Month-spanning Semana: seed 29 Sep + 1 Oct, select 1 Oct, Promedio uses both even though September days are not cells in October.
- Do not add golden/screenshot tests.

### Seam 3 — Almacén (prior art: reopen Alimentos/Comidas, “no se guarda Cumplimiento”)

- After guardarPesaje, a new `abrirDiario` returns that Pesaje.
- Reopen does not store Promedio, arrows, or a weight Meta key.
- Existing Meta/BYOK/Alimento/Comida reopen tests still pass with the extended persist payload.

## Out of Scope

- Deleting a Pesaje back to none.
- Multiple Pesajes on the same Día, intra-day timestamps, or averaging within a Día.
- Meta of weight, Ajustes fields for kg, or judging Pesaje against a target.
- Fasting timer, fasting window, or storing whether the pesaje was fasting.
- Pounds or unit switching.
- Health-platform sync, photo, voice, barcode, meal plans, recipes, composite foods, accounts, micros, iOS.
- Mixing Pesaje into the Día destination (ring, bars, FAB) or into the Cumplimiento color cascade.
- Weekly charts, sparklines, body-fat, BMI.
- New calendar/chart/health packages.
- Changing capture, Confirmación, Catálogo, BYOK, or live Meta.
- Persisting destination, fold state, or Promedio snapshots.

## Further Notes

- Canonical language: `CONTEXT.md` (Pesaje, Semana, Promedio). Local diary: ADR-0002. This feature contradicts ADR-0003’s “body weight is out” on purpose — ADR-0010 must say so.
- Implementer should keep Flutter thin over Diario: mean, Semana window, replace, and validation belong on Diario so widget tests only switch destinations, type kg, tap Guardar, and read copy.
- Reuse the month calendar; do not copy-paste a second grid. Cell content is the only fork (Cumplimiento color vs Pesaje kg).
- Green means the Promedio dropped, red that it rose. That is a display of direction, not a Meta. Someone in a surplus would still see red on a rise — accepted for this personal tracker.
