### Status & document-type rules

- Real **PAID/COMPLETED** payment with verified collection -> the only eligible status for **payment receipt export**.
- `PENDING`, `RECONCILING`, `FAILED`, `UNKNOWN` -> **NO `إيصال سداد` export** or fake settled receipt; continue offering `تفاصيل العملية` and truthful status, possibly separate not-proof transaction-details view.
- `REFUNDED` -> preserve original paid receipt if one was legally issued; refund requires a SEPARATE dedicated `إشعار استرداد`/credit-note legal decision and authoritative provider confirmation, never quietly reuse original receipt as current paid proof.
- If payment amount/status/method/address or issuer legal field is unknown, omit unsupported optional field or block compliant issuance rather than fabricate.
- Do NOT assign an ETA e-receipt UUID/QR or claim official tax integration; Egyptian Tax Authority e-receipt is a separate regulated system with technical registration and issuing obligations. Official reference: https://portal.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/e-receipt-services and https://pos.eta.gov.eg/ar/faqs . Accounting/legal counsel must decide whether platform KONFRM or property Owner legally issues the underlying invoice/ETA e-receipt, the proper tax registration number and treatment of deposit vs total booking.
- Check Apple App Review/HIG and Google Play Developer Policies for physical-stay transactions, user privacy, file download/share and misleading receipts. Android must offer suitable native Save As/Share UX, iOS Document Share / Files when later available.
