# Project Proposal — Permit Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**research(rooms): read the height a room's area is taken at, so stepped rooms get their section's outline (follow-up of #90, RE-374)**

*Project description:*
## What

RE-374 (#1587, the follow-up of #90) reads a room's outline from the edges of its stored solid, but only where the solid is a prism of its floor up to 4 ft above it, so that the floor's outline is the room's area. A room whose solid changes below that height keeps its record box: a raised floor, a step, a pocket or a low bulkhead. Four rooms of Autodesk's Snowdon Towers Architectural 2024 sample are stepped so, and 593 rooms of the models measured keep their box today (RE-374 section 5).

For one of the four, Revit's area (`Vim.Room.Area` in the sample's later VIM edition, local only) is neither the floor's area nor the record box's. It equals the solid's horizontal section 4.0 ft above the floor to 0.001 ft², not its section at 3.9 ft (0.04 ft² off) or the floor (4.6 ft² short). Of the other three, one matches Revit's area at any section from 2 ft up, one at a section 6 ft up, and one at no height. All of this is local, against the VIM. Related: #1207 (stepped rooms' volumes).

## To do

1. Find where the height a room's area is taken at is stored, and the elevation it is measured from, from byte observations (a Level's or the room's own record, compared across rooms whose sections need different heights). 4 ft is measured on one room and is not taken as a default from anything outside the files.
2. Slice the solid at that height (a horizontal section of a solid whose faces are planes and vertical cylinders) and write that outline for rooms whose floor is not their section.
3. Explain the room that matches at a 6 ft section and the one that matches at no height before writing either.
4. Measure the area against Revit's IFC4 export where one holds the room (the BIM4LCA office at building permit), and against the VIM where none does (Snowdon Towers, whose IFC4 export holds no `IfcSpace`). Show which of the 593 rooms on their box take Revit's area to 0.1% from the section and which stay on the box with a stated reason, and that none of the rooms the walk and the edges read today moves.

## Done when

The four stepped rooms take Revit's area to 0.1%, or stay on their box with a measured reason, and no other export moves.
