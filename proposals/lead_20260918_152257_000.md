# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**[Beam] Top cover is not read when the beam is JOINED to the floor above it**

*Project description:*
Reported by the owner from live use:

> if there is a floor above beam and i did not hide the floor or slab it can not read top cover as slab and beam are joined

## The mechanism, and why it is not a beam-specific bug

When two elements are **joined**, Revit merges their solids at the interface and the shared faces stop existing as separate faces. A read that depends on finding the beam's **top face** therefore finds nothing — or finds some other face — while the same beam with the floor hidden or unjoined reads correctly.

Hiding the floor changes what the *view* shows, which is why it appears to fix it; the join is still there, but the geometry the read walks comes back differently.

**The same mechanism was just hit on the column side** (#161): an upward ray fired from inside a column joined to its slab found nothing at all, which looks identical to "there is nothing above this column" — a legitimate answer for a ground- or top-storey column, and therefore a dangerous silence.

## Why this is filed separately from the column work

Per this repo's rule that a ticket never mixes two structural elements: the column side stays in #161, this is the beam tool's own. What they share is a **cause**, and whatever is learned about detecting joins belongs in `RFT.lib` once both sides know what they need.

## What needs establishing

- exactly which read fails — the cover parameter itself, or the face-based geometry read that feeds it
- whether `JoinGeometryUtils.GetJoinedElements` / `IsCuttingElementInJoin` gives a reliable way to detect the condition **without** geometry
- whether the correct behaviour is to read through the join, or to **refuse and say why** — a silently wrong cover is the failure mode this project exists to prevent, and "beam is joined to the floor above; unjoin it or state the cover" is an actionable message

No code should change before that is measured on a live host.
