*Project description:*
Hello DigitalKhatt team,

I am developing QuraLYT, a Quran reading app built with Flutter and FlutterFlow. I use DigitalKhatt Madina for Quranic Arabic text.

I am looking for expert guidance on a text-rendering problem.

Flutter's native "TextAlign.justify" can create excessive gaps between Arabic words. I understand that ordinary space-based justification is different from Arabic kashida justification.

I previously tried the "arabic_text_justification" package, but the rendering and scrolling lag made it unsuitable for my app.

My goal is to preserve accurate Quranic text, diacritics and ayah markers while improving line spacing and maintaining smooth scrolling, without building a completely custom rendering engine.

Could you advise whether there is a practical, performant approach for Flutter, or direct me to the appropriate DigitalKhatt repository or developer who could help?

I would particularly appreciate advice based on real implementation experience.

Thank you for your work on DigitalKhatt and Quran typography.
