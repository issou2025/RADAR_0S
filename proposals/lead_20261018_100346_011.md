## October 18 — MANDATORY reporter communication loop (current Production gap)

**Operator acceptance requirement, highest priority:** Greg explicitly states that the current Task Feedback system fails to notify the person who reported a problem once the correction is completed, and an assigned Manager/owner cannot reliably **email the original reporter to request clarification**. These are required **two-way ticket lifecycle behaviors**, not optional email polish. Accepted #172 implementation only establishes reporter-authenticated `SETUP_CORRECTION` → Work Order Intake and existing Directus **Manager** notification on new Intake; it **does not** prove a reporter-facing closure or clarification response loop.

**Contract:**
1. **Keep existing free-form reporter submission intact.** Preserve its original note, Intake ID, reporter's authorized identity/contact, time, task/Stage/Scene/assignment and source provenance. Do not create a second feedback form or auto-create an active Work Order.
2. **Assigned-owner "Request clarification" action.** Authorized assigned correction owner/Manager enters a specific question and optionally identifies missing context, then submits through a governed ticket comment/event. The system records author/time/recipient/question and changes visible follow-up responsibility to **Waiting on Reporter** without silently releasing the owner of ultimate responsibility. **Send a targeted email to the actual original reporter**, not all assignees or Rick by default. It identifies the finding and a safe **Respond to clarification** link/action, with no need to understand Directus. The reporter's answer attaches to that same Intake/ticket activity history, preserves original text, and alerts the responsible owner. If public/non-SSO reporters are in scope, design a least-privilege, expiring response mechanism without disclosing other tickets; no unsafe unauthenticated access or presumed inbound email ingestion.
3. **Resolution/closure email is mandatory.** On an authorized final outcome for a correction (`Resolved/Verified`, `Deferred`, `Rejected/No Action`, `Duplicate/Linked`, where supported by reviewed lifecycle), **notify the original reporter** of the outcome, plain-language work/decision and reason, responsible owner and link to the ticket. For a feedback item promoted into an actionable Work Order, **promotion alone is not correction completion**: preserve source Intake→WO relationship; notify reporter of eventual linked WO resolution (and any independently resolved feedback outcome), not a misleading "completed" email at promotion. Require a substantive resolution note or reason before closing/defer/reject.
4. **Reporter experience:** they can find **My Submitted Feedback** and view status, questions, their answers, assignments, latest progress, and closure explanation in the Work Management app within their authorized scope. An optional initial submission receipt can use the same infrastructure; at minimum the required **clarification and final outcome** notices must work. Do not confuse email sent with acknowledgement, activity or completion; do not require reporter to chase the owner to get an outcome.
5. **Durable notification semantics:** use the reviewed MSB server/Google Workspace mail path after real runtime reconnaissance. Enqueue messages from accepted durable ticket state/events with stable keys (ticket ID + action/event ID + intended recipient), audit QUEUED/SENT/FAILED and retries; avoid duplicate/out-of-order mails on save or reprocessing, and never mark delivery proven solely from a database insert. If the reporter's contact/email is unavailable, invalid, opted out by applicable policy, or delivery fails, **flag the communication problem to the ticket owner/Manager**, leave it visible and do not silently claim closure was communicated. Notification failure must not falsify correction status. Preserve operational Directus non-mail effects, and retire competing Directus mail Flows only with an explicit, tested cutover.
6. **Accountability / January review:** comments, requests, responses, notifications, disposition, actor attribution, and linked WO/Setup corrections survive 2026 season rollover and feed existing #209 review. Triage coordinators and assigned correction owners are distinct from original reporter. Rick is not a default inbox or proxy for reporter communication.

**Minimum operator acceptance:**
- Greg or another authorized manager opens an existing real-shape `SETUP_CORRECTION`, assigns it to a correction owner, and requests one specific detail from the original reporting volunteer. A test recipient receives the correct notification, opens the limited-scope ticket, submits an answer, and the owner sees an auditable response on that same item.
- The authorized owner resolves a disposable correction with a substantive note. The **original reporter receives the correct closed-loop email** and can review the resolution; no duplicate mail is generated by reloading or retries.
- Explicitly defer, reject and promote-to-WO scenarios notify accurately; a promoted ticket does not falsely claim repair completion; later linked WO completion can be traced back to the reporter.
- Where email is absent or delivery fails, Manager sees **Reporter could not be notified** and can act; no silent dropped message and no false "sent" claim.
- Verify these scenarios against a disposable current-Production clone and the approved mail transport test boundary, with role, idempotence, notification-audit and browser tests before any Production send/cutover.
