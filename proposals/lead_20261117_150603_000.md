### Reproduction

`IfcAdvancedFace` faces whose surface is an `IfcBSplineSurfaceWithKnots` / `IfcRationalBSplineSurfaceWithKnots` are drawn wrong. Revit (ODA) exports hit this regularly: they write sloped and trimmed slab faces as B-spline faces with `SameSense = .F.`, and flat ones as a 2 × 2 control net larger than the face.

Minimal fixture (a flat 20 × 20 rational patch at z = 5 whose surface normal points down, used with `SameSense = .F.` so the face should face up, bounded by a unit right triangle):

```
#1=IFCADVANCEDFACE((#2),#10,.F.);#2=IFCFACEOUTERBOUND(#3,.T.);
#3=IFCEDGELOOP((#21,#22,#23));
#21=IFCORIENTEDEDGE(*,*,#51,.T.);#22=IFCORIENTEDEDGE(*,*,#52,.T.);#23=IFCORIENTEDEDGE(*,*,#53,.T.);
#51=IFCEDGECURVE(#31,#32,$,.T.);#52=IFCEDGECURVE(#32,#33,$,.T.);#53=IFCEDGECURVE(#33,#31,$,.T.);
#31=IFCVERTEXPOINT(#41);#32=IFCVERTEXPOINT(#42);#33=IFCVERTEXPOINT(#43);
#41=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((0.,0.,5.));#42=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((1.,0.,5.));#43=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((0.,1.,5.));
#10=IFCRATIONALBSPLINESURFACEWITHKNOTS(1,1,((#11,#12),(#13,#14)),.UNSPECIFIED.,.F.,.F.,.F.,(2,2),(2,2),(0.,1.),(0.,1.),.UNSPECIFIED.,((1.,1.),(1.,1.)));
#11=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((-10.,-10.,5.));#12=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((10.,-10.,5.));
#13=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((-10.,10.,5.));#14=IFCCARTESIANPOINT((10.,10.,5.));
```

1. Lower the face with `advanced_face::plan::lower_face` (or mesh any model containing it with `process()`).
2. Measure the mesh area and the triangle normals.

A curved, unbounded B-spline face (for example a degree (2, 1) arch whose Su × Sv points up) shows the winding fault alone.

### Expected vs actual

| | Expected | Actual on `main` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat bounded B-spline face (fixture above) | 0.5 m², the bounded triangle, facing up | ≈ 400 m², the whole 20 × 20 control patch, facing down |
| Curved B-spline face, `SameSense = .T.` | normals along Su × Sv | normals along −(Su × Sv) |
| Curved B-spline face, `SameSense = .F.` | normals along −(Su × Sv) | normals along Su × Sv (into the solid) |

Two causes, both in `rust/geometry/src/processors/advanced_face/`:

1. **Winding.** `tessellate_bspline_surface` (`bspline.rs`) winds each grid quad along −(Su × Sv), unlike the cylinder and revolution grids, which wind along Su × Sv. `FacePlan` then reverses `SameSense = .F.` faces, so those end up facing into their solid (lit from below in the viewer).
2. **No trimming.** B-spline faces are tessellated over the whole knot domain; bounds are ignored (the known gap logged by `note_untrimmed_bspline_face`). For a flat face written as an oversized control net, that draws the whole control quad past the solid's edge.

**Scope of the fix (#10529):**
- Wind B-spline grids along Su × Sv.
- A bounded B-spline face whose control net is planar is that plane, so lower it through `lower_planar` and triangulate its bounds, like an `IfcPlane` face. Rational surfaces keep their existing weight validation (non-finite, ≤ 0 or mis-shaped `WeightsData` is still refused).

**Out of scope:** trimming curved B-spline faces by their bounds. They stay untrimmed, as now.

**Impact:** any fixture with B-spline faces changes output, so the determinism manifests and the IfcOpenShell parity reference need re-pinning with the fix.
