# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Revit 2027: stair landings are written with no IfcSlabType where Revit types them LANDING (CHL, 14 landings)**

*Project description:*
From HWT-25's run of 0.24.0 on the confidential Revit 2027 structural model CHL (#1829, [comment on #1841](https://github.com/DrunkOnJava/rvt-rs/issues/1841)), counts only. Its 14 stair landings are written as `IfcSlab` parts of their `IfcStair` with no type object. Revit's export types each one by an `IfcSlabType` `LANDING`. Every other slab in common agrees on its type's `PredefinedType` (59 `BASESLAB`, 26 `FLOOR`).

On Revit 2024 the landings' types already match Revit's. On Snowdon Towers Architectural (Revit's local-only IFC4 export against rvt-rs's at `88714209`, IfcOpenShell 0.9.0.post1, counts only), both exports have:
- 20 landing slabs, each a part of its `IfcStair`, each `.LANDING.`;
- 17 of them typed by an `IfcSlabType` `LANDING`.

So the gap is on the 2027 save, or on the kind of landing CHL holds, not the rule as a whole. Two things to check:
- whether the landing type join (`native_landing_type` in `element_provenance.rs`) is read on Revit 2027;
- whether CHL's landings are of a kind Snowdon's are not: monolithic or sketched landings, or landings whose type record is not found.

No model Measure fetches pairs a Revit 2027 stair with Revit's export, so the check runs through HWT-25's CHL counts, or through a probe of the 2027 samples' landings against their 2024 saves (`bodies_across_saves.py` style).

**Done when** a 2027 landing gets its `IfcSlabType` `LANDING` wherever its 2024 save's landing does, nothing else moves, and CHL's 14 match. Or a measured negative says why a 2027 landing's type is not read.

Related: #1841 (closed), #1829, #1977.
