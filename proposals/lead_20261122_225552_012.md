## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Provenance "?" disclosure is unreadable: raw dl of hash, machine paths and unformatted values; print the rule as a Loom expression**

*Project description:*
**Where found:** screenshotting `web/src/examples/provenance-masks-system.ddd` (node + React/mantine v9, scaffolded detail page). Companion to #6422 (hash-only rule, duplicated leaves).

**What a reader sees today** after clicking "?" next to Bonus:

```
▼ ?
Rule      fd1c1c33
Value     10695.0000
salary    155000.0000
percent   6
salary    155000.0000
rating    9
```

- It is a bare `<details>` + `<dl>`: no card/popover, no pack styling. Opening it pushes the row tall, and the value floats to the vertical middle of the expanded block.
- "?" is the only affordance, and it doesn't say "how was this computed".
- Paths are identifiers (`costPerHead`, `percent`), not the field/param labels the rest of the page uses (`Cost Per Head`). Values are the raw wire encoding (`155000.0000`) instead of the page's money/number formatting.
- The rule is a content hash. The thing a human wants, the *formula*, is not shown (#6422).
- Inputs read from the row and parameters of the operation are indistinguishable. The operation that wrote the value (`awardBonus`) and when/who are not shown, though `provenance_records` stores the actor.

**Expected:** a human-readable explanation. For example:

> **Bonus = 10,695.00**
> set by **Award Bonus** · rule `salary × percent / 100 + salary × rating / 1000`
> = 155,000.00 × 6 / 100 + 155,000.00 × 9 / 1000
> Inputs: Salary 155,000.00 · Percent *(argument)* 6 · Rating 9 ↗ *(itself provenanced, links to its own explanation)*

**Rethink: make Loom expressions printable for humans.** The rule snapshot already has the RHS `source` and lowered `ir`. A printer (the AST printer `src/language/print/` exists for `unfold`) could render a rule:
1. as Loom source (for developers),
2. with labels instead of identifiers and the inputs substituted (for users), localized through the i18n catalog like other labels.

It needs a decision on where that text comes from: shipped with the client per `snapshotId` at build time, or carried in the lineage. And the frontends should render it through a pack component rather than a raw `<details>`. Applies to all six frontends + HEEx, which share the `ProvenanceInfo` primitive.
