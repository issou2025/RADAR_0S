## Notion — proposed prerequisite

Provide the missing, opt-in testing support so an independent validator can
reliably check Walkable moons without changing the player's real settings or
confusing camera movement, hovering or injected setup with actual player travel
and ground contact.

Cover selectable generated moon starting contexts and honest fixture records;
read-only body/ground/local-frame/gravity/sky observations; ordinary-input
save/quit/restart, saved views and Reset in disposable normal profiles; and
real-input journey profiling with bounded-resource/loading/retirement evidence,
comparison conditions and release-build provenance.

This is testing infrastructure only. It does not add walkable moon gameplay,
moon-relative production save behavior, a Surface detail rename, Help pages,
teleportation or a mini map. Missing gameplay must be reported as unavailable,
not fabricated to make the tests succeed. Prove the new test paths on existing
production behavior, then rerun #1215's independent testability review.
