## Goal

BrowserSkill prepares a form through the accessibility tree, **fills every applicable control and reads it back without per-control approvals, witnesses, bank-grants, widget recognizers or refusal paths**. The model answers from existing owner facts/operator brief, and missing facts use the existing owner-question **request, not a Gate**. No per-step check for non-Submit fill/select/choose/check/upload/focus/blur or in-form Next/Continue.

Exactly **one code-owned `check_pre_submit`** runs before a classified Submit click or Enter/implicit form submit. Five substantive items are pre-click; the sixth records unconfirmed possible submission as `submit_uncertain`, **never `applied`**. Failing checks persist item/reason for existing Luna `needs_human`, correction/requeue and the same check next run. Leave production prefill/session available when possible (best effort); only salary, new facts and owner accounts route to the owner.
