## What happens

## Expected

## Suggested fix

1. Run `uvx ruff check --select F401,F841 --config 'builtins=["unicode","basestring","long","unichr","xrange","__revit__"]' revit_mcp startup.py`.
2. Remove what it reports, except the tracked bugs above.
3. Add `F401` to the extension step of `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.

## How to check

CI passes. Keep the change free of logic edits: imports and dead assignments only.
