# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**feat: create views and sheets**

*Project description:*
Documentation work starts with views and sheets: a plan per level, a section through a problem area, a sheet with the views placed on it. None of that can be created today.

## Tools

All are actions with `dry_run`, `summary`, `verification` and one named undo entry, refused in read-only mode. All except `revit_place_views_on_sheet` are allowed as `revit_batch` steps.

### `revit_create_view`

`revit_create_view(kind, name=null, level=null, view_family_type=null, template=null, scale=null, box=null, element_ids=null)`

- `kind`: `floor_plan`, `ceiling_plan`, `structural_plan`, `section`, `3d` or `drafting`. Area plans and elevations are out of scope.
- Plans need `level`. `view_family_type` defaults to the first type of the matching `ViewFamily`.
- `section` and `3d`: `box` as `{min_mm:[x,y,z], max_mm:[x,y,z]}` or `element_ids` to fit a box around the elements plus 1000 mm. A `3d` view gets a section box; a `section` is cut through the box center, looking along +Y, sized to the box.
- `name` must be unique; Revit's default name is kept when `name` is null.
- `template`: optional view template name, applied after creation.

### `revit_duplicate_view`

`revit_duplicate_view(view, mode="duplicate", name=null)` with `mode` `duplicate`, `with_detailing` or `dependent` (`ViewDuplicateOption`).

### `revit_apply_view_template`

`revit_apply_view_template(views, template)`: applies a template to one or more views; reports views where the template type does not match.

### `revit_create_sheet`

`revit_create_sheet(number, name, title_block=null)`

- `title_block`: family or `Family: Type` name; default is the first loaded title block. A duplicate sheet number is refused.

### `revit_place_views_on_sheet`

`revit_place_views_on_sheet(sheet, views)` where `views` is a list of `{view, x_mm=null, y_mm=null}`.

- Without coordinates, viewports are laid out left to right in the title block area, wrapping to a new row, with 20 mm gaps.
- Schedules are placed with `ScheduleSheetInstance.Create`; other views with `Viewport.Create` after `Viewport.CanAddViewToSheet`. A view already on another sheet is refused by name.
