## The problem

The phone app (Flutter for Android and iOS, #50) does several things that both app stores review closely:

- it reads the **whole photo library**, not only photos the person picks;
- it reads each photo's **EXIF location and time**;
- it runs the weed screening **on the device**, in the background where the OS allows it;
- it uploads **exact coordinates with their accuracy**, plus an optional cropped photo, but only for reports the person approves one by one (#56, #57);
- it downloads a signed model and watchlist bundle (#59).

Apple and Google each need a justification for every permission, purpose strings, a privacy label or Data safety form, and a privacy policy. Apple also needs a privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`), and Google needs a declaration form for full photo access. None of this exists yet. Two things could block release outright:

1. **Google Play's Photo and Video Permissions policy.** Since 28 May 2025, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` is allowed only when broad photo access is the app's *core* function and the system photo picker can't serve it. Google may refuse our declaration. The app needs a design that still works with the picker alone.
2. **GPL-3.0-or-later (#32) and the App Store.** Apple's terms of use add restrictions that the GPL forbids (the 2010 FSF and VLC case). With outside contributions under inbound = outbound licensing, App Store distribution of a GPL build may not be allowed.

Exact locations are the point of the app: they are its scientific value (#61). So the declarations must say plainly that exact locations are collected, not play it down. An inaccurate label or form is grounds for rejection or removal.

## Plan

Deliver `docs/mobile-store-compliance.md` as a working draft that the app (`mobile/`, `docs/mobile.md`) is built against:

1. **A permission-by-permission table** (iOS and Android), giving why each permission is needed and draft purpose strings. On iOS, location comes from the photo's own metadata, so the app requests **no** Core Location permission. On Android it needs `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION`. Unused media permissions that plugins add through manifest merge are removed.
2. **A draft Apple privacy nutrition label** and **privacy manifest**: data types, linked or not linked, no tracking, required-reason APIs, and a note on Flutter plugin manifests.
3. **Draft Google Play Data safety answers**, including the rule that on-device-only processing is not "collected".
4. **The full-library-access justification for Play**, and a **fallback design** if it is refused: a picker-only flavour using the photo picker's location-metadata opt-in (August 2026 Mainline) or `ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION`.
5. **The prominent disclosure and consent screens** that come before each permission request.
6. **Background-work limits**: no foreground service, WorkManager or BGProcessingTask with charging and idle constraints, and an in-app scan as the baseline.
7. **A draft privacy policy outline** under the NZ Privacy Act 2020 (IPPs 1–13 including IPP 3A from 1 May 2026, notifiable breaches, privacy officer), consistent with #57's schema, consent and withdrawal and #60's rights. It is marked as a draft pending legal review.
8. **Account and data deletion** (Apple 5.1.1(v), Play account deletion), tied to device tokens.
9. **The GPL and App Store risk**, with options.
10. **A pre-submission checklist** for each store.

Facts that depend on unmerged work (#59 bundle, #60 rights, #61 `consent.location`, PR #85 intake) are marked as such.

## Other options considered

- **Picker-only from day one** (no `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, no full iOS access): it passes review most easily, but it gives up passive discovery, which is the app's reason to exist. Kept as the fallback flavour, not the default.
- **Requesting Core Location on iOS** to tag photos with no EXIF: rejected for now. It adds a permission and a privacy-label entry for little gain, and #54 handles missing locations.
- **Coarsening locations to soften the declarations**: rejected (#61). Exact points are the science.
- **Publishing only on Google Play and F-Droid** to avoid the GPL conflict: listed below as a licence option.

## Decisions left to the user

- **Publisher entity and developer accounts**: an individual or an organisation, such as a charity or incorporated society. Apple and Google organisation accounts need a D-U-N-S number, and Apple 5.1.1(ix) expects sensitive-data apps to come from a legal entity. A new personal Play account must also run a closed test (12 testers for 14 days) before production.
- **Privacy contact and privacy officer** (Privacy Act s 201), with a postal and email address for the policy and the store listings.
- **Retention period** for reports, crops and tombstones (#57 implements withdrawal but no time-based expiry).
- **Hosting region and providers**, which IPP 12 and the policy must name (for example NZ or Australia).
- **GPL against the App Store.** Options are an app-store additional permission (GPLv3 s 7) granted before outside contributions arrive, a separate permissive licence for `mobile/`, a CLA, or no App Store build.
- Whether the app enrols devices itself, which makes in-app account and data deletion mandatory, or uses operator-issued tokens.
- Whether crops are uploaded at all (#57, #60).
