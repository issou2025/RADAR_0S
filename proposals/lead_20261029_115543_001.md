# Project Proposal — Pdf To Revit

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Milestone 1: IFC in, drawings out (tracking)**

*Project description:*
The first milestone: **IFC in, correct drawings out.** What users ran into on the calls, in the comments and in the smoke test was drawings from real IFC models, so that comes before new formats, permit packs and country standards.

**Done when:** a fixed set of real models (the acceptance set below) opens reliably and fast, and the plans, sections, elevations, schedules and sheets come out correct on screen, in PDF and in DXF: openings cut, door and window symbols right, cut faces joined, and `ifcsheet build --project` printing what the app shows.
