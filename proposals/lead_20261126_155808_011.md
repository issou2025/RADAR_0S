Image generation is cloud-only. On-device models can't generate images, so this is a natural "cloud earns it" moment.

### Scope
1. **Pre-made images for the 3 reviewed recipes.** Generate once, using a tool script and real Firebase AI Logic image generation: one image per step that has a visible cue, plus the finished plate. That's about 15 images, which Terry reviews before they ship as app assets.
   - Each image is labelled **"AI illustration — not a photo of your food"**.
   - The prompts come from the step's visible cue and the recipe ingredients only.
2. **On demand for AI-drafted recipes.** A "Show me what it should look like" button.
   - Gated by a new `CloudRoute.images` (now in the repo's `CLOUD_ROUTES`).
   - Respects local-only settings and connectivity, and asks for consent if needed.
   - Capped per session (e.g. 4 images), recorded in the ledger as text→image, and given the same label.
   - Results are cached for the session.
3. **Safety wording.** An illustration never shows doneness, internal temperature or safety ("compare the look, not the safety"), and gets no meat "done" shots. Heated steps keep the camera/thermometer disclaimers.
4. **Verify against primary docs before coding.** Confirm the current Firebase AI Logic image-generation API in the Dart `firebase_ai` package, the model ids and price, and record the price in `docs/COST-LEDGER.md`. Keep the model id configurable.
