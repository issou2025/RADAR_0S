## Project Understanding

**Store publishing checklist (Google Play + App Store)**

*Project description:*
Everything needed before the ILTIAM app is submitted to Google Play and the App Store. Related: #23 (go-live checklist), #36 (production environment), #6 (the privacy documents in `docs/privacy/`).

Nothing here means ILTIAM is legally approved, clinically validated or ready for real patients. Each item must be checked when it is done.

## Accounts
- [ ] **Google Play developer account.** A new **personal** account must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row** before it can publish to production. An **organization** account avoids that and needs a **D-U-N-S number**. Decide which, and plan the time.
- [ ] **Apple Developer account.** App Store Review Guideline **5.1.1(ix)**: apps in highly regulated fields, including healthcare, must be submitted by a **legal entity** that provides the service, not by an individual. Decide which legal entity submits.

- [ ] Terms URL: `https://iltiamcare.com/terms` (`terms-v1`), public and tested.
- [ ] Account deletion URL: `https://iltiamcare.com/delete-account`, public, with a working way to request deletion **without the app**.

## Google Play forms
- [ ] **Data safety** form from `docs/privacy/play-data-safety.md`.

- [ ] **Account deletion**: the in-app path and the web link above.
- [ ] Privacy policy URL, target audience (adults; minors only through a guardian), content rating.

## App Store
- [ ] **App privacy labels** ("nutrition labels"), from the same data as the Play data safety form.
- [ ] **In-app account deletion** (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): apps that have accounts must let users start account deletion inside the app. ILTIAM has Profile → Privacy → Delete my data; check that Apple accepts our flow (a request reviewed within 30 days that anonymises the record, because medical records are kept), and explain it in the review notes.
- [ ] Review notes and a demo account for Apple's reviewers.
