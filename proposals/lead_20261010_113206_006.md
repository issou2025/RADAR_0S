# Project Proposal — Static Website

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Mobile app for iOS and Android**

*Project description:*
### Goal and why

A TaskFest app for iOS and Android, with the same features as the website, signing in the way an
app should, and notifying through the phone's own push system. Primarily, like the whole project,
for the experience: how AI-assisted development copes with a mobile stack, its tooling and its
stores.

### To find out first

- **The technology**, compared with references:
  - native apps for iOS and Android;
  - a framework that renders native apps from one portable code base;
  - a framework that reuses at least some of the TypeScript we have (the generated wire types);
  - anything else worth considering.
- **Sign-in**: is client-side OAuth the way to go for an app, and what does it mean for the
  backend, which today is a backend-for-frontend holding the tokens itself?
- **Push notifications**: through APNs and FCM, for
  - due-date reminders;
  - changes made by others — which presupposes sharing, a feature that does not exist yet: named
    here as a dependency, not designed.
- **Offline**: online only, readable offline, or editable offline — compared, not presumed.
- **Anything forgotten** above.

Depth: a written comparison first. Throw-away spikes (on a branch, never merged) for the two or
three shortlisted options come after it has been read — they need Xcode and the Android SDK,
neither installed yet, and about 40 GB of free disk.

### Done when

- Investigation: the comparison is on this epic, Gunnar has chosen, and the choice is recorded
  under `doc/decisions/`.
- Epic: the app runs on an iPhone and an Android phone, installed through TestFlight and Play
  internal testing, with every feature of the website, sign-in, and push notifications for due
  dates.

### Constraints

- **Testers only**, no public store release: TestFlight and Google Play internal testing. That
  still needs both developer accounts — Apple Developer Program $99 a year, Google Play $25 once.
- Test devices: an iPhone and an Android phone.
- The API contract stays generated from the backend record (`doc/api/`); an app does not get a
  hand-written copy of it.
- Secrets — signing keys, push credentials — are never committed.

### Decisions — yours or mine

Mine: the technology; the sign-in shape; which push events, and when a reminder fires; the level
of offline support; anything that costs money (developer accounts).
Yours: how the comparison is written and referenced, the shortlist for the spikes, how the epic is
cut into stories once the decisions are made.

### Out of scope

- A public App Store or Play Store release.
- Designing sharing between accounts.
