### What happened?

My Office stereo pair (2x Play:5 Gen 2) plays, skips and seeks fine on its own. As soon as I add the Pool pair (2x Sonos Move Gen 1) to the group in Music Assistant (MA), every seek, skip or fast-forward makes every speaker except the leader (Office L) drop the music. I can hear Office R and the Pool pair attempt to re-stream the music but it often stutters in and out and may take up to 30 seconds to get back to having all of the speakers playing the same content. All speakers have clean wifi (not SonosNet). 

I have used the Sonos 27 app to recreate the exact scenario (same leader, same grouping, same music) with zero dropouts despit scrubbing and fast forwarding leading me to believe this is on the MA side. 

Reproduced on 2.10.4 and again on 2.10.12 right after a fresh container start. I timed both tests (Nov 18, my local time CST); the timelines and a log comparison are in the AI analysis section below.

### How to reproduce

1. Play an album on the Office stereo pair (native Sonos output) and scrub a few times. Speakers should work as intended.
2. In MA, add additional speakers to the group. Listen to ensure all speakers have the source material playing. 
3. Scrub, skip, or fast-forward.
4. Every speaker except the leader (Office L) drops for a few seconds up to about 30 seconds.
5. Remove Pool and scrub again: clean (tested on 2.10.4).
