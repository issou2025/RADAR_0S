# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Sloped roofs need a Revit 2024/2025 pitched-roof oracle**

*Project description:*
Roofs export with their sketched outline (RE-50), but as level plates: a roof's slope is not read. A slope decode needs an oracle, a Revit 2024 or 2025 file with pitched roofs and Revit's own geometry of it (an IFC or VIM export), and none of the public sources checked has one.

## Searched (2026-09-23)

| source | what it has | why it does not serve |
|---|---|---|
| Snowdon Towers (bldrs-ai/test-models, local only) | 20 roofs with Revit's IFC4 and a VIM | all flat except one small piece, 2112673, whose faces slope 10.8 to 16.7 degrees; the "sloped" patches on the others are tapered insulation |
| RE1 (Drshelden/IFC-ECS, MIT), Core Interior, Einhoven, Projeto1, teste_export_2025 | Revit exports | no roofs |
| 255ribeiro/intro_ifc modelo_bim | one IfcRoof aggregating four 30-degree slab parts | Revit 2023; element records are read from 2024 |
| vimaec/vim-hackathon | Wolford_Residence.r2024 v1 and v2 VIMs | no matching .rvt published |
| datadrivenconstruction samples | rac_basic_sample_project with pitched roofs, and a DAE of it | Revit 2023; the only 2024 sample holds four walls |
| buildingSMART/Sample-Test-Files | Revit-exported IFCs | no native .rvt |

## What would unblock it

Any of these, with the .rvt and Revit's IFC or VIM export of it:
- a 2024 or 2025 upgrade of rac_basic_sample_project;
- the Wolford Residence .rvt;
- a small purpose-made project with a hip roof and a gable roof.

Clean-room: the oracle is Revit's exported geometry, compared against byte observations.
