# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Sheets: drag a view from the project browser onto a sheet to place it**

*Project description:*
Remark from the maintainer's review of dev build 19cdda7 (31 October 2026), index #87.

**What:** a view is placed on a sheet by **dragging it from the project browser onto the sheet**, as in Revit.

**What exists:** a viewport is placed with the command *Place viewport* (2096, `take bim sheet command …` in `views/sheets/bim_sheet_workspace_ui.dl`). It takes the editor's current plan view (`editor's planViewId`) and asks for a click on the sheet. The project browser (`ui/panels`) opens views on a click but has no drag.

**Proposal:**
- Press on a view row in the project browser and drag it over an open sheet. A ghost of the viewport (its crop or extent at the view's scale) follows the pointer, and releasing places it, with snapping to the sheet's grid and to other viewports' edges.
- A view already on another sheet is refused with a message, since a view sits on one sheet, as in Revit. Schedules and legends may appear more than once.
- Dropping several selected views lays them out in a row.

**Owner:** split between two computers.
- Computer 1 (Noach, Elize): dragging from the browser in `ui/panels`, plus the drop routing, which may need a few lines in the shell.
- Computer 2 (Maarten): placement on the sheet in `views/sheets`.
