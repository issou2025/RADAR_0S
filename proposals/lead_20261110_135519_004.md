# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Epic: Publish Hoppin on the App Store and Google Play (Capacitor)**

*Project description:*
## What and why
Ship Hoppin to the **Apple App Store** and **Google Play** as installable apps, using **Capacitor** (the existing React web app bundled inside a thin native shell). One codebase: hoppinclub.com keeps working exactly as today.

## Decisions already made (do not reopen)
- **Capacitor**, not React Native/Flutter (no rewrite) and not a pure web wrapper.
- **Bundle the built site inside the app** (`webDir: apps/web/dist`). Do NOT point the WebView at `https://hoppinclub.com` (faster, works offline, and avoids Apple guideline 4.2 "just a website").
- **No payments, prices, "Upgrade" buttons or links to pay anywhere in the apps.** v1 is free. Monetisation is a later decision (web checkout vs in-app purchase) and is out of scope.
- **No photo upload or storage.** Posters are drawn on the phone from photos the person picks (already built).
- App id (bundle id / applicationId): **`club.hoppinclub.app`** (confirm with owner before first store upload: it cannot change later).
- App name: **Hoppin**.
- Accounts: Apple Developer Program ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 once) are created by the owner, not the agent. Google requires a **closed test with 12 testers for 14 days** before production for a new personal account: start it as early as possible.
