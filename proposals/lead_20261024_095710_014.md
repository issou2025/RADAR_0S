## Scope
- **Track C (Flutter app)**: an isolated Apple Health module under `app/` that reads, with permission, activity, workout and health data on the phone.
- **Profile prefill**: Apple Health values become *candidate* answers in the mascot conversation, which the user confirms or corrects, never silently committed (MASTER_PLAN "Mascot conversation"):
  - height, weight
  - weekly exercise minutes (Exercise Time / workouts)
  - sleep hours
  - blood pressure
  - alcohol (if logged)
  - possibly age and sex
- **Trackers for the game**: steps, active energy, workouts and resting heart rate shown on the twin as *activity* data, kept separate from the confirmed health profile, quiz game health and experiments (AGENTS.md separation rule).
- Read-only. LifeTwin never writes to Apple Health.

## Not touching without agreement
- `server/` and `contracts/`. If prefilled values need a provenance field (e.g. `source: apple_health`) or tracker data needs a schema, that will be a separate contract PR coordinated with Track B first.
- Track A model inputs. Tracker data does not become a model feature unless A's manifest supports it.

## Open decisions (pending)
- Privacy boundary: proposal is that raw HealthKit samples stay on the device and only the user-confirmed summary values leave it, exactly like typed answers.
- Whether the signing team supports the HealthKit capability (checking now).

Design priority: extremely simple for older users. One clear "Use my iPhone's Health data" choice, a plain-language explanation of what is read, a read-back for confirmation, and a full typed/voice path for anyone who says no.

Branch will be `codex/c-apple-health`.
