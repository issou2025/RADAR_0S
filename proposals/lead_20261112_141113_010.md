## How to ask for a review

1. Open your PR as usual, with the Arabic as a draft. Put the changed lines in a table in the PR body: where it shows, the English, your Arabic.
2. Comment here: `Bureau review: #<PR>`, with one line on who reads the text (parent, child, store reviewer).
3. The Bureau answers on your PR, line by line: OK, or the wording to use and why. It then signs `Arabic-Bureau: reviewed <head sha>`.
4. The Bureau sends the cleaned lines to Karim as a numbered sheet. His approval is still the final word on wording. Nothing new ships before it.

**Default if silent:** a PR with Arabic and no Bureau line is treated as not ready for verification.

Before you write, read the word list. The three mistakes it prevents most often:

- two names for one thing (`إقران` and `ربط`, `تصفية المواقع` and `تصفية الويب`);
- a setting or button name that differs from what the phone shows (Samsung says «الضبط», stock Android says «الإعدادات»);
- a sentence translated from English word for word, with no subject of its own.
