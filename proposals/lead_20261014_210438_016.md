About Acme Corporation
For nearly a century, Acme Corporation has been the global leader in catapult innovation, giant rubber band manufacturing, and high-speed rocket sled deployment. From our legendary Dehydrated Boulders to our proprietary Portable Holes, we supply world-class hardware and software solutions to coyotes, secret agents, and visionary troublemakers worldwide.

We are transforming our legacy hardware ecosystem into a modern, cloud-native platform. We need a Senior Backend Engineer to help us build high-throughput, sub-millisecond API endpoints that can handle millions of real-time explosive requests per second.

The Role
As a Senior Backend Engineer on our Core Systems team, you will design, scale, and maintain the distributed services powering our flagship order processing and automated trap-triggering platform. You will ensure our delivery services maintain 99.999% uptime—even when being pursued by a Roadrunner at Mach 2.

What You'll Do
Architect & Scale: Build high-performance microservices for our Instant Tunnel Infrastructure and real-time inventory tracking for high-demand items (e.g., Earthquake Pills, Giant Magnets).

Latency Reduction: Optimize our low-latency trigger systems to ensure spring-loaded boxing gloves fire in under 10 milliseconds.

Fault Tolerance & Reliability: Redesign legacy monoliths to gracefully handle unexpected structural failures, cliff-edge drops, and anvil misfires.

Data Pipelines: Process massive telemetry streams from millions of IoT-enabled rocket skates and track real-time trajectory analytics.

API Design: Expose secure, well-documented REST and gRPC APIs for third-party henchmen and internal automation workflows.

What We're Looking For
5+ years of experience building and operating scalable, distributed backend systems in Go, Rust, Java, or Python.

Database Expertise: Strong proficiency with PostgreSQL, Redis, and high-throughput event streaming architectures (Kafka, RabbitMQ).

Cloud & Infrastructure: Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, AWS/GCP, and Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform).

Edge Case Handling: Proven track record of debugging rare, chaotic edge cases (e.g., when a painted tunnel on a cliffside wall suddenly becomes navigable by external traffic).

System Resilience: Experience implementing circuit breakers, rate limiters, and fail-safes for systems operating in unpredictable, high-velocity environments.

Bonus Points
Prior experience with physical telemetry, ballistics computation, or explosive ordnance API design.

Familiarity with Quantum Anvil Mechanics or non-Newtonian gravity handling.

A deep, personal commitment to never using Acme products against yourself.
