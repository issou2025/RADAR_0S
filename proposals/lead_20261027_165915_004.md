## New check: 'Surface' without 'Body'

Revit doesn't display a product whose only representation is `'Surface'` (for example `IfcFaceBasedSurfaceModel`) and has no `'Body'`. Solibri and Dalux display it. This was seen on KNM RIVeg on 2026-10-15: 52 products, fixed by retagging them as `'Body'` (spruceledger `revit-hides-surface-representation`). Today the engine only counts these products into the set. Nothing flags them.

Wanted: a finding per model that lists the products with no Body and a Surface representation. This matters most for **LARK and RIVeg**, because they come from surface-oriented software, not BIM-native tools.
