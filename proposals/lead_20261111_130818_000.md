# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Snowdon Towers Architectural: roofs 1916596 and 964559 stay level blocks (a plane edge across a curved face that is not isoparametric; a cylinder face with several trim loops)**

*Project description:*
Two of Autodesk's Snowdon Towers Architectural's 20 roofs (Revit 2024) stay the level block of their sketch outline after #1683's ruled-surface fix drew the other four from their saved meshes. Each has one decoder diagnostic and its saved mesh holds no primitive (measured against Revit's own IFC4 export of the model, local only; counts and percentages only here):

| roof | diagnostic | over Revit's volume |
|---|---|---|
| 1916596 | `shared curved edge changes its fixed parameter` | 74% |
| 964559 | `multiple cylinder trim loops unsupported` | 42% |

**1916596.** `refined_plane_edge` in `src/research/native_saved_mesh.rs` refines the edge a plane face shares with a curved face (a cylinder, a surface of revolution, a ruled or a cone surface) in the curved face's parameter domain, and requires the edge to be an isoparametric interval there: `u` or `v` constant along it. This roof's edge is not, as the edge of a slanted plane across a cylinder is not. The same diagnostic is on 25 elements of this model (the railings KAI-5 and KAI-6 among them) and on `rac_basic` and the `rme` samples, so a rule here moves more than this roof.

**964559.** RE-407's negative: a cylinder face with more than one trim loop. The roof's outline holds a face of that kind.

**Done when:** each of the two roofs is drawn from its saved mesh and within 1% of Revit's volume by a rule that moves no element it should not (`ifc.sha256` byte-identical wherever the diagnostic is not raised, and the elements that do change named with their scorer lines), or a measured negative says why a trimmed edge of that kind cannot be walked from the file's bytes. The 25 elements that raise the first diagnostic are counted in the same pass, since a general rule for a non-isoparametric plane-to-curved edge draws them too.

Related: #1683 (the four ruled-surface roofs and the six-roof census), #1191, #1635 (railings), RE-407, RE-420, RE-422.
