*Project description:*
## Context & Objective
Following viral developer interest on Twitter, Reddit, DEV.to, and LinkedIn for the **`UI = f(State) / What the f?!`** t-shirt design, evaluate and set up a zero-risk, zero-upfront-cost **Print-On-Demand (POD)** storefront for official `BlocSignal` apparel and community merchandise.

---

## 👕 Platform Candidates

### 1. **Cotton Bureau** *(Recommended)*
* **Pros:** Standard for developer and tech podcast merchandise (high-end Next Level / Bella+Canvas blanks, tri-blends).
* **Fulfillment:** Handles printing, global shipping, tax compliance, customer support, and direct payouts.
* **Cost:** 100% zero upfront cost.

### 2. **Spring (formerly Teespring)**
* **Pros:** Direct integration with YouTube merch shelves (for live stream channels like Flutter Humpday Q&A), Linktree, and social profiles.
* **Cost:** 100% zero upfront cost.

### 3. **Printful / Printify + `blocsignal.dev` / Jaspr**
* **Pros:** Can be embedded natively into `blocsignal.dev/merch` with Jaspr web components and Stripe checkout.
* **Cost:** Pay-per-order API backend.
