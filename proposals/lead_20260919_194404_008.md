# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Part: Mirror plane from face inside Assembly ignores parent Body placement**

*Project description:*
### Problem description

Part → Mirror calculates an incorrect mirror plane when a planar face of a PartDesign Body inside an Assembly is used as the reference plane and the Body has a non-zero Placement. I found this when my part had an unexpected and un-designed offset in Z.

The selected face is evaluated in the Body's local coordinate system, while the Placement of the Body in the Assembly appears to be ignored.

The source Body itself remains at its correct global position. This causes the mirrored solid to be placed incorrectly and to overlap the source solid.

Demo file attached.

[mirrorfail.zip](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32260779/mirrorfail.zip)

### Workbench affected?

Part

### Steps to reproduce

1. Create a new document.
2. Create an Assembly.
3. Create/add a PartDesign Body inside the Assembly.
4. Create a sketch on the XY plane.
5. Pad a simple rectangle to some value, maybe 15 mm.
6. With the Body at Placement Z = 0 mm, use:

       Part → Mirror

   Select the Body as source and the upper planar face of the Pad as the mirror plane.
   The result is a correctly placed, mirrored body on the other.

7. Now set the Body Placement to:

       Position Z = +2 mm

8. Repeat Part → Mirror using the same upper face as the mirror plane.

   The result is a wrongly placed, mirrored body inside the other body. It is 'translated' in -z by exactly twice the former z offset as a result of the incorrect mirror plane.

Also: Everything just breaks when you then try to move bodies in the tree.


### Expected behavior

The Body is locally:

    Z = 0 ... 15 mm

With Body Placement Z = +2 mm its global position is:

    Z = 2 ... 17 mm

Therefore the selected upper face is globally located at:

    Z = 17 mm

The mirror plane should therefore be:

    Base.Z = 17 mm

and the mirrored Body should occupy:

    Z = 17 ... 32 mm

### Actual behavior

Part Mirror uses:

    Base.Z = 15 mm

which is the **LOCAL** Z coordinate of the selected face.
The parent Body Placement of +2 mm is apparently not applied to the mirror-plane reference.
The resulting mirrored Body therefore occupies:

    Z = 11 ... 26 mm

instead of:

    Z = 17 ... 32 mm

The source and mirrored solids overlap by 4 mm, exactly twice the ignored Body Placement offset.

### Important observation

The issue appears to require the Body to be inside an Assembly.With the Assembly Body at Placement (0,0,0), mirroring works correctly. As soon as the Body is translated, the mirror plane is offset by exactly the Body translation. This suggests that Part Mirror evaluates the planar reference face in local Body coordinates instead of transforming it through the Body / Assembly placement.

### Related issue

This may be conceptually related to #28133 ("Part: Mirror of App::Link ignores placement"), but this case concerns
the placement of the *mirror-plane reference face* inside an Assembly, not the placement of the mirrored App::Link/source object.
