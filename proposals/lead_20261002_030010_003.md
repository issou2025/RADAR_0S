## Why
On 2026-10-01 the owner confirmed in desktop Revit that a generated one-box family's **Width** drives its geometry: both probes of the pair widened (verdict on #787). Production families still ship the old chain, which moves nothing (#901, on the trapeze).

## DONE
1. **The born law on every drive path.** `drive=True` authors the born in-plane law:
   - no `m_constrInfo` back-edges;
   - view-less sketch locks;
   - `m_dimVersion` 6;
   - `m_sideRefPlaneCurveBased`;
   - the curve bit;
   - the labelled segment form.

   `finalize` does not re-add back-edges to a document that carries the law.
2. **Multi-part drive.** One parameter drives the matching sketch edges of every part it governs, through shared reference planes. The first customer is the trapeze:
   - **Strut Length:** the two end planes, which every tier's end segments lock to.
   - **Rod Spacing:** the two rod planes, which the rods and their washers and nuts lock to.
   - **Tier Spacing:** a plan-independent plane per tier. This one needs the extrusion end, so it waits for #787 Case B.
3. **A probe staged for each lane before it is claimed.** At minimum: the trapeze with Strut Length driving, and the panelboard with Width/Depth driving and its front parts following. Each gets its own desktop verdict (hard rule 4). Until then, the reports keep saying "value-only" for any parameter without a verdict.
