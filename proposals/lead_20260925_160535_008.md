# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**[Impeller] Poor performance with svg files that contain masks with Luminance**

*Project description:*
### Steps to reproduce

1. Use Samsung S24 or A70
3. Turn on Impeller renderer
4. display an SVG with about 8 Luminance masks
5. scroll

Or

1. Use iPhone 12
3. Turn on Impeller renderer
4. display an SVG with about 24 Luminance masks
5. scroll

### Expected results

No laggy behaiour, No crashes

### Actual results

The app laags and sometimes even crashes 
if the Masks are above 16 on the S24
or if the masks are above 24 on iPhone 12
