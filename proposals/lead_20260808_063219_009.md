# Project Proposal — Flutter App

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**Rename Todo to Outcome across identifiers, storage and the wire**

*Project description:*
## User Story

As a developer, I want every "Todo" in the codebase to say "Outcome", so the largest single vocabulary divergence stops teaching new readers the wrong model.

## Scope

One PR, staged as commits so each is independently green and `main` stays deployable.

1. **Dart identifiers.** `Todo` → `Outcome`, `TodoDao` → `OutcomeDao`, `TodoList` → the CONTEXT name for a GTD list, plus providers, models and their generated siblings. No table, no wire.
2. **Local storage.** `todos` → `outcomes`, `todo_tags` → `outcome_tags`, `todo_id` → `outcome_id`. Drift migration; `codec.table` updated. The wire is untouched by this commit.
3. **Wire.** `todosCollection` and `todoTagsCollection` values, the `jeeves://todo_tag/…` and `jeeves://action/backfill/…` derived-id URIs (the latter mirrored in Alembic 0028 and pinned by a cross-language golden vector), and the regenerated `spec/sync/*.json`. Atomic — codec, reducer, derivation and vectors move together or the fixtures disagree.
4. **UI copy** wherever "todo" is user-visible.

**Op-log history is the open question in step 3.** A derived-id URI change re-mints identities, so already-authored ops must be handled deliberately: either the fleet has no enrolled device and the rename is free, or one device re-authors the log at an epoch boundary and the others reset. Settle this before writing step 3 — it is the only part of this issue whose approach is not already determined.
