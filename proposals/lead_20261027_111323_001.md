# Project Proposal — Structural Drawings

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**[P0][agent] ROOT — Quantity takeoff ground-truth experiment: the mandatory evidence gate before the pivot**

*Project description:*
### Goal

Establish, against **independently verified real-world measurements**, whether BuildSmart can produce trustworthy construction quantities. This is the mandatory evidence gate before any commitment to the Construction Intelligence pivot. Owner directive 2026-10-27.

The governing principle, in the owner's words: **a mathematically correct calculation is not evidence that its input measurements are correct.** Five things are evaluated separately — source measurement accuracy, geometric calculation accuracy, material quantity accuracy, completeness of required inputs, and provenance and auditability.

The goal is **measured trustworthiness, not another visually impressive demonstration.**

### Current behavior

#257's audit (PR #258) established the starting position, and two of its findings decide how this experiment must be built:

- **No third dimension and no material exist anywhere in the product**, and every domain-looking figure is self-declared as not one. BuildSmart has never possessed a measured number: its arithmetic is internally consistent and externally unvalidated, with closure proven as an exact integer identity and provenance carrying a full type with almost no instances.
- **No plan ingestion exists and none ever did** — no PDF, raster, OCR, DXF, DWG, IFC or SVG-parsing dependency on any of 71 branch tips or anywhere in history; `python-multipart` is absent from the lockfile, so the service physically cannot accept an uploaded file. **Therefore comparison B — quantities from automatically extracted measurements — has nothing to run against and must be reported as not applicable, never quietly folded into A.**

And #242's R1 (PR #259) measured why the existing validators must not be reused here: 0 of 19 real plans were carried, and in **18 of 19** the refusal was a policy refusal unrelated to geometry. **The validators encode what the generator may build, not what a building is** — which is exactly the directive's instruction that they must not serve as general building-validity validators.

One structural fact in the experiment's favour, worth stating before any work: of the four first materials, **three need only plan-view geometry plus door widths** — flooring area, ceiling paintable area and baseboard length. Only **wall paintable area needs a height**, and the directive forbids inferring it. So the third dimension is a **dataset requirement**, not an engine gap, for three of the four.

### Required behavior

Executed through children, with the dataset first because it gates everything:

1. **#263 — the dataset contract and acquisition.** Obtain a small set of legally usable architectural plans with independently verified measurements and known quantities, preferring real projects with documented as-built measurements or professionally verified takeoffs. Record source, measurement method, units, revision and reliability for every reference value. **Report BLOCKED if suitable ground truth cannot be obtained. Synthetic fixtures must never be substituted and called real-world accuracy, and ground truth must never be manufactured from BuildSmart's own calculations.** No paid acquisition without the owner's approval.
2. **#265 — the measurement and provenance schema, the quantity formulas and the evaluation harness.** Six separated layers: plan and document ingestion, measurement extraction, canonical geometry, quantity calculations, product specifications, pricing. Heights, finish types and product coverage are **inputs with provenance**, never inferred from plan geometry. Acceptance thresholds are **fixed and committed before any final evaluation result is inspected.**
3. **#267 — the ground-truth comparison report and the recommendation.** Per reference project: absolute error, percentage error where meaningful, missing measurements, unsupported categories, manual correction effort, provenance completeness, and failure and abstention rates. **A and B reported separately and never conflated.** Then an evidence-backed GO, CONDITIONAL GO or NO-GO, and a proposed minimal architecture for the next phase. Where accuracy is insufficient, say whether the failure originates in the measurements, the geometry, the material assumptions or the calculation logic.

### Acceptance Criteria

AC-1: the dataset's provenance is recorded per reference value — source, measurement method, units, revision, reliability — or the work reports BLOCKED with what was tried
AC-2: no ground-truth value is derived from BuildSmart's own calculation, and no synthetic fixture supports any real-world accuracy claim
AC-3: the six layers are separated, and no generator validator is used as a general building-validity check
AC-4: wall heights, finish types and product coverage enter as inputs carrying provenance and are never inferred from plan geometry
AC-5: acceptance thresholds are committed before any final evaluation result is inspected, shown by commit order
AC-6: comparison A and comparison B are reported separately, with B stated as not applicable while no extraction capability exists
AC-7: the production flow is preserved, no existing test is weakened, and nothing is merged, deployed or paid for without the owner's approval

### Out of scope

Any production change. Merging or deploying anything. Paid data acquisition without the owner's approval. Marketplace or supplier work, which the directive defers behind this gate. Any regulatory compliance claim. Materials beyond the four named. Inferring a dimension the plans do not carry.
