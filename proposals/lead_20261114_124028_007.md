# Project Proposal — Bim Modeling

## Project Understanding
You need assistance with: 
**mesh-compile: certify relation faces without pcurves across a tangent break**

*Project description:*
Follow-up to #454 (ADR 0110).

**Need.** A B-rep face over a surface relation whose edges carry no pcurves is reported `Unbounded` ("the surface is not certifiably invertible along an edge without a pcurve") when it touches a tangent break of the swept curve. Examples: a patch ending exactly at a polyline profile's corner, or one crossing the corner with the profile as an edge curve. The same faces certify with pcurves. The lens oracle's break rule refuses to certify continuity across the break. Faces between the corners of a polyline or kinked profile are a common layout in IFC and Revit data (IfcSurfaceOfLinearExtrusion over a polyline profile, Revit extrusions).

**Proposal.** Certify the inversion on each side of the break separately, with one-sided derivative bounds, and join the two sides at the break, where the parameter is known exactly. Unbounded by name stays the answer where that fails.

**Done when** a planar patch ending at, and one crossing, a C0 corner of an extruded B-spline profile mesh certified without pcurves, tested against the exact surface, with a probe mutant.
