## Invitation to Incubate BeamCraft in `artcraft-candidates`

Hi @Apibim-com!

Impressive work on **BeamCraft**! Bringing structural steel detailing, parametric DSTV NC generation, and open IFC handling into a modern, pure-Rust CAD workbench is a massive contribution to open engineering software.

### Context: The `artcraft-candidates` Community Incubator

The Artcraft community recently established the [`artcraft-candidates`](https://github.com/artcraft-candidates) GitHub organization as an official community incubator for domain-specific Craft applications:
- **Incubator RFC:** [storytold/artcraft#2336](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2336)
- **BeamCraft Candidate Tracking Issue:** [storytold/artcraft#2338](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2338)
- **Community BIMCraft Request:** [storytold/artcraft#2157](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2157)

### Proposal

We would be thrilled to invite you to stage or mirror **BeamCraft** under the incubator:
👉 **`https://github.com/artcraft-candidates/beamcraft`**

### Benefits:
1. **Community Reach:** Connect directly with architects, engineers, and developers looking for open-source CAD/BIM tools.
2. **Shared Foundation:** Collaboration on 3D GPU viewports (`wgpu`), spatial trees, and drafting sheet generation alongside other Craft CAD and GIS initiatives.
3. **Structured Upstream Path:** A defined path towards graduation into the broader Artcraft ecosystem.

Looking forward to your thoughts! Let us know here or at [storytold/artcraft#2338](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2338).
